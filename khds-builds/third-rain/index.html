Third Rain is not a real restaurant or commissioned client project. Kodawa Labs created it as a research synthesis: a public test of KHDS ideas, decisions and limits.

01

Why it exists

From observation to application

Research is only useful when it can survive a real design.

KHDS—the Kodawa Hospitality Design System—studies how hospitality businesses communicate arrival, atmosphere, photography, reservation and story. Individual cases reveal mechanisms; comparison reveals recurring patterns.

After the second ten-restaurant cycle, the next honest test was not another report. It was a fictional business with constraints: a place, a cuisine, service formats, prices, policies, access needs and a reason to exist. Third Rain is that test.

The goal was not to display every finding. It was to decide which findings could coexist without turning hospitality into interface complexity.

The restaurant

The fictional premise

East African cooking, organised by the movement of rain.

Third Rain imagines a Toronto restaurant where climate is not decoration. Rain determines ingredients, preservation, gathering and memory—so it becomes the organising logic for menu, language, atmosphere and guest choice.

06

KHDS apps

Build 01 status

The journey is demonstrated; the standalone app is not yet embedded.

Third Rain includes a designed direct-reservation journey that tests format choice, expectation, dietary disclosure and access preparation. It does not currently embed a production KHDS application.

Future synthesis builds may embed tools such as ReserveAI or other KHDS apps when the tool genuinely improves the hospitality model. Backstories will always state what is live, what is a prototype and what remains proposed.

App disclosure · Prototype journey only
07

What it cannot prove

The evidence boundary

A coherent website is not proof of a coherent restaurant.

The build can test information architecture, narrative coherence and prepared choice.

It cannot prove food quality, staff behaviour, atmosphere, accessibility performance or guest emotion.

It does not promote, validate or demote any KHDS pattern by itself.

It should be judged as a synthesis experiment—not evidence from an operating institution.

The continuing programme

Ten studies. One synthesis. Then the research continues.

Third Rain establishes the format. Every future KHDS fictional website will receive its own research backstory, app disclosure and evidence boundary here in the showcase.
