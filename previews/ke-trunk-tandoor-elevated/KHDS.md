# Kodawa Hospitality Design System — Sprint 1

## Signature
Pacing is the Kodawa Labs signature. The system should reveal information calmly, with one clear idea per scene.

## Permanent principles
1. Emotion before interface.
2. Story before features.
3. Photography before decoration.
4. Restraint before complexity.
5. Motion with purpose.
6. Mobile is the primary experience.
7. Pacing is our signature.
8. Respect the brand.
9. Every element must create emotion or business value.
10. The restaurant should own the guest relationship.

## Architecture
- `styles/tokens.css` — shared decisions and values
- `styles/typography.css` — type roles
- `styles/base.css` — layout foundation
- `styles/components.css` — reusable primitives
- `styles/motion.css` — pacing and motion
- `styles/principles.css` — always-on principles panel
- `styles/trunk-tandoor.css` — restaurant-specific expression
- `khds-principles.js` — rule reminder panel

## Principles panel
The bottom-left panel stays available during design reviews. Click a rule once to mark it **watch**, twice to mark it **risk**, and a third time to clear it.

Append `?principles=off` to the URL to hide the panel for a clean client presentation.

## Rule
KHDS should make future projects faster and more consistent without making them look alike.
