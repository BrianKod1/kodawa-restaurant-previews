The Spot

A CBD staple hiding in plain sight.

The Branch sits up the left staircase inside Kenya Cinema Plaza on Moi Avenue — a casual, welcoming room serving hearty, traditional African home-style cooking rather than a fine-dining performance. There's seating on two floors, plus a balcony overlooking the street for anyone who wants a view with their meal.

A large hot-water basin greets guests at the entrance, and the upstairs bar pours cocktails, mocktails and shots alongside the food menu — a spot built for a proper sit-down meal, not a quick bite.
