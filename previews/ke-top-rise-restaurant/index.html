The Spot

A CBD favourite that doesn't shout about it.

Tucked into Lyric House on Kimathi Street, Top Rise has built a loyal following the old-fashioned way — good food, a big comfortable space, and service that keeps people coming back. It's the kind of place regulars call a hidden gem, mostly because it doesn't try to be anything else.

The dining area is large enough for groups without feeling like a hall, and the balcony seating looking out over Kimathi Street is the spot regulars ask for by name.
