The Room

Exclusivity, comfort, and design — done properly.

Set inside Crowne Plaza Nairobi in Upper Hill, Sikia is built around an a la carte menu that fuses local and international cuisine — the kind of place regulars book for business lunches, and where "just a hotel restaurant" stops being a fair description within the first course.

Lunch runs from noon to 3pm, dinner from 6:30 to 10pm, Monday through Saturday. Private dining is available, and the wine list is built to match the ambition of the food.
