The View

Eighteen floors above Westlands.

270° Rooftop sits on the 18th floor of the Escada building on Lantana Road — high enough that the traffic and noise of Westlands fall away, replaced by an open-air view of the city that stretches nearly the full compass.

The kitchen runs a fusion menu that moves between Italian pasta and Asian seafood plates, backed by a full bar and a cocktail list built for watching the sun go down over Nairobi. Reservations are recommended, especially for evening tables.
