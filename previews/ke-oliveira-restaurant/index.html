The Room

A CBD room that doubles as a gallery.

Set on Biashara Street opposite After 40 Hotel, Oliveira built its name on fine African and Continental dishes — then widened the menu with Chinese and grill options and a full bar, making it as much an evening spot as a lunch counter.

What sets the room apart is on the walls: original local art hung through the dining space, turning a meal into something closer to a gallery sitting — and occasionally the backdrop to a proper event, from fashion showcases to dinner collaborations.
