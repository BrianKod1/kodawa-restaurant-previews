The Space

A lifestyle stop built for the whole day.

The Summer Lounge sits on the ground floor of Sarit Centre in Westlands — a bright, design-forward room built to move with the day, from relaxed breakfasts and business lunches through to a livelier evening scene with cocktails and DJ sets.

The kitchen keeps things global and easy to share: burgers, pasta, Mediterranean bites, and a dessert menu built for the photo before the bite. It's become a regular stop for both weekday lunch meetings and weekend socializing.
