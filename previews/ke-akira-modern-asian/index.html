The Kitchen

Contemporary Asian, precisely made.

Akira sits on the 1st floor of Westgate Mall in Westlands, with sleek wood styling and minimalist furniture that set a calmer tone than most of Nairobi's dinner-and-drinks scene. The menu runs wide — sushi and sashimi through to ramen, wok-fired rice, robata skewers, and steaks — built by chefs working across the breadth of modern Asian cooking.

Seating flexes with the occasion: large tables for groups, a full bar for solo diners, or the Japanese-style low tables with floor cushions for something more immersive.

Built for date nights and long dinners.

Akira's design does a lot of the work — wood tones, low light, and a seating plan spaced out enough that neighbouring tables stay out of earshot. It's landed as a go-to for date nights, celebrations, and the kind of dinner you don't want to rush.
