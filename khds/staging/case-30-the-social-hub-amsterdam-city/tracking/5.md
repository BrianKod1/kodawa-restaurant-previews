### Post–Case #30 synthesis

The recurring mechanism across Cases #27–30 is broader than membership gating alone.

The synthesis architecture is:

**Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture**

> A hospitality structure in which one institution supports multiple persistent or transactional relationships while actual participation remains conditional on role, product, current rules, time, capacity and access state.

The existing:

**Membership-Gated / Recurring-Entitlement Hospitality Architecture**

is retained as a subtype.

It remains particularly descriptive of Cases #27–29.

Case #30 does not weaken that subtype.

Instead, it demonstrates that similar entitlement, capacity and lifecycle separation can persist in a mixed-use institution with substantial public access.

### Research decisions

**Decision 29**

Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture is retained as provisional comparison architecture below formal pattern maturity after the Case #30 synthesis gate.

**Decision 30**

Membership-Gated / Recurring-Entitlement Hospitality Architecture is retained as a narrower subtype. Case #30 demonstrates that the broader mechanism can survive outside an institution that is uniformly membership-gated without authorizing formal pattern or confidence movement.

### Falsification requirements

- role-specific entitlement continues to diverge from service-specific capacity;
- public and controlled participation states remain meaningfully distinct;
- one brand can preserve legible relationship boundaries at operational scale;
- cross-property rights remain conditional on local capacity;
- digital relationships continue beyond physical visits without creating governance gaps;
- recovery responsibility remains legible when roles and systems overlap;
- and the architecture survives additional institution types with independent operational evidence.
