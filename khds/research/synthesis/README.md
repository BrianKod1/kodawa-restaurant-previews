## Purpose

Research Synthesis Builds convert each completed ten-case KHDS research cycle into an original fictional restaurant and a working hospitality website.

They exist to prove that KHDS knowledge can shape a coherent guest experience across:

- arrival;
- atmosphere;
- photography and proof;
- reservation, access and participation;
- story and narrative;
- operational clarity;
- human assistance;
- and suitable technology.

The output is not a visual mood board. It must function as a believable hospitality system.

## Track

Research Synthesis Builds form:

**Track C — Research Synthesis Builds**

Track C is separate from:

- **Track A — Independent Hospitality Research**; and
- **Track B — Production Validation**.

## Governance Boundary

```text
Synthesis Quality

≠

Research Validation
```

A fictional restaurant, successful website or working application integration cannot independently:

- promote a pattern;
- change pattern confidence;
- validate a pattern;
- or create a Research-Derived KHDS Principle.

Track C may reveal implementation questions, missing states, technology opportunities and falsification targets. Those findings must return to Track A or Track B before they can affect formal research maturity.

## Ten-Case Synthesis Rule

After every ten completed KHDS hospitality cases:

1. Complete all case dossiers.
2. Complete the five cross-case comparisons.
3. Reconcile canonical governance and all mirrors.
4. Identify the strongest transferable findings from those ten cases.
5. Identify contradictions, failure states and unresolved boundaries.
6. Create an original fictional restaurant whose operating model requires those findings.
7. Build the restaurant's production-quality website.
8. Integrate KHDS applications only where the hospitality model justifies them.
9. Add the completed site to the Kodawa Labs Showcase.
10. Record implementation learnings without treating them as research validation.

## Synthesis Build Standard

Each fictional restaurant must establish:

- a distinct name and identity;
- a specific location and audience;
- a coherent hospitality model;
- an organising logic that affects more than visual style;
- believable menus, prices, schedules and policies;
- an arrival system;
- an atmosphere system;
- a photography and proof strategy;
- a reservation or participation system;
- a story supported by the operating model;
- accessibility and human-assistance paths;
- capacity boundaries and failure recovery;
- responsive desktop and mobile behaviour;
- and a working primary guest action.

The restaurant must not be a renamed copy of any researched institution.

## Application Integration Rule

KHDS applications are integrated selectively.

An application belongs in a synthesis build only when it improves the guest or operator journey established by the restaurant's hospitality model.

Application fit is evaluated through five questions:

1. Does it solve a real guest or operator problem in this concept?
2. Does it support the restaurant's organising logic?
3. Are its data, consent and privacy requirements proportionate?
4. Does it include a usable human fallback or failed-route recovery?
5. Can a visitor use the integration rather than merely see it advertised?

Potential integrations include:

| KHDS application or system | Appropriate use |
|---|---|
| ReserveAI | Direct reservations, availability, waitlists, confirmations and table management |
| GuestOps AI | Pre-arrival preparation, preferences, accessibility support and post-booking assistance |
| DropHop | Destination-led restaurants, hotel stays and travel-dependent hospitality journeys |
| Analytics systems | Direct-booking conversion, abandonment, demand and revenue measurement |
| CRM and automation | Permission-based confirmations, reminders, recovery and guest relationships |

```text
Available Technology

≠

Appropriate Technology
```

Not every synthesis build requires an application.

Not every suitable application belongs in the visible guest interface.

## Kodawa Labs Showcase Rule

Every completed synthesis build becomes a Kodawa Labs Showcase candidate.

Before publication, it must have:

- a complete restaurant concept;
- a working responsive website;
- a clear primary action;
- accurate fictional-project disclosure;
- no unsupported client or performance claims;
- completed mobile and desktop checks;
- functional embedded applications where included;
- and a concise explanation of the KHDS research cycle behind it.

New KHDS synthesis builds will progressively replace older showcase concepts.

Replacement should be gradual:

1. Publish the new synthesis build.
2. Confirm that its live experience works.
3. Add it to the hospitality collection.
4. Retire one weaker or less representative legacy concept.
5. Preserve an archive record when the older concept contains useful historical work.

The intended result is:

> A Kodawa Labs Showcase made primarily of working hospitality concepts built from documented research, operational reasoning and appropriate technology.
