# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a passenger know or possess before arriving, and how much uncertainty remains before physical lounge entry?

## Evidence

Preparation differs by access route.

A direct-booking user may arrive with a prepaid lounge relationship created before reaching the airport. The operator also permits walk-in use, so advance booking is not the only preparation model. [S1][S3]

Partner-card and travel-pass users are instructed to present the relevant credential together with a boarding pass. Their preparation therefore depends on proving eligibility rather than simply proving a direct lounge purchase. [S4]

PPL Pass relies on a voucher-based entitlement. Its published rules describe validity and visit-duration conditions distinct from a normal direct-pay package. [S5]

These pathways create different pre-arrival objects:

- booking confirmation;
- payment relationship;
- boarding pass;
- eligible partner credential;
- travel pass;
- PPL Pass voucher.

Possessing one valid access mechanism does not establish that every optional service will be available after arrival. Operator terms retain availability conditions for services requested at the lounge. [S3][S5]

## Opening-hours conflict

The most important unresolved pre-arrival issue is the published operating-hours conflict.

S1 states daily hours of 05:30-01:00. [S1]

S2 states daily hours of 05:30-22:30. [S2]

Both were inspected for this case.

The dossier does not choose one value as authoritative without further evidence.

For a passenger planning late-evening use, that disagreement can materially affect whether the lounge appears available before a flight.

Published hours conflict != confirmed operating reality.

## Interpretation

Different access relationships create different preparation requirements before the passenger reaches the lounge.

Passenger eligibility != proof of lounge entitlement.

Credential entitlement != guaranteed capacity.

Pre-arrival confidence != guaranteed optional-service availability.

The source conflict also demonstrates that published institutional information can itself create uncertainty before arrival.

## Limits

The source set does not establish what message a passenger receives when the lounge is expected to be full.

It does not establish how delayed flights, schedule changes or unavailable optional services are communicated before arrival.

The opening-hours conflict remains unresolved.
