# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does the lounge relationship end, and what happens when visit duration, lounge hours or flight timing no longer align?

## Evidence

The public access products establish time-bounded rather than indefinite lounge use.

Pearson publishes direct-pay access in two-, three- and six-hour packages. [S2]

PPL Pass typically provides up to three hours per visit. [S5]

These products therefore create an entitlement boundary that can occur before the passenger's wider airport journey ends.

Lounge relationship duration != passenger journey duration.

## Closing-time conflict

Departure analysis is complicated by the unresolved source conflict over daily operating hours.

S1 states 05:30-01:00. [S1]

S2 states 05:30-22:30. [S2]

The dossier does not determine which closing time reflects actual current operations.

For a late-evening passenger, the difference could materially change when lounge hospitality ends.

Published closing time != verified physical closure time.

## Interpretation

Case #32 is useful because departure from the hospitality relationship can be triggered by several different boundaries:

- expiry of a purchased time package;
- expiry of a pass-based visit period;
- voluntary departure;
- possible lounge closing time;
- onward progression to the passenger's flight.

These boundaries are not shown to be identical.

A passenger can remain inside the wider airport journey after lounge entitlement ends.

Physical presence in the airport != continuing lounge entitlement.

## Limits

The inspected sources do not establish:

- extension rules;
- overstay handling;
- re-entry after departure;
- treatment of delayed flights;
- treatment of cancelled flights;
- what happens if the lounge closes before expected;
- whether unused time is recoverable;
- whether a denied optional service affects departure rights;
- the escalation route for a disputed duration or closing-time issue.

The S1/S2 hours conflict remains unresolved.

## Next evidence

Inspect official extension, re-entry, delay, closure and customer-support rules and obtain dated confirmation of current operating hours before making stronger departure claims.
