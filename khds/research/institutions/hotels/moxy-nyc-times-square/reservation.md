# Reservation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Which Moxy NYC Times Square experiences use separate booking, reservation or inquiry mechanisms, and does one commercial relationship guarantee access or capacity in another?

## Evidence

The hotel room product has its own availability and booking flow, distinct from dining, rooftop and event pathways. [S1][S4]

Bar Moxy follows a different commitment model. Marriott states that no reservation is required. [S2]

Magic Hour uses another model. Reservations are encouraged, walk-ins are accepted and hotel guests may present a Moxy Times Square room key for priority access. [S2][S4]

That hotel-guest priority remains explicitly conditional. Moxy states that Magic Hour entry is subject to capacity restraints, private events and doorman discretion. [S4]

This establishes a bounded distinction:

Hotel room reservation -> hotel guest status -> Magic Hour priority.

Magic Hour priority != guaranteed Magic Hour entry.

Magic Hour also supports separate bottle-service and event inquiry pathways. Public event material identifies venue-specific capacities and private-event configurations. [S5][S7]

Legasea follows restaurant-specific reservation rules. Marriott states that larger parties face additional conditions, including credit-card requirements and table availability rules. [S2]

The public record therefore shows several commitment mechanisms inside one property:

- hotel room booking;
- Bar Moxy with no reservation requirement;
- Magic Hour reservations and walk-ins;
- hotel-guest priority at Magic Hour;
- bottle-service inquiry;
- private-event inquiry and buyout configurations;
- Legasea restaurant reservation rules.

## Interpretation

Case #31 provides direct public evidence that physical co-location and shared institutional branding do not create one reservation or entitlement system.

A hotel reservation creates a hotel relationship and some role-specific benefits, but it does not automatically reserve service-specific capacity elsewhere in the property.

The strongest supported boundary is:

Product relationship != service-specific capacity.

The Magic Hour evidence is especially important because hotel-guest priority exists without becoming guaranteed access.

This demonstrates that priority and entitlement are not identical concepts:

Hotel guest > general priority position in some circumstances.

Hotel guest != guaranteed rooftop capacity.

Reservation architecture also varies by service rather than following one property-wide rule.

This is directionally consistent with Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture because relationships sharing one property retain distinct commitment and capacity conditions.

## Limits

Public evidence does not establish:

- internal inventory allocation between hotel guests and public Magic Hour customers;
- whether hotel packages can create guaranteed rooftop inventory;
- the exact priority sequence when Magic Hour reaches capacity;
- how doorman discretion is operationalized;
- whether hotel, restaurant and event reservation systems share customer identity;
- internal oversell, waitlist or recovery rules;
- whether hotel staff can override venue-specific inventory.

Separate reservation pathways do not by themselves prove separate backend systems or legal operators.

Hotel-guest priority must not be interpreted as guaranteed access because the operator expressly qualifies it.

## Synthesis-gate relevance

Reservation evidence creates meaningful transfer pressure beyond the membership-led cases that preceded Case #30.

Case #31 demonstrates that a hotel stay can coexist with independently conditioned capacity relationships under one property identity.

The strongest supported boundaries are:

Hotel relationship != uniform cross-property entitlement.

Guest priority != guaranteed service capacity.

This supports the broader Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality hypothesis more strongly than a simple bundled-amenities interpretation would predict.

However, reservation evidence alone does not justify pattern promotion, confidence movement, validation, principle creation or synthesis before the scheduled Case #40 gate.
