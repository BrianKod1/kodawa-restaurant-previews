# Reservation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What does a berth request or CIQ booking actually secure, and which vessel, timing, regulatory and shore-service states remain conditional?

## Berth enquiry is not explicitly a confirmed berth

ONE°15 provides a Berth Enquiry form collecting contact, membership, berth type, request type, arrival/departure dates and vessel information. [S2]

Therefore:

Berth Enquiry Submitted
!=
Berth Confirmed.

The public form establishes an enquiry workflow, not automatic allocation.

## Berth pricing depends on vessel characteristics

Wet-berth rates vary by vessel length. Charges are based on physical vessel measurement or berth length, whichever is greater. Catamarans, trimarans or large monohulls exceeding allocated width can incur additional berthing fees. [S1]

Therefore:

One Berth Reservation
!=
One Standard Commercial Unit.

And:

Vessel Length
!=
Only Berthing Cost Variable.

## Member and non-member states differ commercially

Published berthing rates distinguish member and non-member pricing, while temporary or term membership can add surcharges. [S1]

Therefore:

Same Vessel
+
Same Berth
!=
Same Commercial State.

## CIQ booking is a separate reservation

The 24/7 CIQ service requires its own advance reservation and is available to members and visiting pleasure craft subject to fees and booking requirements. [S3]

Therefore:

Berth Reservation
!=
CIQ Reservation.

This is a central Case #47 separation.

## CIQ booking is tied to an approved regulatory submission

The CIQ workflow requires vessel details, crew and passenger details, passport/travel information and clearance timing. Changes to the approved crew or passenger list require cancellation and resubmission of the ICA submission and a new CIQ request. [S3]

Therefore:

CIQ Slot
!=
Transferable Generic Time Slot.

It is coupled to a specific regulatory state.

## Berthing after clearance is still conditional

ONE°15 explicitly states that staying at the marina after clearing into Singapore is subject to berth availability and applicable marina charges, and overnight berthing should be reserved separately. [S3]

Therefore:

CIQ Clearance Reservation
!=
Overnight Berth Entitlement.

## CIQ is capacity- and time-dependent

Bookings are subject to availability and first-come, first-served confirmation. [S3]

Therefore:

CIQ Request Submitted
!=
CIQ Slot Confirmed.

## Large-vessel CIQ reservation needs additional berth review

For vessels over 125ft, the marina reviews CIQ reservations based on berth availability because the normal CIQ area is size-constrained. [S3]

Therefore:

Regulatory Eligibility
!=
Physical CIQ-Berth Availability.

## Late arrival changes the commercial state

ONE°15 applies late-arrival charges when a vessel arrives more than ten minutes after its confirmed CIQ booking time. [S1][S3]

Therefore:

CIQ Reservation Held
!=
Unlimited Arrival Window.

## Strongest reservation boundaries

1. berth enquiry versus confirmed berth;
2. one berth versus one standard commercial unit;
3. same vessel/berth versus same member commercial state;
4. berth reservation versus CIQ reservation;
5. CIQ slot versus transferable generic slot;
6. CIQ reservation versus overnight berth entitlement;
7. CIQ request versus confirmed slot;
8. regulatory eligibility versus physical CIQ-berth availability;
9. CIQ reservation versus unlimited arrival window.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that berth reservation and immigration clearance are naturally separate administrative processes.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance strengthens if the guest experiences them as one arrival journey but they can fail, change or complete independently.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- exact berth-confirmation workflow;
- berth cancellation and amendment rules;
- confirmation SLA;
- occupancy hold duration;
- rate-lock behaviour after enquiry;
- internal coupling between berth and CIQ bookings;
- no-show rates.
