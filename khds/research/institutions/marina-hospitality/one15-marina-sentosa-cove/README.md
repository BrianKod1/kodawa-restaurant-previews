# ONE°15 Marina Sentosa Cove — KHDS Case #47

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Institution

ONE°15 Marina Sentosa Cove is being studied as an integrated yacht marina and waterfront hospitality destination in which one guest journey can depend on vessel dimensions, berth suitability, CIQ clearance, crew/passenger state, utilities, security, membership, hotel, dining, recreation, events and departure clearance.

The case includes:

- wet and dry berthing;
- superyacht accommodation;
- 24/7 CIQ clearance;
- vessel, crew and passenger documentation;
- berthing/unberthing and line-handling support;
- utilities and fuel;
- gated/security access;
- hotel accommodation;
- member-only and public dining;
- fitness/recreation;
- events;
- departure clearance and post-stay closure. [S1]-[S22]

Case #47 tests whether hospitality architecture changes when the operator does not own the guest’s transport asset but must host, provision, regulate and coordinate around it.

## Research date

Initial public-evidence research: 2026-11-12

## Research question

How does an integrated yacht marina and waterfront hospitality destination maintain one coherent arrival-to-shore journey when berth suitability, vessel dimensions, CIQ clearance, crew and passenger manifests, utility provisioning, fuel, security access, membership, hotel and dining entitlement, and departure timing operate as separate but interdependent service states?

## Competing explanation

The strongest competing explanation is ordinary marina administration:

vessel enquiry
+ berth allocation
+ CIQ
+ berthing
+ utilities/fuel
+ shore-side services
+ departure clearance
+ unberthing.

Under that explanation, ONE°15 is simply a premium marina club with normal yacht, border-control and private-club procedures.

Case #47 therefore tests whether the coupling among guest, vessel, berth, regulatory state and shore-side entitlement creates hospitality continuity and recovery problems beyond standard marina administration.

## Initial findings

### One destination contains multiple service relationships

ONE°15 presents marina, membership, hotel, dining, fitness and events separately within one destination. [S5][S6]

Therefore:

One Destination != One Service Relationship.

### Berth suitability depends on vessel state

Berthing depends on vessel dimensions and vessel configuration. [S1][S2]

Therefore:

Berth Available != Berth Suitable for Every Vessel.

And:

One Yacht != One Standard Capacity Unit.

### Berth enquiry is not berth confirmation

The public form is an enquiry workflow. [S2]

Therefore:

Berth Enquiry Submitted != Berth Confirmed.

### Berth and CIQ are separate reservations

The CIQ process is independently booked and documented. [S3]

Therefore:

Berth Reservation != CIQ Reservation.

### CIQ does not imply overnight berth entitlement

ONE°15 states that a vessel may clear through the marina without staying and that staying afterward remains subject to separate berth availability. [S3]

Therefore:

CIQ Clearance Reservation != Overnight Berth Entitlement.

### Vessel documents and people documents are separate readiness states

CIQ requires vessel, crew and passenger documentation. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Documentation Complete
!=
Passenger / Crew Documentation Complete.

### Manifest changes can reset approval state

Changes after ICA approval require cancellation/resubmission and a new CIQ request. [S3]

Therefore:

Previously Approved Clearance State
!=
Current Clearance State After Manifest Change.

### Physical arrival does not equal clearance

The vessel must complete CIQ formalities. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Physically Arrived != Vessel Cleared.

### Passenger arrival does not equal shore access

Passengers and crew must remain onboard until clearance is complete. [S3]

Therefore:

Passenger Arrived != Passenger Free to Disembark.

### Berth occupancy does not imply every local service is active

Utilities, fuel and security are separately managed. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Berthed != Utilities Active Automatically.

And:

Marina Presence != Security Access Everywhere.

### Berthing does not normalize shore-side entitlement

Hotel, club, dining, fitness, recreation and events remain separate states. [S8]-[S14]

Therefore:

Berthing Entitlement != Universal Shore-Side Entitlement.

### Vessel occupancy and hotel occupancy differ

The marina can host a vessel overnight while hotel stays remain a separate product. [S1][S3][S11]

Therefore:

Vessel Occupant != Hotel Guest.

### Public and private dining entitlements differ

Latitude is restricted to members and hotel guests, while other venues are open to the public. [S8][S9]

Therefore:

Marina Guest != One Universal Dining Role.

### Departure requires renewed regulatory readiness

Arrival clearance does not automatically authorize departure clearance. [S3]

Therefore:

Arrival Clearance Completed
!=
Departure Clearance Automatically Granted.

### Departure clearance does not equal unberthing

Unberthing remains an operational marina state. [S3][S4]

Therefore:

Departure Clearance Complete != Vessel Physically Unberthed.

### Waterfront narrative unity hides local state complexity

ONE°15 presents one coherent “home by the water” lifestyle across marina, hotel, dining, membership and events. [S15][S16][S19]-[S22]

Therefore:

Narrative Destination Unity
!=
Operational / Entitlement Unity.

## Berth-coupled hospitality test

Case #47 introduces a specific question:

Can one hospitality journey remain coherent when the guest’s privately owned or operated transport asset becomes a central capacity, regulatory and service object inside the hospitality destination?

The public evidence shows that:

- berth suitability depends on vessel state; [S1][S2]
- regulatory clearance and berth entitlement differ; [S3]
- passenger/crew state can determine regulatory readiness; [S3]
- utilities, fuel and security are local service states; [S1][S4]
- shore-side access depends on membership, hotel, dining and event state; [S8]-[S14]
- departure requires new regulatory and operational closure. [S3][S4]

Therefore:

Guest Journey State != Berth State Alone.

This generates a non-canonical research direction around Berth-Coupled Hospitality Architecture.
