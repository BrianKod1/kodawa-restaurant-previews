## Strongest competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that ONE°15 is a premium marina club with normal yacht, border-control and private-club procedures.

That remains credible.

A stronger architecture interpretation requires evidence that state mismatches materially affect:

- access;
- timing;
- service continuity;
- recovery;
- revenue;
- guest experience.

## Falsification standard

Berth-Coupled Hospitality Architecture would weaken if:

- berth suitability rarely affects guest continuity;
- vessel and guest states rarely diverge;
- CIQ rarely affects shore access;
- utilities/security are effectively automatic after berthing;
- membership/dining/hotel entitlements rarely create journey friction;
- departure clearance rarely changes closure;
- recovery is consistently owned by one service domain.

Current public evidence does not establish those falsifying conditions.

## Current bounded conclusion

Case #47 supports a bounded conclusion:

ONE°15 presents one coherent waterfront lifestyle destination while publicly documenting distinct vessel, berth, regulatory, utility, security, membership, hotel, dining, recreation, event and departure states.

This is materially relevant to KHDS.

It does not justify formal pattern promotion.
