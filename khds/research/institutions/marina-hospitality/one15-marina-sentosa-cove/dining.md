# Dining

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does dining operate when public visitors, members, hotel guests and marina users share the same waterfront destination but do not share identical restaurant entitlement?

## Dining is not one access model

ONE°15 publishes multiple dining venues with different service formats and access rules. [S8]

Therefore:

Dining at ONE°15
!=
One Restaurant Relationship.

## Latitude is restricted

Latitude Restaurant & Terrace is for ONE°15 Marina members and hotel guests only. [S8][S9]

Therefore:

Public Presence at Marina
!=
Latitude Dining Entitlement.

This is one of the clearest shore-side entitlement boundaries in Case #47.

## Public-access restaurants are different

ONE°15 states that WOK°15 Kitchen and Boaters Bar are open to the public. [S8]

Therefore:

Restaurant Located Inside Marina Destination
!=
Members-Only Automatically.

## Marina user does not automatically equal dining member

The public dining rules distinguish members, hotel guests and members of the public rather than treating all marina visitors as one dining category. [S8]

Therefore:

Marina Guest
!=
One Universal Dining Role.

## Reservation channels differ by role

ONE°15 directs Members to contact restaurants directly, while members of the public are directed to online restaurant reservation channels. [S8]

Therefore:

Same Restaurant Interest
!=
Same Reservation Path.

## Latitude reservation still requires confirmation

The Latitude reservation form states that reservations are subject to confirmation by the team. [S9]

Therefore:

Dining Request Submitted
!=
Dining Reservation Confirmed.

## Private dining adds commercial conditions

Latitude private dining rooms are subject to minimum-spend conditions. [S9]

Therefore:

Private Room Available
!=
Private Room Entitlement Without Commercial Threshold.

## Dining operates on local time states

The venues have different operating hours and last-order times. [S8]

Therefore:

Venue Exists
!=
Dining Service Available at Every Time.

## Dining can affect land-side access logistics

Public dining guests with valid same-day reservations can use designated complimentary shuttle arrangements, while other access modes remain available. [S8]

Therefore:

Dining Reservation
Can Activate
A Separate Arrival / Transport Entitlement.

This is a bounded service-link observation, not evidence of unified backend state.

## Dining promotions can be role-specific

Current promotions include venue-specific and time-specific offers, while some club promotions are member-only. [S11][S14]

Therefore:

Dining Outlet Access
!=
Promotion Entitlement Automatically.

## Strongest dining boundaries

1. dining destination versus one restaurant relationship;
2. public marina presence versus Latitude entitlement;
3. marina-located restaurant versus members-only automatically;
4. marina guest versus one universal dining role;
5. same dining interest versus same reservation path;
6. dining request versus confirmed reservation;
7. private room availability versus unconditional private-room entitlement;
8. venue existence versus time-available dining service;
9. dining access versus promotion entitlement;
10. dining reservation versus no effect on transport/access state.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is ordinary private-club restaurant access.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance is whether these access differences create friction or recovery needs when a boater experiences the property as one integrated destination.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- restaurant capacity;
- waitlist operation;
- reservation-system integration;
- how marina users are classified for dining;
- denial or recovery rates;
- room-charging or berth-account charging;
- cross-venue spend attribution.
