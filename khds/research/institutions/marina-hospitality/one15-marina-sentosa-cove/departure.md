# Departure / Unberthing & Exit Clearance

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

When does the marina journey actually end when berth release, unberthing, utilities, departure CIQ, crew/passenger presence, security access, billing and post-stay relationships can close at different times?

## Physical readiness to leave is not regulatory readiness

The CIQ process applies to departure as well as arrival and requires an advance booking plus vessel, crew and passenger documentation. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Ready to Depart
!=
Departure Clearance Ready.

## Departure clearance requires people-state alignment

For departures, ONE°15 recommends that everyone be onboard at least 30 minutes before the confirmed CIQ booking time to avoid missing the clearance slot. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Present
!=
Departure Party Ready.

And:

Confirmed Departure Slot
!=
Departure Clearance Guaranteed Regardless of Passenger State.

## Manifest changes can reset departure readiness

Changes to crew or passenger lists after ICA approval require cancellation/resubmission and a new CIQ request. [S3]

Therefore:

Previously Approved Departure State
!=
Current Departure State After Manifest Change.

## CIQ scheduling remains time-sensitive

Bookings are advance-only, subject to availability, and early/late changes should be communicated; cancellations or no-shows can create charges. [S3]

Therefore:

Departure CIQ Booking
!=
Unlimited Departure Window.

## Regulatory investigation can extend closure

ONE°15 states that further inspection, detention or extended CIQ processing can create additional costs and delays. [S3]

Therefore:

Departure Process Started
!=
Departure Cleared on Expected Timeline.

## Same-day arrival and departure are possible but require separate valid state

ONE°15 states that a vessel can clear into Singapore and depart the same day subject to valid booking, regulatory requirements and clearance approval. [S3]

Therefore:

Arrival Clearance Completed
!=
Departure Clearance Automatically Granted.

## Berth release is a separate operational state

Marina staff publicly handle unberthing, mooring, line handling, berth occupancy and licence validity. [S4]

Therefore:

Departure Clearance Complete
!=
Vessel Physically Unberthed.

And:

Vessel Unberthed
!=
Berth Administrative State Closed Automatically.

## Utilities must cease independently

Electricity and water usage are monitored as berth services. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Departing
!=
Utility State Closed Automatically.

The public record establishes monitoring, not the exact close-out workflow.

## Security entitlement can outlive or end separately from vessel movement

Keys and security passes are separately managed by marina operations. [S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Departure
!=
Security Credential State Closed Automatically.

## Guest relationships can persist ashore

Hotel, dining, membership, recreation and event relationships have their own contacts, reservations and operating states. [S8][S10][S12][S14]

Therefore:

Vessel Leaves Marina
!=
Every Guest Relationship Closed.

A person can remain a club member, hotel guest, diner, event participant or future customer after the vessel departs.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. vessel ready versus departure-clearance ready;
2. vessel present versus departure party ready;
3. confirmed slot versus guaranteed clearance regardless of people state;
4. previous approval versus current state after manifest change;
5. departure booking versus unlimited departure window;
6. process started versus cleared on expected timeline;
7. arrival clearance versus automatic departure clearance;
8. departure clearance versus physical unberthing;
9. unberthing versus berth administrative closure;
10. vessel departure versus utility closure;
11. vessel departure versus security credential closure;
12. vessel departure versus every guest relationship closed.

## Multi-Owner Recovery / Responsibility Continuity pressure

Departure can cross:

- marina operations;
- vessel owner/captain/agent;
- crew/passenger state;
- ICA/Customs/regulatory state;
- utilities;
- security;
- hotel/dining/member services.

Therefore:

One Marina Departure
!=
One Closure Owner.

## Berth-Coupled Hospitality pressure

The departure chain is:

Guest / Crew Ready
+
Vessel Ready
+
CIQ Ready
+
Berth Release
+
Utility Closure
+
Security Closure
+
Unberthing.

Therefore:

Physical Departure
!=
Administrative / Hospitality Closure.

This is directional evidence only.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is ordinary marina departure and border-control administration.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that cross-state mismatches materially affect recovery, delay, cost or guest experience.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- berth checkout procedure;
- utility billing close-out timing;
- security-pass return workflow;
- unberthing wait times;
- departure-clearance failure rates;
- actual dispute/recovery ownership;
- post-departure account settlement.
