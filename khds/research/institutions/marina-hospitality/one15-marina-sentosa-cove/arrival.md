# Arrival / Berthing & Clearance

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What changes when the vessel physically arrives, and how do berth, clearance, crew, passenger, visitor, utility and security states convert physical presence into usable marina and shore access?

## Physical arrival does not equal clearance

A vessel arriving for CIQ proceeds to the designated CIQ berth for immigration and clearance formalities. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Physically Arrived
!=
Vessel Cleared.

## Passenger arrival does not equal disembarkation entitlement

All listed passengers and crew must remain onboard and present until clearance is completed. [S3]

Therefore:

Passenger Arrived
!=
Passenger Free to Disembark.

This is one of the strongest Case #47 boundaries.

## Visitor access also depends on clearance state

Guests or visitors not part of the clearance process may board only after required clearance formalities are complete. [S3]

Therefore:

Visitor Invited
!=
Visitor May Board Vessel.

## Crew/passenger presence is itself a clearance requirement

All passengers and crew listed on the manifest must be onboard and present during clearance. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel at CIQ Berth
!=
Clearance Ready if Manifested Persons Are Missing.

## CIQ and overnight berth states remain separate after arrival

A vessel can clear through ONE°15 without overnight berthing. If it will remain at the marina, berth reservation is separate and subject to availability. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Cleared
!=
Overnight Berth Activated.

## Berthing involves active marina operations

ONE°15 publicly describes marina staff as assisting with berthing and unberthing, line handling, towing and checking berth occupancy and licence validity. [S4]

Therefore:

Berth Assigned
!=
Vessel Securely Berthed Automatically.

## Berth occupancy and licence state are distinct

Marina operations explicitly include checking both berth occupancy and licence validity. [S4]

Therefore:

Berth Occupied
!=
Berth Licence Valid Automatically.

## Utilities are separate local service states

The marina provides metered electricity and potable water, and marina operations monitor electricity and water usage. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Berthed
!=
Utilities Active / Consumed Automatically.

## Fuel is another independent service state

ONE°15 operates a fuel dock with petrol and diesel sales, while marina staff responsibilities include fuel-dock operation. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Berthed
!=
Fuel Service Completed.

## Security access is locally controlled

The marina advertises gated access, 24-hour security and CCTV; marina staff responsibilities include issuing keys and security passes and monitoring security. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Marina Presence
!=
Security Access Everywhere.

## Shore-side hospitality remains a separate entitlement layer

ONE°15 combines marina access with hotel, dining, fitness and member spaces, but those services have their own access and commercial relationships. [S1][S5][S6]

Therefore:

Berthing Entitlement
!=
Universal Shore-Side Entitlement.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

1. vessel physical arrival versus vessel cleared;
2. passenger arrival versus disembarkation entitlement;
3. visitor invitation versus boarding entitlement;
4. vessel at CIQ berth versus clearance readiness;
5. vessel cleared versus overnight berth activated;
6. berth assigned versus securely berthed;
7. berth occupied versus berth licence valid;
8. vessel berthed versus utilities active;
9. vessel berthed versus fuel service complete;
10. marina presence versus security access everywhere;
11. berthing entitlement versus universal shore-side entitlement.

## Berth-Coupled Hospitality pressure

Case #47 now exposes a coupled state chain:

Vessel State
+
Berth State
+
Clearance State
+
Crew / Passenger State
+
Utility State
+
Security State
+
Shore Entitlement State.

Therefore:

One Marina Arrival
!=
One Operational State.

This is directional evidence only.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is standard marina and border-control procedure.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that mismatches among these states materially affect journey continuity, recovery, experience or commercial outcomes.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- real-time berth assignment;
- docking wait times;
- clearance exception handling;
- line-handling response times;
- utility activation workflow;
- security-pass provisioning time;
- actual recovery ownership when berth, clearance and guest states diverge.
