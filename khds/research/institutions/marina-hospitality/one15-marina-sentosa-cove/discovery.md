# Discovery

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does a boater discover the correct marina journey when berth suitability, membership status, vessel dimensions, CIQ needs, shore-side hospitality and service access are separate but interdependent states?

## ONE°15 presents one destination with several service domains

ONE°15 Marina Sentosa Cove presents itself as an integrated waterfront destination combining marina berthing, hotel, dining, fitness, social experiences and member privileges. [S1][S5]

Therefore:

One Destination
!=
One Service Relationship.

## Marina discovery is not the same as club discovery

The public site separates Marina, Membership, Hotel, Dining, Events and other offerings. [S1][S5][S6]

Therefore:

Marina Guest
!=
Club Member Automatically.

And:

Berthing Interest
!=
Hotel / Dining / Membership Entitlement.

## Berth discovery is vessel-dependent

The marina advertises 270 wet berths including 33 mega-yacht berths, 60 dry berths and accommodation for superyachts up to 220ft. [S1]

Berthing pricing varies by vessel length, and width-related vessel configuration can trigger surcharges. [S1]

Therefore:

Berth Available
!=
Berth Suitable for Every Vessel.

And:

One Yacht
!=
One Standard Capacity Unit.

## The vessel itself becomes a discovery variable

The berth enquiry form asks for membership status, type of berthing, request type, arrival and departure dates, vessel name, vessel type and size. [S2]

Therefore:

Guest Interest
!=
Sufficient Berth-Match Information.

The marina must know something about the vessel before a berth relationship can be evaluated.

## CIQ is a separate journey path

ONE°15 operates a 24/7 Customs, Immigration and Quarantine service for pleasure craft and yachts, but the CIQ reservation process is separate from overnight berthing. [S1][S3][S7]

Therefore:

CIQ Needed
!=
Berth Reserved Automatically.

And:

Berth Needed
!=
CIQ Needed Automatically.

## International arrival creates a regulatory discovery layer

CIQ requires vessel, captain, crew, passenger and travel details, and different rules apply depending on whether the vessel is entering or departing Singapore. [S3]

Therefore:

Marina Journey
!=
Shore-Side Hospitality Journey Only.

## Scale changes berth-discovery requirements

The CIQ area can normally accommodate vessels below 125ft, while larger vessels require review based on berth availability. [S3]

Therefore:

Vessel Eligible for Marina
!=
Vessel Automatically Suitable for CIQ Berth.

This is one of the strongest Case #47 discovery boundaries.

## Marina support spans multiple operational services

Published marina operations include berthing and unberthing, towing, line handling, berth occupancy, licence validity, electricity and water monitoring, fuel-dock operation, security passes and liaison with Customs and Immigration. [S4]

Therefore:

Marina Discovery
!=
Dock-Space Discovery Only.

## Strongest discovery boundaries

1. one destination versus one service relationship;
2. marina guest versus club member;
3. berthing interest versus shore-side entitlement;
4. berth available versus berth suitable;
5. one yacht versus one standard capacity unit;
6. guest interest versus sufficient berth-match information;
7. CIQ need versus berth reservation;
8. vessel eligible for marina versus CIQ-berth suitability;
9. marina discovery versus dock-space discovery only.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that marinas naturally need vessel dimensions, berth categories and border-clearance information.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance becomes stronger if these separate states can diverge while the guest expects one coherent arrival-to-shore experience.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- berth-matching algorithms;
- live berth inventory;
- berth-allocation latency;
- conversion from enquiry to confirmed berth;
- occupancy by berth class;
- the internal identity link between vessel, owner, crew and guest;
- how frequently CIQ and berth availability conflict.
