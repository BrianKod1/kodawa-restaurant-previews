# Stay / Berth Occupancy & Shore Use

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does the hospitality state evolve after a yacht is securely berthed when berth occupancy, utilities, security, hotel use, recreation, member privileges, vessel servicing and shore access remain separate?

## Berth occupancy is not the entire stay state

ONE°15 publicly describes a marina stay alongside hotel, dining, recreation, fitness and club facilities. [S5][S8][S10][S11][S12]

Therefore:

Vessel Berthed
!=
Guest Stay Fully Defined.

A marina guest can continue into several different shore-side states.

## The vessel can remain the guest's accommodation environment

The marina supports overnight berthing separately from the onshore hotel product. [S1][S3][S11]

Therefore:

Overnight at Marina
!=
Hotel Stay Automatically.

And:

Vessel Occupant
!=
Hotel Guest.

## Hotel use creates a separate entitlement state

The destination markets waterfront hotel stays and room offers independently from berthing. [S5][S11][S12]

Therefore:

Berth Holder
!=
Hotel Room Holder.

This is a direct example of two accommodation states coexisting at one destination.

## Utilities are berth-local service states

The marina provides electricity and potable water, while marina operations monitor electricity and water usage. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Berth Occupied
!=
Utility Use Active Automatically.

And:

Berth Charge
!=
Every Utility State Automatically Included.

The public record establishes metering/monitoring but not every commercial bundling rule.

## Fuel is a separate vessel-service relationship

The marina operates a fuel dock and publicly identifies fuel operation as a distinct marina responsibility. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Vessel Staying at Marina
!=
Fuel Service Active Automatically.

## Security entitlement remains local

ONE°15 uses gated access, security passes, keys, CCTV and security monitoring. [S1][S4]

Therefore:

Guest Recognized
!=
Every Physical Area Accessible.

## Clubhouse facilities create differentiated access states

ONE°15's clubhouse facilities include a Members' lounge, changing areas, steam room, lockers, children's facilities and other recreation spaces with specific operating hours and membership context. [S10]

Therefore:

Shore Access
!=
Every Club Facility Entitlement.

## Recreation pricing differentiates member and guest states

Published fitness classes distinguish member, member's guest and trial pricing, and some activities require advance RSVP. [S13]

Therefore:

Member's Guest
!=
Member Entitlement.

And:

Physical Presence at Club
!=
Activity Participation Confirmed.

## Event participation adds another entitlement state

The event calendar contains Member-exclusive events and other activities priced differently for Members and Members' guests. [S14]

Therefore:

Club Presence
!=
Event Entitlement Automatically.

## Boater community is a distinct relationship

ONE°15 operates boater-specific community programming such as the Boaters' Meeting, which is exclusive to registered boaters. [S14]

Therefore:

Marina User
Can Hold
A Distinct Community Entitlement
Beyond Berth Occupancy.

## Service ownership is partitioned

ONE°15 lists separate operating contacts/hours for Marina, Hotel, Membership, Fitness, Recreation, Dining and Events. [S12]

Therefore:

One Destination
!=
One Service Desk State.

This is strong pressure toward Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture.

## Strongest stay boundaries

1. vessel berthed versus guest stay fully defined;
2. overnight marina stay versus hotel stay;
3. vessel occupant versus hotel guest;
4. berth holder versus hotel room holder;
5. berth occupied versus utilities active;
6. marina stay versus fuel service;
7. guest recognized versus every physical area accessible;
8. shore access versus every club facility entitlement;
9. member's guest versus member entitlement;
10. club presence versus activity/event entitlement;
11. one destination versus one service desk.

The stay layer now contains:

Berth State
+
Vessel State
+
Utility State
+
Security State
+
Accommodation State
+

Therefore:

Stay at Marina
!=
One Local Service State.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that a marina club naturally operates different services with different access rules.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that these differentiated states materially affect continuity, recovery or the guest's perception of one integrated destination.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- berth-level utility activation workflow;
- guest-to-vessel identity linkage;
- hotel-booking linkage to marina bookings;
- security-pass provisioning latency;
- recreation denial or exception rates;
- cross-service recovery ownership;
- actual use of club facilities by visiting boaters.
