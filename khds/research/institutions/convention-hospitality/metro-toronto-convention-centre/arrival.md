# Arrival / Move-In & Attendee Access

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What does arrival mean when exhibitors, freight, contractors, organizers and attendees enter through different processes, at different times, with different safety, access and service entitlements?

## Event arrival has multiple phases

The Operating Guidelines distinguish move-in, attendee/event operation and move-out. Attendee Wi-Fi activates with attendee registration/sessions or exhibit opening and is not available during move-in or move-out. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Exists != Same Service State Across Event Phases.

## Exhibitor arrival is logistics-controlled

Freight and equipment cannot move through main public entrances, passenger elevators or escalators. Exhibitors use designated loading facilities and scheduled processes such as Voyage Control where applicable. [S4][S6]

Therefore:

Physical Arrival at Venue != Freight Access Authorized.

Public Entrance != Exhibitor Logistics Entrance.

## Loading access is time-specific

Organizer move-in schedules remain subject to MTCC approval, and exhibitors can require scheduled unloading slots. [S4][S6]

Therefore:

Valid Exhibitor != Any-Time Loading-Dock Access.

## Show Management participates in arrival authorization

Exhibitors are directed to Show Management for move-in/out instructions and Voyage Control codes. [S6]

Therefore:

Venue Infrastructure != Complete Arrival Authority.

## Security begins before the attendee-facing event

The Licensee's security responsibility applies from initial move-in through completion of move-out. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Security State != Attendee-Open State Only.

## Access control is role-sensitive

Security may monitor entrances, high-traffic areas, valuable displays, capacity and PPE during move-in/out. [S4]

Therefore:

Valid Event Presence != Same Access Conditions for Every Role.

## Venue arrival is not event admission

MTCC lists events but does not sell tickets; ticketing belongs to the event organizer or affiliated ticketing channel. [S8]

Therefore:

Arrival at MTCC != Event Admission Automatically.

Venue Open != Attendee Entitled to Specific Event Entry.

## Guest Services is venue support

Guest Services provides wayfinding and facility information. [S9]

Therefore:

Venue Assistance != Event Admission Authority.

## Registration areas remain venue-controlled public space

Registration and pre-function areas are public/common areas requiring MTCC approval for event use. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Registration Located at Venue != Registration Area Under Organizer's Unrestricted Control.

## Accessibility can remain resource-constrained

Courtesy wheelchairs are first-come, first-served, and visitors needing emergency-evacuation support are asked to notify Security upon arrival. [S10]

Therefore:

Accessible Venue Arrival != Courtesy Wheelchair Guaranteed.

Accessibility Need Known to Visitor != Emergency Support State Activated Automatically.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

1. event existence versus uniform service state across phases;
2. physical arrival versus freight authorization;
3. public entrance versus logistics entrance;
4. exhibitor validity versus any-time dock access;
5. venue infrastructure versus complete arrival authority;
6. security state versus attendee-open state;
7. valid presence versus uniform access conditions;
8. venue arrival versus event admission;
9. venue open versus specific-event entitlement;
10. venue assistance versus admission authority;
11. venue registration versus unrestricted organizer control;
12. accessible arrival versus guaranteed courtesy wheelchair;
13. accessibility need versus emergency-support activation.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish attendee credential-scanning ownership, queue-time performance, failed-ticket escalation, dock wait times, registration-system integration with MTCC security, or wheelchair demand versus supply.
