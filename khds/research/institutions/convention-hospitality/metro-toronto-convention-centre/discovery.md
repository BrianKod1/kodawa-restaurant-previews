# Discovery

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does a planner, exhibitor or attendee discover the relevant MTCC relationship when event booking, show participation, ticketing, exhibitor services, accessibility, supplier services and venue navigation are separate public pathways?

## Role-specific discovery

MTCC separates planning, exhibiting and attending into different public journeys. Planner resources focus on booking and event design; exhibitor resources focus on services and move-in/out; attendee resources focus on events, directions, parking, guest services and accessibility. [S2][S5][S8][S9][S10]

Therefore:

One Event != One Discovery Path.

Planner != Exhibitor != Attendee.

## Event enquiry is multi-state

The booking enquiry asks for event type, dates, whether a set-up day is required, estimated attendance, room setup, breakout rooms, exhibition component, approximate exhibitors or hall size, public/trade status and food-and-beverage requirements. [S1]

Therefore:

Venue Enquiry != Simple Room Search.

## Public visibility is incomplete

MTCC's calendar distinguishes public and trade events but states that not every hosted event is listed. [S8]

Therefore:

Event Hosted at MTCC != Event Publicly Discoverable on MTCC Calendar.

Venue Event != Public Event Automatically.

## Ticketing is external to the venue

MTCC states that it does not sell tickets to events and directs attendees to the event for ticket purchase and information. [S8]

Therefore:

Venue Discovery != Ticketing Relationship.

MTCC Attendee != MTCC Ticket Customer Automatically.

## Exhibitors encounter a separate supplier ecosystem

MTCC publishes exhibitor forms and a dedicated Exhibitor Services Centre. Services are divided among in-house, official exclusive and official preferred providers. [S5][S7]

Therefore:

Exhibitor Participation != One Supplier Relationship.

One Venue != One Service Owner.

## Move-in discovery depends on Show Management

MTCC tells exhibitors to use the Exhibitor Kit supplied by Show Management or speak with the Show Manager for move-in/out timing. [S6]

Therefore:

Venue Loading Capacity != Exhibitor Move-In Authorization.

## Attendee venue support is not event admission

Guest Services provides wayfinding, lost-and-found and visitor information. [S9]

Therefore:

Attendee Venue Support != Event Registration or Ticketing.

## Accessibility resources can remain capacity-limited

Courtesy wheelchairs are available first-come, first-served. [S10]

Therefore:

Accessibility Need != Specific Courtesy Resource Guaranteed.

## Strongest discovery boundaries

1. one event versus one discovery path;
2. planner versus exhibitor versus attendee;
3. venue enquiry versus simple room search;
4. hosted event versus public calendar visibility;
5. venue discovery versus ticketing relationship;
6. exhibitor participation versus one supplier relationship;
7. one venue versus one service owner;
8. loading capacity versus move-in authorization;
9. venue support versus event admission;
10. accessibility need versus guaranteed courtesy resource.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that MTCC is a conventional venue with role-specific web pages. That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance becomes stronger if those role-specific pathways correspond to independently governed service states that must be coordinated during one event.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish booking-enquiry conversion, attendee discovery conversion, exhibitor service-order completion, shared identity across role systems, or current demand for accessibility resources.

## Next evidence
