# Departure / Move-Out

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

When does a convention event actually end when attendee-facing activity can stop before exhibitor dismantling, freight removal, cleaning, security, key return and dock operations are complete?

## Attendee departure is not event-system closure

MTCC Attendee Wi-Fi remains active for the event but explicitly excludes the move-out phase. [S4]

Therefore:

Attendee-Facing Event End != Venue Operational End.

The event can transition into a different operational phase after attendee services stop.

## Move-out is a separately governed phase

The Licensee's security responsibility continues until completion of move-out. [S4]

Therefore:

Doors Closed to Attendees != Security Responsibility End.

## Freight removal remains controlled after the show

Move-out uses designated loading areas and logistics processes rather than public entrances. MTCC can use Voyage Control and dock scheduling to manage these movements. [S4][S6]

Therefore:

Event Program End != Freight Access Uncontrolled.

## Electrical service can terminate on its own timeline

The Operating Guidelines state that electrical services are disconnected shortly after move-out of exhibits begins. [S4]

Therefore:

Move-Out Begins != Every Technical Service Remains Active.

## Cleaning responsibility survives the public event

The Licensee is responsible for costs related to removal of event waste, recycling, crates, pallets, packing material, lumber and litter before opening, during the event and following move-out through MTCC's exclusive cleaning service. [S4]

Therefore:

Attendee Departure != Cleaning Obligation Complete.

## Exhibitor booth-cleaning compliance can affect organizer cost

Interior booth cleaning is exclusive to MTCC, and additional charges may reach show management where exhibitors or contractors do not comply with the cleaning exclusivity. [S4]

Therefore:

Individual Exhibitor Non-Compliance
Can Affect
Organizer-Level Cost State.

This is a direct nested-dependency boundary.

## Late move-out creates a new occupancy state

The current Operating Guidelines distinguish approved late move-out from unapproved late move-out and apply additional occupancy/labour costs where material remains beyond contracted time. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Contract End Time != Automatic Physical Clearance.

And:

Physical Material Remaining != Contractual Occupancy Closed.

## Move-out closure includes loading-dock clearance

Late move-out ends only once material has been removed from exhibit halls and loading-dock areas. [S4]

Therefore:

Exhibit Hall Empty != Move-Out Complete Automatically.

## Key return can persist beyond physical move-out

MTCC applies charges when show-management keys or key cards are not returned within the stated grace period after move-out. [S4]

Therefore:

Physical Move-Out Complete != Administrative Closure Complete.

## Lost property can outlive the event

MTCC Security handles lost and found, including after an attendee has left the venue. [S12][S19]

Therefore:

Guest Physical Departure != Guest-Service Relationship Necessarily Closed.

## Parking departure has its own payment/exit state

MTCC publishes separate parking entry and exit payment procedures for its garages. [S18]

Therefore:

Event Departure != Parking Transaction Complete Automatically.

## Waste and donation programs can continue after service

MTCC's sustainability programs allow food and leftover-event items to be redirected or donated. [S15][S16]

Therefore:

Event Asset No Longer Needed != Asset Lifecycle Closed Automatically.

The public record does not establish how often individual events use this option.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. attendee-facing end versus operational end;
2. attendee doors closed versus security responsibility ended;
3. program end versus uncontrolled freight access;
4. move-out begins versus all technical services remain active;
5. attendee departure versus cleaning obligation complete;
6. exhibitor non-compliance versus isolated exhibitor cost only;
7. contract end time versus physical clearance;
8. material remaining versus occupancy closed;
9. exhibit hall empty versus move-out complete;
10. physical move-out versus administrative closure;
11. guest departure versus guest-service relationship closure;
12. event departure versus parking transaction completion;
13. asset no longer needed versus asset lifecycle closed.

## Multi-owner recovery pressure

Move-out can involve:

- show management;
- exhibitors;
- general-service contractors;
- MTCC loading-dock staff;
- security;
- cleaning;
- electrical/service suppliers;
- parking;
- lost-and-found/security teams. [S4][S6][S12][S18]

Therefore:

One Event Closure != One Operational Owner.

This is strong directional pressure toward Multi-Owner Recovery / Responsibility Continuity.

This remains a research model, not a canonical pattern.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that convention venues naturally need teardown and cleaning after attendees leave.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that phase transitions, entitlement changes and ownership handoffs materially affect continuity, cost or recovery.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- average move-out duration;
- frequency of late move-out;
- dock-congestion metrics;
- cleanup turnaround time;
- frequency of exhibitor non-compliance charges;
- technical-service shutdown incidents;
- incident ownership after attendee departure;
- whether MTCC exposes a unified closure-state dashboard to organizers.
