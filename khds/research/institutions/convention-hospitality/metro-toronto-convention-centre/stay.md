# Stay / Event Participation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does participation operate after an event is open when attendee, exhibitor, organizer, production, technology, accessibility, cleaning and security states remain role- and phase-specific?

## Event participation is not one service state

MTCC's 2026-27 Operating Guidelines distinguish attendees/delegates from show managers, staff, speakers, exhibitors, volunteers and registration companies for technology-service purposes. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Participant != One Uniform Role.

And:

Valid Event Presence != Uniform Service Entitlement.

## Attendee Wi-Fi is phase- and role-specific

MTCC Attendee Wi-Fi is arranged and paid for by show management and is activated when attendee registration/sessions begin or when the exhibit portion opens. It excludes move-in and move-out phases. Production-related users and event-management functions require separate service arrangements. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Open != Wi-Fi Entitlement for Every Role.

And:

Event Contracted != Technology Service Active at Every Phase.

## Public Wi-Fi and attendee Wi-Fi are different service states

Public Wi-Fi is available in public/common/pre-function spaces on a best-effort basis, while attendee Wi-Fi is an event-specific service with broader event-space coverage and different conditions. [S4]

Therefore:

Internet Available at Venue != Same Network Entitlement Everywhere.

## Business-critical connectivity is separately provisioned

MTCC recommends production/exhibitor-level or wired services where connectivity is business critical. [S4]

Therefore:

Casual Connectivity Entitlement != Business-Critical Connectivity Readiness.

## Exhibitor services remain separately orderable during participation

MTCC's online ordering tool allows exhibitors to order booth cleaning, high-speed internet, parking passes and telecommunications separately. [S13]

Therefore:

Exhibitor Admitted != Exhibitor Services Fully Provisioned.

## Cleaning exposes another nested service boundary

MTCC provides some general cleaning within common and event areas, while registration, feature areas and interior booth cleaning can incur separate charges and may be exclusive MTCC services. [S4]

Therefore:

Venue Cleaning Present != Every Event Area Cleaning Included.

And:

Exhibitor Booth Present != Interior Booth Cleaning Included.

## Security persists across the event lifecycle

MTCC maintains perimeter/internal patrol security, while the Licensee remains responsible for security within licensed event spaces from initial move-in through completion of move-out. [S4]

Therefore:

Venue Security != Complete Event Security Ownership.

## Guest Services supports venue participation, not event-program ownership

Guest Services supports wayfinding, lost and found, facility questions, local attractions and other visitor needs. [S12]

Therefore:

Venue Guest Support != Event Program Support Automatically.

## Lost property crosses service ownership

Lost and found is handled by MTCC Security even though the lost item may arise during an organizer-controlled event. [S12]

Therefore:

Event-Originated Guest Problem != Organizer-Owned Recovery Automatically.

This provides directional pressure toward Multi-Owner Recovery / Responsibility Continuity.

## Accessibility remains resource- and context-sensitive

Courtesy wheelchairs are available first-come, first-served. Theatre accessibility includes designated wheelchair seating, but availability is tied to specific event/theatre conditions. [S10][S19]

Therefore:

Accessible Facility != Every Accessibility Resource Guaranteed on Demand.

## Meeting-space capability is locally variable

MTCC's 77 meeting rooms vary in size, configuration, built-in technology and facilities. [S20]

Therefore:

Meeting Room Assigned != Uniform Room Capability.

## Strongest stay / participation boundaries

1. event participant versus one uniform role;
2. valid event presence versus uniform service entitlement;
3. event open versus Wi-Fi entitlement for every role;
4. event contracted versus technology active at every phase;
5. venue internet versus one network entitlement;
6. casual connectivity versus business-critical readiness;
7. exhibitor admission versus fully provisioned services;
8. venue cleaning versus every area included;
9. exhibitor booth versus booth cleaning included;
10. venue security versus complete event-security ownership;
11. venue guest support versus event-program support;
12. event-originated problem versus organizer-owned recovery;
13. accessible facility versus guaranteed accessibility resource;
14. meeting-room assignment versus uniform capability.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that large convention venues naturally sell or allocate services separately.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance strengthens if role- and phase-specific service states must remain coordinated so that the event feels continuous despite distributed ownership.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- real-time service activation dashboards;
- exhibitor-service fulfilment rates;
- Wi-Fi incident/recovery performance;
- attendee queue or congestion metrics;
- cleaning-service SLA performance;
- lost-and-found recovery rates;
- cross-team incident ownership.
