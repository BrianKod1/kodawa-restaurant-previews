# Reservation / Event Contracting

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What does an organizer actually secure when an event becomes contracted at MTCC, and which exhibitor, attendee, public-area and supplier entitlements remain separately governed?

## Enquiry is not contract

MTCC provides a public booking-enquiry process. [S1] The current Operating Guidelines state that they are an integral part of the License Agreement and must be reviewed by the Licensee. [S4]

Therefore:

Booking Enquiry Submitted != Event Space Contracted.

Event Interest != Licensee Status.

## Licensee is not every participant

The Operating Guidelines use the Licensee/Licensor relationship for organizer-side obligations. [S4]

Therefore:

Licensee != Every Event Participant.

## Contracted space does not confer unrestricted public-area control

Main entrances, registration areas and pre-function spaces are public/common areas outside the Licensee's License Agreement. Registration, exhibits, displays and signage there require advance MTCC approval. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Space Contracted != Public Areas Controlled.

Registration Activity != Automatically Authorized by Venue Contract.

## One contract does not activate all services

MTCC uses in-house, official exclusive and official preferred supplier relationships. [S4][S7]

Therefore:

License Agreement != Every Service Order.

## Some services are venue-exclusive

MTCC retains exclusive control over food and beverage and telecommunications in the facility. [S4][S7]

Therefore:

Organizer Choice of Venue != Organizer Choice of Every Supplier.

## Exhibitors remain separate service customers

MTCC's Exhibitor Services Coordinator works with individual exhibitors, and exhibitors can separately order services such as parking, booth cleaning, internet, telecommunications and dock parking. [S4][S5]

Therefore:

Organizer Contract != Exhibitor Service Order.

Exhibitor Participation != All Venue Services Included.

## Technology entitlement is role-specific

The Operating Guidelines distinguish attendees/delegates from show managers, staff, speakers, exhibitors, volunteers and registration companies. Attendee Wi-Fi does not cover every production role or every phase. [S4]

Therefore:

Attendee != Event Production Participant.

Valid Event Role != Uniform Technology Entitlement.

## Occupancy is time-bounded

Access outside contracted occupancy can incur additional charges unless otherwise agreed. [S4]

Therefore:

Event Contracted != Venue Access at Any Time.

## Strongest reservation / contracting boundaries

1. enquiry versus contracted event;
2. event interest versus Licensee status;
3. Licensee versus every participant;
4. contracted space versus public-area control;
5. registration versus automatic authorization;
6. License Agreement versus every service order;
7. venue choice versus every supplier choice;
8. organizer contract versus exhibitor service order;
9. attendee versus production participant;
10. valid event role versus uniform technology entitlement;
11. contracted event versus unrestricted access time.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish the executed standard License Agreement beyond the published guidelines, base-rental deposit structure, organizer cancellation economics, or system integration between organizer, exhibitor and attendee records.
