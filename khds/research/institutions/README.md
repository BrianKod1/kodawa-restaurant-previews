Purpose

KHDS began with restaurants because dining provides a concentrated hospitality journey: discovery, arrival, atmosphere, participation, service, payment and memory.

Institution research tests whether KHDS findings transfer to hospitality systems with:

longer or differently bounded guest journeys;

multiple service environments;

overnight, repeated or open-ended participation;

more complex access and capacity systems;

greater privacy and data requirements;

additional staff, partner or operator handoffs;

distributed responsibility;

and more consequential failure recovery.

The purpose is not to make every hospitality institution behave like a restaurant.

It is to determine:

which mechanisms recur;

which mechanisms change by institution type;

which new failure states appear;

which boundaries survive sector transfer;

and what evidence would justify formal maturity movement.

Selection Rule

Institutions are selected for research value rather than prestige alone.

Selection considers:

contradiction value;

pressure on current Candidate and Emerging patterns;

a materially different hospitality model;

official evidence availability;

operational and ethical boundaries;

geographic and cultural variation;

pressure on the institution-comparison architecture;

and potential relevance to future Kodawa Labs products.

The next institution must not be selected merely to confirm what KHDS already believes.

It should expose a meaningful transfer question or failure condition.

Governance Rule

Non-restaurant institutions belong to:

Track A — Independent Hospitality Research

They may contribute to pattern maturity only through the same evidence and governance standards applied to restaurants.

Sector Expansion

≠

Automatic Transferability

A restaurant pattern does not become a universal hospitality pattern merely because it appears plausible in a hotel, wellness venue or shared venue.

Research must establish:

what mechanism recurs;

what conditions make it recur;

what changes by sector;

what new failure state appears;

what contradiction survives;

what claim boundary remains necessary;

and whether the available evidence demonstrates mechanism, performance, experience, outcome or causality.
