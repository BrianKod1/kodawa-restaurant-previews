# KHDS Hospitality Institution Research

## Status

Expansion began with **KHDS Case #21 — Fogo Island Inn**. Its public-evidence dossier is complete; administrative closure is complete at public-evidence scope as recorded in `khds/state/current-case.json`.

First institution type: **Boutique Hotel**.

## Purpose

KHDS began with restaurants because dining provides a concentrated hospitality journey: discovery, arrival, atmosphere, participation, service, payment and memory.

The next research phase tests whether KHDS findings transfer to hospitality institutions with:

- longer guest journeys;
- multiple service environments;
- overnight or repeated participation;
- more complex access and capacity systems;
- greater privacy and data requirements;
- additional staff handoffs;
- and more consequential failure recovery.

## Institution Categories

Initial categories are:

- boutique hotels;
- wellness and spa institutions;
- food halls;
- members' clubs;
- cultural hospitality venues;
- resorts and retreats;
- hostels and social stays;
- event and conference venues;
- and other institutions where welcome, care, access and participation are central to the operating model.

Category inclusion does not create a fixed quota beyond the active research cycle.

## Shared Five-Dimension Interpretation

| Dimension | Non-restaurant interpretation |
|---|---|
| Arrival | Discovery, pre-arrival preparation, physical threshold, check-in, orientation and handoff |
| Atmosphere | Environmental, social, sensory and operational conditions across the stay or visit |
| Photography | Proof of place, use cases, rooms, services, access, difference and institutional capability |
| Reservation | Booking, eligibility, capacity, allocation, participation, modification, cancellation and recovery |
| Story | Origin, worldview, place, service model, institutional role and evidence-supported outcomes |

## Selection Rule

Institutions are selected for research value rather than prestige alone.

Selection considers:

1. contradiction value;
2. pressure on current Candidate and Emerging patterns;
3. a materially different hospitality model;
4. official evidence availability;
5. operational and ethical boundaries;
6. geographic and cultural variation;
7. and potential relevance to future Kodawa Labs products.

## Governance Rule

Non-restaurant institutions belong to:

**Track A — Independent Hospitality Research**.

They may contribute to pattern maturity only through the same evidence and governance standards applied to restaurants.

```text
Sector Expansion

≠

Automatic Transferability
```

A restaurant pattern does not become a universal hospitality pattern merely because it appears plausible in a hotel, spa or venue.

The research must establish:

- what mechanism recurs;
- what changes by sector;
- what new failure state appears;
- and what claim boundary remains necessary.

## Case #21 Direction

KHDS Case #21 studied Fogo Island Inn, a boutique-scale hotel / inn in Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

Primary transfer question:

> How do KHDS arrival, atmosphere, proof, participation and story patterns change when hospitality extends across pre-arrival, check-in, an overnight stay, multiple service requests and departure?

Priority pressure areas include:

- layered arrival across time;
- direct booking and guest-relationship ownership;
- room and service capacity proof;
- privacy and role-bounded guest data;
- human assistance across multiple handoffs;
- accessibility before and during the stay;
- atmosphere through continuity;
- optional versus required participation;
- service recovery;
- and post-stay relationship design.

The selected institution is [Fogo Island Inn](hotels/fogo-island-inn-newfoundland/README.md). Case #22 has no selected institution and is not authorized.
