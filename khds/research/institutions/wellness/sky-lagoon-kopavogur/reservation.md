## Supported observations

The operator-branded brochure distinguishes Saman public changing and Sér private changing. Its two-person option pairs passes with drinks and a shared platter. [S12](https://pub-mediabox-storage.rxweb-prd.com/exhibitor/document/exh-b418eda1-3201-4177-859b-809de7da96ac/8bd30938-933e-4b46-82e0-5ba3c9c18294.pdf) Accessed 2026-09-09.

The Icelandair guide describes both pass types; its offer remains a separate seller channel. [S13](https://www.icelandair.com/arora/sky-lagoon-guide/) [S4](https://www.icelandair.com/vacations/tours/sky-lagoon-pure-pass-without-transfers/) Accessed 2026-09-09.

Historical seller scope, inspected 2026-09-09: the Icelandair admission-only Saman offer included admission, ritual, shared changing and towel; its eligibility was minimum age 12, with ages 12–14 accompanied by an adult. [S4](https://www.icelandair.com/vacations/tours/sky-lagoon-pure-pass-without-transfers/) This records that offer, not newly verified direct-venue eligibility.

The US-facing package terms identify Icelandair as contracting party. Separate add-on tours are non-refundable but may change to equal/higher-value alternatives subject to availability and an amendment fee; package-included tours are non-refundable and non-changeable. [S6](https://www.icelandair.com/support/terms-and-conditions/packages/) Contract and Standard Tours sections; original inspection and focused reread 2026-09-09, source undated. These are seller/package distinctions, not universal Sky Lagoon terms or an assessment of any particular booking.

An advertised offer is not simultaneous proof of admission, changing, ritual-room or assistance capacity. Test whether a prospective guest can identify their seller, inclusions and change route before committing; booking failure should not require repeated sensitive disclosure. No such test was performed.

## Interpretation

Keep seller, package, privacy entitlement, refreshments and transport distinct; do not combine conditions across channels.
