## Research summary

**What we observed.** Seller eligibility and package distinctions ([reservation](reservation.md), S4/S6), request-handling limits ([pre-arrival](pre-arrival.md), S6), published arrival guidance ([arrival](arrival.md), S13), documented equipment (S11) and a seasonal return timetable ([departure](departure.md), S14) are attributable public evidence. The original photo and three later frames show selected compositions, not the whole experience.

**What KHDS can infer.** Sale, entitlement, assistance acceptance, participation and return have different responsibility boundaries. The [mechanism analysis](knowledge-extraction.md#mechanism-records) connects those boundaries to evidence levels and unresolved recovery. Published instructions do not demonstrate successful service.

**What Kodawa Labs could build or test — proposed applications.**

| Guest problem | Proposed application | Practical test, not yet performed |
|---|---|---|
| Confusing seller and inclusion/change rules | Channel-specific entitlement card | Ask readers to identify the seller, paid extras and change route from contrasting sample offers |
| Mistaking request receipt for accepted assistance | Receipt/forwarding/acceptance status display | Simulate a request and test whether readers can identify the accepting owner and unresolved limits |
| Uncertainty about declining a ritual step | Attributed participation-alternatives card | Test whether readers distinguish published seller advice from confirmed venue options; require dated venue rules before deployment |
| Late pickup or missed return leaves responsibility unclear | Timed travel card with exception owner | Simulate a missed connection and test whether the next responsible recipient is explicit |

Revenue, conversion, service and recovery improvements remain unmeasured hypotheses.

**What remains uncertain.** Direct contract, current individual equipment suitability, venue confirmation of seller guidance, comprehensive participation alternatives, current menu/dietary handling, exit/payment recovery and desktop/mobile presentation. No operational, causal or independent community-benefit outcome is established.

## Limitations and portability

The supported browser runtime reported no available browser; discovery returned an empty list. The host must expose a supported browser connection before rendered reading can be verified. A more specific installer or credential requirement could not be established. Official HTML requests returned loading shells in this session; this is not evidence that the website is defective. Desktop/mobile rendering, bookings, payments and submitted forms were not tested.

Official documents and operator HTML were the next fallback, followed by attributed tourism-directory material. The Icelandic accessibility PDF was readable although its English counterpart returned 404. Undated documents are not current guarantees. Promotional photos cannot prove access, service, crowding or health outcomes. Current-menu search extracts are excluded.

Local clickable links stay inside this folder and work in a standalone copy containing all fourteen files. External sources require network access: their documents and photographs are not bundled. The pre-revision ZIP is not evidence that these additions are bundled; this review neither rebuilds nor certifies an archive.
