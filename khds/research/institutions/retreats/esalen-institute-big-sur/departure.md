# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How do room possession, campus access, program participation, meals and optional services end relative to one another?

## Departure is not one moment

Planning guidance states that rooms must be vacated by 10 AM while guests may remain on the property until 2 PM. [S3]

This creates at least two distinct closure states:

- room possession ends;
- property access ends later.

Therefore:

Room checkout
!=
immediate end of guesthood.

- morning room checkout;
- afternoon use of common facilities;
- optional treatment extensions;
- separate program schedules.

Therefore:

Different expiration times
!=
automatically different hospitality relationships.
