# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a traveller know or possess before arrival, and how strongly does airport journey state determine whether the airside hotel can actually be reached?

## Evidence

Pre-arrival preparation is constrained by the hotel's location inside Istanbul Airport's secure international airside area.

The operator states that access to YOTELAIR Airside requires a valid boarding pass for an international flight. [S1][S2]

The Sleep Cabin product carries the same airside eligibility condition. [S3][S4]

The Sleep Cabin booking material also states that only hand luggage is permitted rather than large checked luggage. [S4]

These conditions mean that having an accommodation reservation does not by itself establish that the guest can physically reach the product.

The YOTEL Istanbul comparison material separates the Airside and Landside hotels according to terminal position and traveller state. [S2]

The Landside sister hotel is before security and passport control and can be accessed without the same international-flight boarding-pass requirement. [S5]

The public evidence therefore creates several relevant pre-arrival objects and conditions:

- accommodation reservation;
- international-flight boarding pass;
- airside eligibility;
- baggage status;
- security and passport-control position;
- correct selection of Airside versus Landside property.

## Interpretation

The strongest bounded observation is:

Hotel reservation != guaranteed physical accessibility.

A second distinction is:

Airport journey state != accommodation preference.

The traveller's flight, boarding-pass, baggage and terminal circumstances can determine whether the booked hospitality product is reachable.

That makes pre-arrival eligibility part of the hospitality relationship rather than merely background airport context.

However, the evidence does not establish how frequently eligibility problems occur or how effectively YOTEL prevents incorrect bookings.

## Limits

The inspected sources do not fully establish handling for a traveller who must collect checked baggage before an onward flight.

They do not fully establish recovery when a traveller books Airside but cannot legally or operationally enter the secure international transit area.

The effect of unusual transfer itineraries, immigration requirements, cancelled flights or changed boarding-pass status has not been comprehensively documented.

Booking-confirmation and reminder communications have not been systematically inspected.

## Next evidence

Inspect confirmation emails, booking-flow warnings and official arrival guidance for boarding-pass eligibility, baggage handling, transfer conditions, passport control and security.

Determine how early the Airside-versus-Landside boundary is communicated and what recovery path is offered when a booked guest cannot reach the selected property.
