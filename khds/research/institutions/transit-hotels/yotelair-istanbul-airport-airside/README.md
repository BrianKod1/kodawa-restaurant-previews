# Case #33 - YOTELAIR Istanbul Airport (Airside)

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Institution

YOTELAIR Istanbul Airport (Airside) is being studied as an airside transit-hotel environment combining hourly accommodation, overnight accommodation, standalone Sleep Cabins, shared lounge access and airport-dependent eligibility conditions.

Sector: transit-hotels

Research access date: 2026-10-05.

This dossier is based on inspected public sources. It is not an on-site audit, transaction test, airport-transfer test, accessibility assessment, service-recovery test or proof of representative guest outcomes.

## Research role

Case #33 tests whether the provisional Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture observed in earlier KHDS cases recurs when lodging is reintroduced inside a constrained airport environment.

The primary falsification question is:

Does Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recur in an airside transit hotel where short stays, overnight stays, airport access conditions and shared hospitality spaces overlap, or do those differences collapse into one conventional hotel-room product?

The case deliberately introduces conditions absent from Case #32:

- a private lodging relationship;
- both hourly and overnight accommodation;
- airport-side eligibility as a prerequisite to physical access;
- a separate single-guest Sleep Cabin product;
- shared bathroom and shower facilities for the Sleep Cabin product;
- separately purchasable lounge access;
- a related Landside sister hotel with materially different airport-access conditions.

## Material source conflicts

The inspected material contains two unresolved duration conflicts.

S2 describes Sleep Cabins as bookable by the hour for a maximum of four hours.

S3 describes Sleep Cabins as bookable by the hour with a minimum of four hours.

The dossier does not reconcile this conflict without further evidence.

S1 also contains inconsistent Revolve Lounge duration language. One section describes the EUR 80 package as four hours, while another describes 24-hour lounge access from EUR 80.

Those duration conditions therefore remain uncertain for Case #33.

## Initial boundaries under test

The evidence creates several distinctions worth testing without treating them as validated patterns:

Passenger eligibility != hotel entitlement.

Airside eligibility != Landside eligibility.

Hourly stay != overnight stay.

Sleep Cabin relationship != standard-room relationship.

Accommodation entitlement != entitlement to every ancillary service.

Lounge access != accommodation booking.

Shared operator identity != identical customer relationship.

Physical co-location != uniform service access.

Airport access condition != accommodation duration.
