# Stay / Participation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does the guest relationship operate during a stay at Singita Lebombo Lodge when accommodation, game drives, walks, wellness, wine, family activities, private vehicles, shared facilities and environmental conditions each have different eligibility, capacity or operating rules?

## Interpretation

The stay is not one service state.

A guest can simultaneously hold several active relationships:

- accommodation guest;
- safari participant;
- dining guest;
- wine-experience participant;
- wellness guest;
- family-programme participant;
- private-vehicle user;
- pool or fitness user;
- cooking-class participant;
- boutique or gallery customer. [S1][S2][S6][S7]

One Stay != One Participation State.

## Accommodation state

The guest's accommodation entitlement is tied to a specific room or villa category and party composition. [S1][S5]

A guest can therefore remain a valid lodge resident while:

- a room is not yet ready;
- an activity is unavailable;
- a child is ineligible for a specific activity;
- a separately charged service has not been purchased;
- a private vehicle is unavailable.

Valid Stay != Universal Service Readiness.

## Game-drive participation

Twice-daily game drives are included in the public lodge rate. [S1][S5]

The 2026 Kruger experience guide states that game-drive vehicles accommodate a maximum of eight people. [S6]

Therefore:

Activity Included != Unlimited Simultaneous Capacity.

Vehicle capacity is a service-level constraint independent of room occupancy.

The evidence does not establish how guests are allocated to individual vehicles or guides.

## Guided-walk participation

Walking safaris are included within the lodge proposition, but the Kruger experience guide recommends a minimum age of 16 years and leaves younger participation to guide discretion. [S3][S6]

Therefore:

Included Activity != Individual Participation Entitlement.

The same guest can be entitled to the overall stay while being ineligible for one activity instance.

## Guide discretion

The 2026 Singita terms state that children participate in safari activities at the discretion of the Singita guide. [S5]

Guide discretion therefore acts as an operational safety gate after the commercial reservation has already been established.

Commercial Entitlement != Safety Approval.

This is a particularly useful Case #41 boundary because the service can be sold as part of the stay without being guaranteed to every person in the party.

## Private-vehicle state

Private vehicles are limited, subject to availability and can be:

- separately paid;
- included for qualifying families;
- included with Lebombo Villa;
- required by family composition. [S1][S5]

The terms also state that a private vehicle must be booked for the entire stay and is not generally guaranteed unless specific payment or party-size conditions are met. [S5]

Therefore:

Vehicle Need != Vehicle Availability.

And:

Vehicle Entitlement Can Be Derived from Party Structure.

This is a separate participation state from accommodation.

## Environmental operating state

High rainfall can require dirt-road closure within the concession under the SANParks operating agreement. [S1]

When routes close, Singita states that alternative game-viewing experiences can be pursued on accessible roads. [S1]

Therefore:

Safari Entitlement != Exact Route Entitlement.

The hospitality relationship can remain active while the specific operating path changes.

## Wellness state

The 2026 Kruger experience guide describes a wellness space shared between Lebombo and Sweni. [S6]

Wellness treatments are excluded from the standard lodge rate. [S1][S5]

Therefore:

Facility Present != Included Treatment Entitlement.

And:

Lebombo Guest != Exclusive Lebombo Facility Inventory.

The current public evidence does not establish booking duration, treatment capacity or cross-lodge priority rules.

## Fitness and yoga state

The fitness space is shared between Lebombo and Sweni, while guided yoga sessions and in-room self-guided materials are available. [S6]

This creates at least two different participation models:

- shared physical capacity;
- distributed in-room self-service.

One Wellness Category != One Capacity Model.

## Wine-experience state

The Kruger experience guide describes personalised tastings and pairings led by sommeliers. [S6]

The lodge site also presents the Wine Studio and wine experiences as part of the broader stay proposition. [S2]

The public record does not establish whether all tastings are included in the room rate, require reservation, or have separate capacity rules.

Discoverable Experience != Proven Included Entitlement.

## Family participation

The Kruger guide lists the Mini Rangers Course, games, crafts, movies and activity booklets, while again stating that children may participate in game drives and bush walks only at the discretion of a Field Guide. [S6]

Therefore:

Family Stay != Identical Activity Set for Every Family Member.

The family product is role-partitioned at the activity level.

## Cooking-class participation

The Singita Community Culinary School at Lebombo offers pre-arranged cooking classes led by a qualified chef. [S7]

The public page states:

- classes should be booked at least 24 hours in advance;
- they are available seven days a week subject to availability;
- a ZAR 3,800 donation per person per class applies;
- bookings can be made through Singita Reservations or on site. [S7]

Therefore:

On-Site Experience != Automatically Included Stay Service.

This is a particularly clear separate commitment object inside the property.

## Shared-property participation

Several facilities and experiences operate across Lebombo and nearby Sweni rather than as isolated single-lodge inventory. [S6]

Therefore:

Guest's Booked Lodge != Every Service's Inventory Boundary.

The evidence does not establish the technical or operational system used to coordinate shared inventory between the two lodges.

## Digital participation

The public record does not establish a guest-facing digital interface that exposes live service states during the stay.

There is no public evidence showing one application where a guest can see:

- next game drive;
- guide;
- vehicle assignment;
- activity eligibility;
- wellness availability;
- dining state;
- recovery owner.

Absence of Public Evidence != Absence of Internal System.

No internal-system claim is made.

## Responsibility continuity

During the stay, responsibility can shift among:

- lodge operations;
- field guides;
- trackers;
- sommeliers;
- chefs;
- wellness staff;
- shared Lebombo/Sweni facilities;
- SANParks-linked environmental operating constraints. [S4][S5][S6][S7]

Guest-Facing Continuity != One Operational Owner.

The evidence does not establish a single recovery authority across every service.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

The stay contains both bundled and separately monetised relationships.

Bundled examples include accommodation, meals, beverages, game drives, walks and laundry. [S1][S5]

Separately monetised or excluded examples include wellness, boutique purchases, transport to the reserve and the Community Culinary School cooking class. [S1][S5][S7]

Public inclusion/exclusion does not establish:

- contribution margin;
- utilisation;
- attachment rate;
- labour cost;
- displacement cost;
- effect on retention.

Included Value != Measured Economic Contribution.

## Failure states

Potential participation failures include:

- game-drive capacity conflict;
- guide refusal for safety reasons;
- unavailable private vehicle;
- route closure during heavy rain;
- unavailable wellness slot;
- shared facility conflict;
- cooking-class unavailability;
- guest expectation that an excluded experience is included.

The public evidence establishes the underlying rules, not observed failure frequency.

## Strongest participation boundaries

1. stay entitlement versus activity eligibility;
2. room capacity versus vehicle capacity;
3. commercial inclusion versus safety discretion;
4. lodge identity versus shared Lebombo/Sweni service inventory;
5. safari entitlement versus route availability;
6. bundled stay versus separately committed on-site experiences.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that luxury safari lodges naturally offer optional activities and operational scheduling.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires repeated evidence that these states change responsibility, entitlement or recovery in a way that cannot be represented safely as one undifferentiated stay.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- daily itinerary allocation;
- vehicle/guide scheduling logic;
- actual occupancy utilisation;
- waitlists;
- service recovery;
- wellness booking process;
- shared-facility prioritisation;
- real-time guest communications;
- internal guest profile continuity.

## Next evidence

Seek evidence on activity scheduling, guide/vehicle assignment, wellness reservations, guest-request routing, service recovery, and whether one guest profile follows the guest across accommodation, safari, dining and ancillary services.
