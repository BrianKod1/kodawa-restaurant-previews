# Arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Where does responsibility shift when a Lebombo guest reaches the reserve, airstrip or lodge, and how do transport completion, check-in state, environmental access and service readiness differ?

## Arrival is not one threshold

For an air-arriving guest, the public journey contains at least three distinct arrival states:

1. aircraft reaches Satara airstrip;
2. Singita staff collect the guest and conduct the road transfer;
3. guest reaches Lebombo Lodge and enters the lodge/check-in relationship. [S1]

Airstrip Arrival != Lodge Arrival.

Lodge Arrival != Immediate Room Entitlement Before Check-in.

## External flight versus Singita transfer

Transport to and from the reserve is excluded from the lodge rate. [S1]

The Satara-to-lodge road transfer is included. [S1]

This produces a clear handoff boundary:

External Journey
→ Satara Arrival
→ Singita Pickup
→ Road Transfer
→ Lodge Arrival.

The public evidence identifies the pickup but does not establish contractual responsibility for upstream flight disruption.

## Satara handoff

Singita states that guests arriving by air are collected from Satara by Singita staff and transported to the lodge, approximately 45 minutes away. [S1]

Air Carrier Completion != Hospitality Arrival Completion.

A guest can have completed the flight while still remaining in an active transfer state operated by the lodge.

## Daylight arrival constraint

Flights can only operate during daylight hours. [S1]

Delayed Flight != Merely Later Arrival.

A sufficiently late disruption may affect whether the air leg can operate at all.

The public evidence does not establish the recovery workflow if a flight misses the operating window.

## Private-charter arrival state

Private aircraft access requires prior permission and is limited to approved operators/aircraft conditions. [S1]

Aircraft Capable of Reaching Kruger != Authorized Satara Arrival.

## Road arrival

Singita also publishes approximate road travel times from Johannesburg, Hazyview, Nelspruit, Hoedspruit and Skukuza. [S1]

Air Arrival != Road Arrival.

The public evidence does not establish whether road-arriving guests experience the same identity, security, gate, luggage or welcome sequence as air-arriving guests.

## Check-in state

The current Lebombo page lists check-in at 14h00. [S1]

Reached Lodge != Room-Ready Entitlement Before 14h00.

The site states that earlier check-in requires booking an additional night. [S1]

The evidence does not establish how the lodge handles a guest who reaches the property earlier because of flight scheduling but has not purchased that additional entitlement.

## Environmental arrival dependency

Singita states that high-rainfall periods can require closure of dirt roads in the private concession under its SANParks agreement. [S1]

SANParks independently identifies Lebombo within Kruger and its concession context. [S4]

Property Operating != Every Internal Road Available.

The public evidence does not establish whether the Satara transfer route itself is affected under specific closure conditions.

## Guest state after arrival

Reaching the lodge does not resolve every service state.

A guest may still have separate states for suite readiness, safari participation, private-vehicle entitlement, dining/special requests, wellness and family activity eligibility. [S1][S3]

Physical Arrival != Universal Service Readiness.

## Responsibility chain

A provisional arrival responsibility chain supported by public evidence is:

External transport provider
→ Satara/park access environment
→ Singita pickup
→ Singita road transfer
→ lodge reception/check-in
→ service-specific teams/guides. [S1][S4]

The chain is operationally plausible but not fully proven contractually.

Case #41 must keep those two claims separate.

## Failure states

Potential arrival failures include external flight delay, missed daylight operating window, unauthorized/private-charter access issue, luggage incompatibility discovered before or during air travel, guest reaches Satara but onward transfer is disrupted, early lodge arrival without room entitlement, weather-related road constraint, and child/family expectation mismatch for immediate activities.

Only the underlying conditions are publicly evidenced.

Actual frequency and recovery performance are not.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

1. external transport completion versus Singita transfer start;
2. Satara arrival versus lodge arrival;
3. lodge arrival versus check-in/room readiness;
4. valid lodging entitlement versus activity-specific eligibility;
5. physical road existence versus weather-permitted road use;
6. guest-facing continuity versus multi-owner operational responsibility.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that the 45-minute airstrip transfer is merely part of normal remote-lodge logistics.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that the handoff materially changes recovery ownership, guest state or service fulfilment.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish live arrival monitoring, transfer manifest systems, pickup failure procedure, late-flight recovery, missed-daylight contingency, luggage failure recovery, early-arrival waiting entitlement, check-in sequence, transfer accessibility or guest identity handoff from transport to reception.

## Next evidence

Seek public evidence on transport disruption and cancellation terms, arrival communication, transfer coordination, early arrival, accessibility, check-in workflow, and service recovery after missed or materially delayed inbound transport.
