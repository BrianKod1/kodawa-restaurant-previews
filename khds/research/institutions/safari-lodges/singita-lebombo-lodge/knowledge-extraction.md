# Knowledge Extraction

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Case purpose

Case #41 tests whether remote wilderness hospitality creates meaningful architecture pressure because one guest's external journey, airstrip arrival, lodge transfer, room state, safari eligibility, party composition, environmental state, dining state, departure state and recovery owner can change while the guest still experiences one stay.

Primary falsification question:

How does remote wilderness hospitality coordinate a guest relationship when access, entitlement, service availability and experience delivery depend on transport windows, environmental conditions, safety rules, party composition and multiple operating actors?

## Core institutional mechanism

The strongest Case #41 mechanism is:

**Remote Hospitality Continuity Across Journey and Service States**

A guest may simultaneously occupy distinct states such as:

- confirmed lodge guest;
- external airline or charter passenger;
- Satara arrival;
- Singita transfer passenger;
- arrived lodge guest;
- room-ready or room-not-ready guest;
- safari participant;
- safari-ineligible party member;
- private-vehicle-entitled guest;
- shared-facility user;

- culinary-school participant;
- wellness guest;
- departing guest;
- financially open account holder;
- insurance/recovery claimant;
- post-stay delivery recipient. [S1][S5][S6][S7][S9]

Those states do not automatically activate the same rights, capacity, responsibilities or recovery paths.

## Falsification requirements

### Remote Hospitality Continuity Across Journey and Service States

Would weaken if external transport, transfer, room, activity and departure states have no meaningful effect on entitlement, responsibility or recovery.

### Journey-State / Service-State Separation

Would weaken if a valid lodging reservation always implies compatible transport, room readiness and service eligibility.

### Multi-Owner Recovery

Would weaken if failures consistently resolve through one clear responsible organization without meaningful handoff.

### Nested Hospitality Dependency

Would weaken if Lebombo's services operate independently of protected-area, shared-facility, transport and community-programme dependencies.

### Party-Composition-Driven Service Entitlement

Would weaken if party composition changes only room occupancy and never affects any other service state.

### Commercial Journey Economics

Would weaken as an architecture concern if service path has no meaningful relationship to cost, ancillary spend, cancellation, recovery or repeat value.

No such financial test has been performed.
