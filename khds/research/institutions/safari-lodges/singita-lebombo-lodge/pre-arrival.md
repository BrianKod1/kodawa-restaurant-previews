# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a Lebombo guest resolve before reaching the lodge, including transport mode, daylight/access restrictions, luggage, health, family eligibility, activity expectations, weather and the airstrip-to-lodge handoff?

## Pre-arrival is dependency preparation

A successful Lebombo stay requires more than a confirmed room.

Before arrival, a guest may need to resolve route to the reserve, flight or road timing, airstrip access, luggage restrictions, passport and health preparation, family accommodation rules, expected safari participation, weather-sensitive packing and arrival-date restrictions. [S1]

Room Confirmed != Journey Ready.

## Air journey preparation

Singita states that scheduled flights and private charters can be arranged for the journey and that flights land at Satara airstrip, around 45 minutes from the lodge. [S1]

Flights can operate only during daylight hours and restrictions apply. [S1]

Flight Exists != Any-Time Arrival Option.

## Satara access preparation

For private aircraft, prior permission is required, only a limited number of companies are permitted to land, and Singita directs guests/operators to reservations for recommended charter companies. [S1]

Private Charter Available != Satara Landing Automatically Authorized.

## Luggage preparation

The current access information imposes scheduled-flight luggage restrictions and requires soft-sided luggage. [S1]

Guest Packed != Transport-Compatible Luggage.

## Health preparation

Singita states that its properties are in malaria-risk areas and recommends consulting a medical practitioner before travel. [S1]

It also publishes passport guidance and advises guests to carry sufficient prescription medication because the lodges are remote. [S1]

Reservation Confirmed != Health/Documentation Preparation Complete.

The current evidence does not establish whether Singita verifies those conditions before travel.

## Family preparation

Family composition should be resolved before arrival because it can determine eligible suite type, private-vehicle requirement and activity participation expectations. [S1][S3]

Party Confirmed != Every Activity State Resolved.

## Activity preparation

Walking safaris are promoted as part of the experience, but the dedicated guided-walk page recommends them for guests aged 16 or older, with younger participation left to guide discretion. [S3]

Activity Included != Every Guest Pre-Cleared.

## Weather preparation

Singita publishes seasonal packing guidance and states that during high rainfall periods dirt roads in the concession may need to close under its SANParks agreement for safety and to avoid damage. [S1]

The lodge states it will seek alternative game-viewing experiences on accessible roads. [S1]

Safari Programme Planned != Exact Route Guaranteed.

## Festive calendar preparation

The current public information states that no arrivals or departures occur on Christmas Day or New Year's Day. [S1]

Stay Dates Available != Boundary Movement Available on Every Date.

## Digital and communication preparation

Singita provides central reservations contact details for direct guests and agents and a separate Kruger reception route for lodge contact. [S1]

This suggests at least two communication layers: central pre-arrival reservation coordination and property/region-level contact.

The evidence does not establish how guest context is handed from reservations to lodge operations.

Central Reservation Knows != Proven Lodge Context Continuity.

## Responsibility map

Public evidence supports the following provisional responsibility partition:

- external airline/charter transport: separate from the lodge rate;
- Satara landing permission/environment: constrained by park/airstrip operating rules;
- Satara pickup and onward road transfer: Singita states its staff collect arriving guests;
- lodge check-in and stay services: Singita;
- activity eligibility/safety: guide discretion within Singita's operation;
- concession road use during heavy rainfall: constrained by SANParks agreement. [S1][S4]

This map is provisional.

It does not establish contractual liability for disruption.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that this is ordinary travel preparation for a remote resort.

That remains plausible.

Case #41 should only treat these dependencies as architectural evidence where they demonstrably change entitlement, handoff or recovery.

## Evidence limitations

Current evidence does not establish the pre-arrival email sequence, transfer manifest workflow, flight-status ingestion, medical or dietary data handling, how late inbound transport is communicated to lodge teams, who proactively owns re-planning after a missed daylight connection, or whether reservations and lodge operations share one guest state.
