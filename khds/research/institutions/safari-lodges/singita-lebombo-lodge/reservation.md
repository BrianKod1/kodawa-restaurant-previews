# Reservation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What exactly becomes committed when a guest reserves Singita Lebombo Lodge, and which parts of the stay remain conditional on guest composition, separate transport, guide discretion, timing, capacity or additional payment?

## Reservation is not one commitment object

The public rate structure exposes several commitment objects: standard suite, two-bedroom suite, private-use villa, lodge hospitality bundle, private safari vehicle in some family configurations, separately arranged transport to the reserve, and separately charged wellness and other excluded services. [S1]

One Reservation != One Entitlement Type.

## Accommodation commitment

The current 2026 rate page distinguishes 13 standard suites, two two-bedroom suites and one private-use villa configuration. [S1]

The rate basis also differs by product: standard suites are priced per adult or child per night, two-bedroom suites are priced per suite, and the villa is priced per villa. [S1]

One Property != One Inventory or Pricing Unit.

The evidence does not establish whether these products share one technical inventory system.

## Included bundle commitment

The published lodge rate includes meals, beverages, twice-daily game drives, walking safaris, Satara airstrip road transfers and laundry. [S1]

This creates a broad booked entitlement.

But the entitlement is not universal fulfilment.

Walking/activity participation can remain subject to safety or guide discretion, dirt-road access can be constrained by heavy rainfall, and external transport to the reserve remains outside the lodge rate. [S1][S3]

Included != Unconditionally Fulfillable.

## Family configuration changes commitment

Family composition can alter the accommodation and safari commitment.

For a family including a child under 10, the two-bedroom suite is required under the current public rate rules; parties of four or more receive complimentary private-vehicle use, while parties of up to three are required to pay for private-vehicle use. [S1]

Guest Composition Can Change Both Inventory and Service Entitlement.

## Guide discretion remains outside reservation certainty

Game drives and walks are advertised within the stay, but children's participation is subject to guide discretion. [S1][S3]

Activity Included in Rate != Individual Participation Guaranteed.

## Transport commitment boundary

Singita states that guests can reach Lebombo by scheduled air service or private charter, and that Singita can arrange scheduled flights or private charters. [S1]

However, transport to and from the reserve is listed as excluded from the lodge rate, while the road transfer from Satara airstrip to the lodge is included. [S1]

Lodge Reservation != External Air Transport Entitlement.

External Flight Commitment != Lodge Transfer Commitment.

The current evidence does not establish whether Singita acts as principal, agent or coordinator for every externally arranged flight product.

## Daylight and access restrictions

Flights to the lodge environment can operate only during daylight hours and restrictions apply. [S1]

Private charter access to Satara requires prior permission and only a limited set of companies are permitted to land. [S1]

Transport Booked != Every Arrival Time Operationally Permitted.

## Festive-season restriction

The current information page states that there are no arrivals or departures on Christmas Day or New Year's Day at Singita lodges. [S1]

Lodge Open != Arrival/Departure Permitted on Every Calendar Date.

## Check-in commitment

The current Lebombo information page states check-in at 14h00 and check-out at 11h00, and says guests wishing to check in earlier or check out later are required to book an additional night. [S1]

Arrival at Property != Immediate Room Entitlement Before Check-in.

The evidence does not establish how unexpected transport delay interacts with unused accommodation time.

## Single-room pricing condition

The current rate page applies a single supplement when three or more single rooms are booked in one reservation, beginning from the third single room. [S1]

Individual Room Price != Independent of Reservation Composition.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Reservation economics combine seasonal room/villa pricing, party composition, child pricing, private-vehicle conditions, excluded levies and services, and external transport cost. [S1]

The public record does not establish contribution by component, refund leakage, cancellation revenue, upsell conversion or channel acquisition cost.

Published Price != Proven Unit Economics.

## Failure states

Potential reservation failure states supported or implied by the public evidence include wrong accommodation selected for family composition, expected activity participation later restricted by guide discretion, external flight incompatible with daylight/access constraints, arrival date colliding with festive no-arrival rule, misunderstanding Satara transfer as including the external flight, and early arrival without corresponding room entitlement.

These are bounded hypotheses, not observed failures.

## Strongest reservation boundaries

1. accommodation commitment versus bundled service commitment;
2. booking inclusion versus individual activity eligibility;
3. family composition versus accommodation/private-vehicle entitlement;
4. external transport versus internal transfer;
5. paid transport versus operational access permission;
6. lodge arrival versus room-ready/check-in entitlement.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that the reservation merely bundles standard luxury-safari inclusions and exceptions.

That remains plausible.

The architectural interpretation gains force only if the separate commitments create materially different modification, recovery or responsibility paths.

## Evidence limitations

No direct booking was completed.

The current evidence does not establish deposit rules, cancellation/refund rules, no-show treatment, modification workflow, payment timing, exact inventory coupling, airline/charter cancellation handoff or how private-vehicle availability is technically allocated.

## Next evidence

Inspect current terms and conditions, reservation confirmation structure and transport-arrangement conditions, then test whether a change to one commitment propagates automatically to the others.
