# Discovery

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How clearly can a prospective Singita Lebombo Lodge guest determine which accommodation, safari, transfer, dining, family and wellness experiences are included, separately charged, capacity-constrained or eligibility-dependent before committing to the stay?

## Discovery is bundle discovery

Singita Lebombo Lodge is presented as more than accommodation.

The public lodge material exposes a combined hospitality proposition containing accommodation, meals and beverages, twice-daily game drives, walking safaris, Satara airstrip road transfers, laundry, pool, fitness and wellness facilities, family activities, wine, boutique, stargazing and other experiences. [S1][S2]

Lodge Discovery != Room Discovery.

The prospective guest is discovering a bundle whose components do not all share the same entitlement, eligibility or operating conditions.

## Accommodation categories

The current information page distinguishes 13 standard Lebombo Suites, 2 two-bedroom suites and a private-use Lebombo Villa formed from the two two-bedroom suites. [S1]

These products do not expose one interchangeable occupancy model.

The two-bedroom suites and villa matter especially for family eligibility because children under 10 are not presented as universally eligible for every standard suite configuration. [S1]

Room Available != Every Guest Configuration Eligible.

## Included versus excluded value

The public rate material states that the standard lodge rate includes meals and beverages, twice-daily open-vehicle safaris, walking safaris, return road transfers between Satara airstrip and the lodge, and laundry. [S1]

It separately lists exclusions including tourism and carbon-offset levies, boutique purchases, wellness treatments, transport to and from the reserve, and certain special meal requests. [S1]

Displayed Hospitality Offering != One Universal Included Entitlement.

## Safari activity discovery

Game drives and guided walks are part of the lodge proposition. [S1][S2][S3]

But activity discovery is not identical to activity eligibility.

The public material states that children's participation in game activities remains at guide discretion, and the guided-walk page recommends walking safaris for guests aged 16 or older while allowing younger participation at guide discretion. [S1][S3]

Activity Listed != Activity Guaranteed to Every Guest.

## Family discovery

Family composition can change the product that must be booked.

For families including a child under 10, the current rate information routes the booking toward the two-bedroom suite and can change whether a private vehicle is included or separately payable depending on party size. [S1]

Family Size != Mere Occupancy Attribute.

It can alter accommodation requirement, safari vehicle entitlement, price and activity participation conditions.

## Transport discovery

The lodge can be reached by scheduled air service, private charter or road transfer from several regional origins. [S1]

For air arrivals, Singita states that flights land at Satara airstrip and guests are collected by Singita staff for the onward road journey to the lodge. [S1]

The air journey to the reserve is excluded from the lodge rate even though the Satara-to-lodge road transfer is included. [S1]

Transport to Reserve != Transfer Inside Lodge Hospitality Entitlement.

## Protected-area context

SANParks identifies Singita Lebombo as a luxury lodge within Kruger National Park and describes it as operating in a private concession. [S4]

Lodge Brand != Sole Environmental or Access Authority.

The current public evidence does not establish the complete division of authority among Singita, SANParks, airlines, charter operators and other transport providers.

## Digital discovery

The public Singita site provides lodge overview, accommodation and amenities, current rates, check-in/check-out information, child policies, packing and health guidance, and access directions. [S1][S2]

The current evidence does not establish whether the same conditions are presented identically through travel agents, whether every booking path exposes exclusions before commitment, how mobile and desktop discovery compare, or whether guests understand that an included airstrip transfer does not include the external flight.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

The commercial model appears to combine a high-value inclusive core with separately priced or separately controlled extensions. [S1]

Potential commercial objects include suite or villa inventory, private safari vehicle entitlement, wellness, boutique purchases, transport to the reserve and special requests.

Public availability of an additional service does not establish its contribution margin or attach rate.

High Published Rate != Proven Commercial Contribution by Component.

## Strongest discovery boundaries

1. lodge discovery versus room discovery;
2. included lodge bundle versus separately charged services;
3. accommodation eligibility versus family composition;
4. activity availability versus guide/safety eligibility;
5. transport to the reserve versus included Satara-to-lodge transfer;
6. Singita hospitality versus protected-area and third-party operating dependencies.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that these are ordinary luxury-safari packaging rules rather than evidence of a deeper hospitality architecture.

That explanation remains plausible.

Case #41 should not promote a new pattern merely because the property bundles multiple services.

The stronger architectural interpretation would require evidence that these separate entitlement, access and responsibility states materially change guest or operator decisions.

## Evidence limitations

Current discovery evidence is public-facing and institution-controlled.

It does not establish actual guest comprehension, conversion behaviour, booking abandonment, agent-versus-direct differences, real-time availability coupling, denied-activity recovery or commercial performance by service.

No booking transaction has been completed.

## Next evidence

Inspect direct reservation commitment and modification conditions, suite versus villa booking rules, private-vehicle commitment, early/late arrival conditions, transfer responsibility, cancellation and disruption terms, and whether external transport and lodge services are modified together or separately.
