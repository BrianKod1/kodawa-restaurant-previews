# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What ends when a guest departs Singita Lebombo Lodge, and which financial, transport, data, delivery or recovery relationships can remain active after physical checkout?

## Departure is not one endpoint

The current terms distinguish:

- check-out;
- road or air departure;
- payment/account settlement;
- cancellation responsibility;
- travel-insurance responsibility;
- post-stay fulfilment such as wine delivery. [S5][S6]

Therefore:

Physical Checkout != Universal Relationship Closure.

## Check-out state

The 2026 terms state that standard check-out is at 11h00. [S5]

For Singita Villa Collection properties, including Lebombo Villa, later departure must be pre-arranged, is subject to availability and can carry an additional charge. [S5]

Therefore:

Guest Still on Property != Room Entitlement Automatically Extended.

The departure boundary can have a separate commercial state.

## Road departure

Guests arriving or departing Singita Kruger National Park by car are requested to carry their confirmation voucher and provide a mobile number on which the lodge can contact them while in transit. [S5]

This is a strong continuity signal.

Checkout Complete != Lodge Contact Relationship Immediately Closed.

The lodge may still need to reach the guest during the departure journey.

## Air departure

Air departure from the Lebombo environment depends on access to Satara and the relevant flight operating conditions. [S1]

External air transport remains outside the standard lodge rate. [S1][S5]

Therefore:

Lodge Departure != Airline Departure.

The guest can complete the lodge stay while still remaining in an external journey relationship.

## Departure timing constraints

Flights are subject to daylight and airstrip operating restrictions. [S1]

The lodge also operates within a protected-area/concession environment. [S4]

Therefore:

Desired Departure Time != Universally Available Departure Time.

The evidence does not establish the lodge's recovery workflow when an external departure is materially delayed.

## Financial relationship after departure

The 2026 terms state that, for account-holder payment arrangements, the balance due to Singita is billed on the monthly statement on guest departure and is due within 30 days. [S5]

Therefore:

Guest Physically Departed != Financial Relationship Closed.

This is direct evidence that at least one commercial relationship can persist beyond departure.

The applicability differs by counterparty/payment arrangement, so Case #41 should not generalise this to every direct guest.

## Cancellation and flight-disruption boundary

The 2026 terms state that general cancellation fees apply when a confirmed booking is cancelled for reasons including force majeure, subject to specified exceptions. [S5]

The same terms explicitly state that a flight cancellation not related to the listed exceptional government/property restrictions remains subject to the general cancellation terms and recommends comprehensive travel insurance. [S5]

Therefore:

External Flight Failure != Automatic Lodge Refund Entitlement.

This is one of the strongest Multi-Owner Recovery pressures in Case #41.

A disruption can prevent arrival while the lodge's commercial policy remains separate from the airline's operational failure.

## Exceptional refund boundary

The terms provide specified exceptional circumstances where 100% of Singita accommodation value will be refunded, including regional property closure or certain government border/visa/quarantine restrictions. [S5]

Therefore:

Disruption != One Cancellation Outcome.

Recovery depends on disruption category.

This is policy-level evidence of state-dependent commercial recovery.

## Travel-insurance relationship

Direct guests are required by the current terms to obtain comprehensive travel insurance within 21 days of confirming their booking, covering cancellation or curtailment and events beyond Singita's control. [S5]

Therefore:

Hospitality Reservation != Hospitality Provider Assuming Every Journey Risk.

Part of the recovery architecture is explicitly externalised to insurance.

The evidence does not establish how claims are operationally coordinated.

## Post-stay product fulfilment

The Kruger experience guide states that selected wines can be arranged for delivery to a guest's home. [S6]

Therefore:

Guest Leaves Property != Every Fulfilment Relationship Complete.

A commerce/logistics relationship can persist after the stay.

## Data relationship after departure

Singita's current privacy policy states that personal information can be collected through booking and guest interactions and provides processes for access, correction or deletion. [S9]

This establishes that the data relationship can persist beyond physical departure.

However, Case #41 does not infer internal retention periods or CRM architecture beyond what the policy states.

## Responsibility chain

A provisional departure chain can include:

Lodge checkout
→ Singita road transfer / road-transit contact
→ Satara or road exit
→ external carrier or road provider
→ financial settlement
→ insurance/recovery if journey failure occurs
→ possible post-stay fulfilment. [S1][S5][S6]

One Guest Journey != One Departure Owner.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Departure can affect:

- additional late-departure charges;
- cancellation fees;
- outstanding balance settlement;
- post-stay wine fulfilment;
- potential insurance recovery. [S5][S6]

The public evidence does not establish:

- settlement timing for every direct guest;
- chargeback rates;
- cancellation-margin effects;
- recovery cost;
- lifetime value after service failure.

Departure Date != Economic Endpoint.

## Failure states

Potential departure failures include:

- late checkout without entitlement;
- road/air timing incompatibility;
- external flight cancellation;
- guest unable to depart because of environmental or transport disruption;
- unclear recovery ownership across Singita, carrier and insurer;
- post-stay fulfilment failure.

The cancellation policy establishes commercial outcomes for some disruption classes but not the operational recovery sequence.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. checkout versus physical exit;
2. lodge exit versus external transport completion;
3. physical departure versus financial settlement;
4. external flight failure versus lodge refund entitlement;
5. guest departure versus post-stay fulfilment;
6. guest journey continuity versus multiple recovery owners.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that these are normal hotel cancellation and payment terms.

That remains plausible.

Case #41 becomes architecturally important only because remote transport dependencies make the separation between provider responsibility, journey failure and commercial entitlement especially visible.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- exact checkout workflow;
- transfer manifest closure;
- baggage handoff;
- missed-flight handling;
- rebooking authority;
- insurance claim support;
- on-property waiting rights after checkout;
- post-stay recovery ownership;
- direct-guest settlement process in every booking channel.

## Next evidence

Seek transport disruption procedures, late-departure handling, guest waiting arrangements, payment settlement by channel, post-stay complaint recovery and evidence of how Singita coordinates with carriers or insurers when the guest journey fails after checkout.
