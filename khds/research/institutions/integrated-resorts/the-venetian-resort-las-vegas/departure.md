# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How do hotel, loyalty and venue relationships end, and which relationships persist after accommodation checkout?

## Evidence

Hotel accommodation has a defined checkout relationship governed by published timing and rate conditions. Early or late departure can carry different conditions, and late checkout can depend on availability or applicable benefits. [S2][S3]

Diamond-tier Venetian Rewards material includes late-checkout benefits, showing that loyalty status can alter the hotel-departure relationship. [S7]

Hotel checkout does not terminate Venetian Rewards membership. The loyalty relationship continues beyond a single stay and spans multiple resort domains. [S6][S7]

Spa, dining and venue relationships can also end independently of hotel checkout because they are separately booked, purchased or admitted experiences. [S8][S9][S10]

A non-hotel spa customer may complete a Canyon Ranch visit without ever entering a hotel-departure process. [S8]

Likewise, a dining or venue customer may complete their relationship independently of lodging. [S9][S10]

## Interpretation

Departure from the resort is not one universal endpoint.

At least four different relationship endings are visible:

- hotel checkout;
- completion of a spa visit;
- completion of a dining relationship;
- completion of a venue relationship.

Meanwhile, loyalty membership can persist after all of them.

This supports several bounded distinctions:

Checkout != end of loyalty relationship.

Hotel departure != end of every resort relationship.

Venue exit != hotel checkout.

Spa completion != accommodation departure.

Temporary visit completion != termination of persistent customer identity.

A customer can therefore leave one operational relationship while retaining another.

## Competing explanation

This pattern is also normal in large resorts.

Loyalty programs persist across stays.

Restaurants, spas and entertainment venues naturally have their own service endpoints.

Hotel checkout is conventionally separate from those products.

The architecture claim therefore depends on whether persistent and temporary relationships materially change how identity, settlement, recovery and future recognition are coordinated.

## Limits

The evidence does not establish how unresolved restaurant, spa, gaming or venue charges are reconciled with hotel checkout.

It does not establish whether hotel checkout changes access immediately for guest-only amenities.

It does not establish whether digital credentials expire automatically at checkout.

It does not establish how service-recovery cases spanning multiple domains are closed.

No folio settlement was inspected.

No checkout transaction was performed.

No post-stay loyalty activity was observed.

## Next evidence

Inspect:

- checkout and late-checkout rules;
- folio settlement;
- room-charge cutoff;
- hotel-key or digital-key expiration;
- post-stay loyalty continuity;
- independently booked spa, dining and venue departures;
- cross-domain recovery ownership.

Determine which relationships terminate at departure and which remain active.
