# Case #34 - The Venetian Resort Las Vegas

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Institution

The Venetian Resort Las Vegas is being studied as an integrated resort combining hotel accommodation, gaming, dining, entertainment, spa, loyalty relationships and guest- or product-restricted amenities.

Sector: integrated-resorts

Research access date: 2026-10-07.

This dossier is based on inspected public sources. It is not an on-site audit, transaction test, gaming-system audit, loyalty-account audit, accessibility assessment, service-recovery test or proof of representative guest outcomes.

## Research role

Case #34 tests whether the provisional Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recurs in a large integrated resort where many hospitality relationships coexist inside one institutional identity.

The primary falsification question is:

Does Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recur in a large integrated resort where hotel guests, loyalty members, premium-suite guests, casino users, diners, entertainment visitors and spa users overlap, or are those differences better explained as ordinary amenity segmentation within one conventional resort?

Case #34 deliberately introduces a more complex institutional environment than Cases #32 and #33:

- thousands of hotel suites across The Venetian and The Palazzo;
- gaming and loyalty relationships;
- ordinary and upgraded accommodation relationships;
- hotel-guest-restricted amenities;
- separately purchasable spa access;
- restaurant reservations, walk-ins and group dining;
- venue-specific eligibility;
- loyalty tiers whose benefits extend across multiple resort domains.

## Initial boundaries under test

The evidence creates several distinctions worth testing without treating them as validated patterns:

Resort presence != hotel-guest entitlement.

Hotel-guest status != entitlement to every resort venue.

Ordinary hotel stay != Prestige relationship.

Hotel accommodation != loyalty-tier status.

Loyalty membership != identical tier entitlement.

Hotel-guest eligibility != guaranteed venue admission.

Spa access != hotel accommodation requirement.

Dining relationship != lodging relationship.

Shared resort identity != identical customer relationship.

Physical co-location != uniform service access.

Cross-resort loyalty != proof of unified operational responsibility.

## Governance position

Case #34 begins as a falsification case.

No pattern promotion, confidence increase, demotion, rename, merge, validation or Principle promotion is assumed from the initial evidence.

The strongest competing explanation remains that The Venetian is one conventional integrated resort using ordinary accommodation tiers, loyalty tiers, venue restrictions and separately purchasable amenities.
