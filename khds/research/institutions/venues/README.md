Purpose

Venue research tests hospitality institutions in which one destination may contain multiple operators, activities, access routes, capacity objects and responsibility boundaries.

This category may include:

food halls and shared markets;

members' clubs;

hospitality-led cultural venues;

event-led social spaces;

mixed food, retail and programme destinations;

and later venue formats selected for research value.

The category tests:

multi-intent discovery and arrival;

walk-in access versus booked or ticketed participation;

allocation across seats, sellers, events and private arrangements;

belonging, optional participation and access-versus-exclusivity boundaries;

operator, organiser and venue responsibility;

crowd and route-specific capacity;

atmosphere across changing vendors, programmes and time states;

accessible orientation and human assistance;

failure recovery across shared and operator-specific systems;

and departure when purchases, tickets, stored value or claims remain open.
