3. Core question and bounded answer

Does a more editorially unified shared market remove the responsibility problem identified at Reffen, or does it make the guest-facing identity more unified while the actual participation objects remain separate?

Bounded answer from published evidence: Time Out's editorial frame gathers food, bars, shops and cultural offers; its FAQ describes unreserved common tables; Academy sells edition-specific workshops; Studio is separately located on piso 1; public programmes can occur in the hall; card terms describe a prepaid purchase instrument; private arrangements start as enquiries. These routes plainly have different published prerequisites and consequences. They do not establish the legal structure or successful recovery of a specific counter, course or event. Concept, FAQ, Academy store, Studio, cards, Events & Groups.

The useful research hypothesis is: A curator can unify discovery and meaning while allocation, seller/product obligations and recoveries may remain route-specific; the guest should be able to locate the responsible receiving role before a consequential commitment or failure. This is not evidence that Time Out presently provides or lacks a successful central escalation function.

12. What would falsify the extracted hypotheses?

Unified service bridge: A tested, staffed venue route demonstrably handles seller complaints, seating problems and event changes to resolution, contradicting a claim that guests always face distinct unrecoverable endpoints.

Individually successful routes: In-person observation shows the brand's curator/merchant relationships are easily understood and appropriately recoverable without added explanation, weakening a proposed need for extra interface layers.

Counter variation absent: Current contractual and service documents establish common seller-level terms across the hall; stop describing every vendor's responsibility as necessarily different.

Shared seating robust or constrained: Measure seat suitability and turn-away under contrasting times; revise any implication that access either always succeeds or always fails.

Physical access more precise than floor labels: Qualified route assessment may confirm usable Academy/Studio passage or identify a particular constraint; do not infer either from piso number.

Programme actually consistent in operation: Event organiser may supply a definitive Salsa occurrence and cross-language correction; keep published conflict separate from observed event outcome.

Group card identity established: Actual card documentation may equate group and general market cards or define different terms; retire the “unknown identity” line only with product-specific proof.

Claims of editorial/guest impact tested: Independent decision/visitor and district research may support or refute mechanisms that publication copy alone cannot establish.
