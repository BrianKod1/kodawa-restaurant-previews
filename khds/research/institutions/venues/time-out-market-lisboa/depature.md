Purpose and boundary

Departure is the transition from active dining, event, course or group participation to a usable physical, financial and relational next state. Walking out of Mercado da Ribeira does not necessarily close a wrong order, unused card balance, ticket dispute, unanswered accessibility issue or a group organiser's obligations. Time Out's public architecture presents ordinary communal dining, general TOM prepaid cards, Academy tickets, Studio programmes and private-event enquiries as distinct routes; no universal venue checkout is evidenced. Market FAQ, general TOM card terms, Academy store, Events & Groups.

What must be resolved, handed over or explicitly retained when a guest stops using one part of this shared institution and prepares to leave it?

Departure begins when a person decides to finish the relevant activity or starts arranging exit. It can pause if they enter another counter, programme or shop. It ends analytically when the person can identify a usable route out and understands whether any money, claim, entitlement, belongings, agreement or future contact remains open. This is a research definition, not an observed Time Out procedure.

The general TOM card: departure without monetary closure

The published Portuguese general TOM card terms say it is bought and loaded at the Time Out Shop on piso 0, spendable in participating spaces at the market of purchase, valid for one year from first load, and that unused balance is not refunded under those terms. The terms separately address lost/stolen cards and restrictions. This creates an explicit, published post-visit value state; it does not prove a holder's actual balance, current participating outlet list or recovery experience. This section records what the issuer says, not a legal assessment of the terms or a personal finance recommendation. Time Out Market Cards terms.

Course, event and group consequence windows

The Time Out Academy example Middle East masterclass says its issued tickets are not exchanged, returned or refunded; a cancellation email at least 10 days before the workshop is described as leading to a gift voucher for the amount paid, valid for three months. It also specifies an included meal and a late-entry prohibition once the session starts. Those are example product/edition terms, not a generic right for all Academy courses, a verified voucher issuance or a rule about post-session cancellation. A guest must retain the actual selected edition, ticket and relevant responsible contact for a live problem. Example Academy masterclass, workshop store.

The Studio's individually listed Kanan Gill show publishes a price and date, but not a tested checkout, organiser contract, refund or how the room releases attendees. A priced event page alone cannot settle a dispute if a particular ticket or show changes. Conversely, the Portuguese Salsa listing presents free admission for its own food-hall event; it supplies no general cancellation remedy, seat guarantee or after-event transport instruction. Studio show listing, Salsa listing.

Time and onward movement: venue clock is not a transport clock

Time Out's Portuguese directions page identifies Mercado da Ribeira near Cais do Sodré and lists metro, rail, bus, ferry, tram and other arrival methods. The Portuguese FAQ places Academia Time Out on piso zero beside A Vida Portuguesa and Time Out Bar. These published landmarks locate the Academy at ground-floor level, but do not establish its exact door, internal route, reception or issue handoff, teaching room, step-free path or usable exit. The market-hours presentation does not verify the last return service, night-time accessible route, taxi availability, transport disruption or first-floor-to-exit movement after a specific event. Guest departure planning must use current relevant transport-operator information for the chosen date and need; no route was tested here. Portuguese directions, Studio floor information, Portuguese FAQ.

Property, disposal and the unresolved-help route

Not finding such a process in the inspected pages does not prove staff lacks one. Market overview and contact, Events & Groups contact, Academy product contact.
