1. Purpose and event boundary

This file studies events as dated or otherwise time-bounded hospitality states operating in and alongside the continuing market. Time Out describes curated programmes both in the ground-floor food hall and in the first-floor Studio; it also advertises separately arranged Academy, group and buyout uses. These are not one admission, seating or staffing system. Portuguese event overview, Studio location, Events & Groups.

When one market continues to offer meals while hosting changing programmes, how should each visitor know whether an event is happening, where it takes place, what participation requires and who resolves a failed event route?

An event, for this analysis, temporarily changes what people can do, enter or expect in a defined part of the institution. A busy food hall, background sound or ordinary restaurant transaction is not automatically a separately governed event. Event discovery and readiness begin before arrival, but this file focuses on occurrence-specific admission, coexistence with other guest routes, role changes, capacity and recovery after a person chooses to participate. No visit, admission, organiser interview, booking or event outcome was observed.

4. Same building, materially different programme thresholds

The Studio's individual Kanan Gill: Not This Again page advertises 26 September 2026, 21:00, €30 and a Studio location; these are published listing fields as inspected, not a verified current checkout or ticket ownership. In contrast, the Salsa food-hall listing describes free access. The distinction disproves any inference that all market events are either free or covered by general hall entry. Kanan Gill listing, Salsa listing.

Time Out's Sing Out Bingo listing advertises a free-entry Friday programme on 9 October 2026, describing a musical bingo format linked rhetorically with dinner. The published listing does not state that dining is included with entry, that buying food is compulsory or who allocates any seating or game card; those are occurrence-specific questions. A diner, player and spectator may need different participation explanations despite sharing the same floor. Sing Out Bingo listing.

5. Food-hall coexistence: a capacity and optionality test

A social event inside the food hall can affect sound, standing space, seating, counter circulation, wait times and choice to take part. These are plausible operating effects, not observed facts about Salsa or Bingo. Time Out's event pages place both in the market and its older FAQ describes communal dining; none of the pages inspected shows a programme-specific floor plan, diverted circulation, seat allocation, vendor trading schedule or sensory/accommodation procedure. Salsa, Sing Out Bingo, FAQ.

The research test must separate at least:

programme admission — free, paid, preregistered or privately controlled for the relevant occurrence;

activity capacity — the number of people who can actually join the dancing/game/show safely;

ordinary dining capacity — seats, counters and service available to a non-participant;

internal access — usable entrance, floor transition, toilets and circulation;

food inclusion — whether any purchase, meal or group card is included or expected;

role change — when a diner becomes a participant and whether this needs a new entitlement.

Do not add these independent questions into a single “market capacity” number. One person can dine without joining a performance, join a relevant free programme without a pre-purchased Studio ticket, or hold a Studio entitlement while needing separately purchased food; these are architecture possibilities to test, not observed universal market rights.

6. Private arrangements and groups: enquiry is not entitlement

Time Out's Events & Groups page offers the Studio, Academy, event cards and potential entire-market buyout via enquiry. Its Studio floor description and 400-square-metre area show a space capable of being proposed for event use; they do not disclose an accepted agreement's price, cancellation rule, food inclusion, accessible setup, headcount, exclusive area or receiving staff. A group organiser's confirmation must be checked rather than inferred from the public marketing page. Events & Groups, Studio.

Group event cards are described as pre-loaded spending instruments for the food hall. Time Out also publishes terms for a general TOM card; the pages inspected do not establish whether the marketed group product is the same instrument or has the same loss, expiry, refund and outlet rules. Card possession is not a show ticket, Studio access or guaranteed meal. Events & Groups, general TOM card terms.

8. Transfer and falsification against Reffen
