# Arrival

## Research question

Is arrival one hospitality event, or a set of role-specific entry procedures within the same institution?

## Evidence

The coworking house rules state that hotel reception is available 24/7 while the coworking welcome desk operates on a more limited schedule. [S4]

Coworking access is membership-dependent. Flexible members have restricted access hours, while other coworking members receive broader access. Key cards provide access based on the member's specific membership. [S4]

Public lobby and restaurant/bar access is distinct from controlled coworking access. Member guests must remain accompanied in community-only spaces, and guests using flexible workspace generally require a day pass unless covered by a booked meeting-room context. [S4]

## Interpretation

The same physical address contains several arrival states.

```text
Entering the Building
!=
Entering Every Service Environment
```

and:

```text
Guest of Member
!=
Member

Public Visitor
!=
Coworking User
```

Arrival is therefore partly an access-control problem, not merely a welcome/check-in problem.

## Limits

The sources do not document the full student arrival process, hotel identity verification, accessibility handoff, failure recovery for credentials, or staff decision-making when roles overlap.

## Synthesis-gate relevance

Case #30 shows role partitioning at the threshold of use. Co-location does not collapse identity or entitlement boundaries.
