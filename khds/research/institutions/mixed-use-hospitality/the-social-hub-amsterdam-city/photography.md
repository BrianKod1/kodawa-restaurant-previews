# Photography

## Research question

What can official imagery verify, and where must KHDS refuse to infer operating quality from presentation?

## Evidence

The official media kit contains a structured set of Amsterdam City images, including exterior, restaurant/bar and multiple room concepts. [S10]

The property page also presents official imagery across the institution's accommodation and shared-space offer. [S1]

## Interpretation

Official photography is useful for confirming visible design characteristics, spatial categories and brand presentation.

It is not sufficient evidence for:

- cleanliness over time;
- maintenance quality;
- crowding;
- accessibility in use;
- service quality;
- atmosphere under peak load;
- safety;
- representative guest experience.

```text
Curated Image
!=
Representative Operation
```

and:

```text
Visible Design Quality
!=
Verified Service Quality
```

## Limits

This dossier does not contain independently sampled photography across different occupancy states or times of day.

## Synthesis-gate relevance

Case #30 retains the KHDS boundary between visual proof of space and evidence of operating performance.
