## Case role

Case #30 is the planned synthesis-gate case after Cases #21–29.

It tests whether recurring entitlement and lifecycle boundaries observed most clearly in Cases #27–29 remain visible in a hybrid institution that combines public access, hotel accommodation, student accommodation, coworking membership, events, dining and community programming.

## Core mechanism observed

### Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture

Research direction only.

The same institution can maintain several simultaneous user relationships while determining access and obligations according to role, product, time, capacity, contract and behavioral rules.

Case #30 therefore pressures the narrower provisional label:

**Membership-Gated / Recurring-Entitlement Hospitality Architecture**

The evidence suggests the underlying mechanism may be broader than membership gating.
