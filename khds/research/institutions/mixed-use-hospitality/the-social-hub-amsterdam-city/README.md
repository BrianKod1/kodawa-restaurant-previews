## Institution

The Social Hub Amsterdam City is a mixed-use hospitality institution combining hotel accommodation, student accommodation, extended-stay positioning, coworking, meetings and events, food and beverage, public/shared spaces, community programming and membership-linked benefits. [S1][S3][S5][S9]

The case is deliberately selected as Case #30 because it tests whether boundaries previously observed in private members' clubs survive in a more open hybrid institution.

## Research role

Case #30 is a synthesis-gate falsification case.

The strongest question is whether the recurring mechanism is truly membership-gated hospitality, or whether the broader mechanism is role-partitioned hospitality in which multiple relationships coexist under one brand and one physical property while retaining distinct access, capacity, pricing and lifecycle rules.

## Evidence boundary

This dossier establishes public operator claims, published rules, public booking pathways, published privacy practices and official visual material.

It does not establish representative guest experience, student experience, member satisfaction, belonging, service-recovery effectiveness, accessibility fulfilment, dietary-safety outcomes, internal inventory allocation, data-system integration quality, security effectiveness, profitability or causal value creation.

External practitioner signals and industry references gathered outside this dossier may shape synthesis questions, but they are not institutional evidence for Case #30.
