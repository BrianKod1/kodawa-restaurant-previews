# Departure

## Research question

What actually ends when a person physically leaves The Social Hub Amsterdam City?

## Evidence

Hotel stays have a physical stay endpoint. [S2]

Coworking membership can persist beyond any individual visit. Access cards are to be returned at the end of membership, and lost cards should be blocked to prevent misuse. Private-office contracts carry explicit end-of-contract obligations. [S4]

The privacy policy states that after a Social Hub experience, personal data may continue to be stored in a profile for future service personalization. It also states that semester guests may be enrolled into an alumni relationship, subject to stated communication rights. [S7]

## Interpretation

Case #30 provides strong public-evidence support for:

```text
Physical Departure
!=
Relationship Closure
```

and more specifically:

```text
Checkout
!=
Membership End

Visit End
!=
Data Relationship End
```

The institution has several lifecycle clocks: stay, membership, office contract, data profile, alumni/community relationship and future communications.

## Limits

The public evidence does not establish actual revocation timing across systems, retention periods for every operational record, deletion execution, or whether post-departure personalization produces better outcomes.

## Synthesis-gate relevance

This independently reinforces persistent-relationship boundaries previously observed in Cases #27–29, now outside a private-club context.
