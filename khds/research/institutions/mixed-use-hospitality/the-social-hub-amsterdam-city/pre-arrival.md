# Pre-arrival

## Research question

Which relationships, permissions and data obligations begin before a person physically reaches the property?

## Evidence

Hotel and student users enter digital booking processes before arrival. [S2][S8]

Coworking terms and house rules define access windows, guest rules, benefits, discounts, cross-property limits and key-card expectations before or during membership use. [S3][S4]

The privacy policy states that the data collected depends on the service used and may include identity information, contact details, payment information, dietary preferences, university-registration details and booking preferences. It also describes processing for bookings, communication and service delivery and identifies operational and technology service providers. [S7]

## Interpretation

The case supports:

```text
Physical Arrival
!=
Relationship Start
```

A user can already have contractual, data, eligibility and communication relationships with the institution before entering the building.

The multi-product model also implies that pre-arrival information requirements differ by role.

```text
Shared Property
!=
Shared Pre-arrival Requirements
```

## Limits

The public record does not verify how identity is synchronized across systems, how special requests reach operations, whether accessibility requirements are consistently fulfilled, or whether API/integration governance is effective.

Published privacy policy:

```text
!=
Verified Privacy Outcome
```

## Synthesis-gate relevance

Case #30 extends persistent-relationship logic beyond private membership. Digital and contractual state can precede physical participation across hotel, student and coworking relationships.
