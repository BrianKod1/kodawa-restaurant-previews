# Reservation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What exactly becomes committed when a visitor purchases a Tomorrowland Belgium Pass or Package, and which rights remain conditional on personalization, identity, Bracelet activation, package composition, transport provider, timing or later operational rules?

## Purchase is not admission

The official sales-channel guidance explicitly states that a confirmation email is not a valid Pass or Package for festival access. [S2]

The visitor must still move through purchase, personalization, Bracelet fulfilment/activation and identity-linked access. [S2][S4][S5]

Therefore:

Paid Order != Admission Entitlement.

## Immediate payment creates the order

The order guide states that payment must be completed immediately; otherwise the order is cancelled and the Passes or Packages return to sale. [S3]

Therefore:

Selected Inventory != Reserved Inventory Until Payment.

## Main Buyer and individual visitors are different roles

The order is initially managed through a Main Buyer and Tomorrowland Account. [S3][S4]

After purchase, each Pass or Package must be assigned to an individual visitor through personalization. [S4]

Therefore:

Purchaser != Every Entitled Visitor.

One commercial order can contain multiple individual access relationships.

## Personalization converts order inventory into named entitlement

For standard Passes and Packages, the Main Buyer can claim a Pass or send it to another person for personalization. [S4]

After personalization, the entitlement becomes identity-specific.

Order Line Item
→ Named Visitor Entitlement.

## Failure to personalize can dissolve the reservation

Tomorrowland states that Passes and Packages must be personalized by the stated deadline. If an order is not personalized in time, Tomorrowland reserves the right to cancel it and return inventory to sale, with a refund to the Main Buyer subject to the stated refund fee. [S4]

Therefore:

Paid Order != Permanently Secured Entitlement.

## Global Journey changes when personalization occurs

Global Journey tickets are personalized during purchase rather than through the later standard-ticket personalization flow. [S4]

Therefore:

One Tomorrowland Brand != One Reservation Sequence.

## DreamVille reservation is coupled to festival admission

DreamVille Packages include multi-day festival access and a selected DreamVille accommodation type. [S6][S19]

DreamVille cannot be purchased as standalone accommodation for a one-day ticket. [S6]

Therefore:

Accommodation Reservation != Independent Lodging Reservation.

## Zone entitlement is derived from package type

A DreamVille Package does not grant access to every restricted accommodation zone. [S7]

Therefore:

Accommodation Confirmed != Universal Accommodation-Area Access.

## Global Journey is a multi-provider package

Flight, train and bus Global Journey products combine admission and accommodation with external transportation and transfers. [S9][S10][S11]

Therefore:

One Reservation != One Service Provider.

## Hotel package state

Hotel packages include accommodation, breakfast, festival admission and daily transport between accommodation city and the festival. [S18]

Therefore:

Hotel Reservation Inside Global Journey != Hotel Stay Alone.

## Reservation versus service guarantee

The 2026 general terms state that programme or stage availability can be changed for compelling reasons such as weather, security or artist cancellation while the entry ticket remains valid. [S1]

Therefore:

Valid Event Entitlement != Specific Programme Guaranteed.

## Strongest reservation boundaries

1. selected inventory versus paid order;
2. paid order versus named visitor entitlement;
3. named entitlement versus usable Bracelet access;
4. Main Buyer versus individual visitor;
5. festival admission versus DreamVille accommodation;
6. DreamVille accommodation versus internal zone access;
7. event entitlement versus programme guarantee;
8. one package versus one service provider.

## Competing explanation

Personalization and package rules may be ordinary anti-fraud and event-ticketing controls.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance becomes stronger where those controls materially change accommodation, transport, payment, zone access, recovery or responsibility.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish internal reservation identifiers across systems, inventory synchronization, oversell handling, payment-failure recovery, fraud review, or modification propagation across transport and accommodation.

## Next evidence

Inspect pre-arrival information continuity: shipping, Bracelet activation, travel information, transport changes, cashless setup, accessibility and the transition from a named reservation into a physically usable arrival credential.
