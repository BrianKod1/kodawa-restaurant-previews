# Arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

At what point does a Tomorrowland Belgium visitor become operationally admitted to the festival or DreamVille, and how do Bracelet status, identity, time window, package type, accommodation zone, re-entry rules and accessibility affect the transition from purchased entitlement to physical access?

## Arrival is an entitlement validation event

Tomorrowland states that a valid Bracelet is required for festival access and that identity-linked access rules apply. [S2][S5]

The 2026 terms establish festival check-in cutoffs even for visitors holding valid entry rights. [S1]

Therefore:

Valid Ticket != Entry at Any Time.

Arrival requires both entitlement and temporal compatibility.

## Festival check-in windows

For the 2026 edition, visitors can check in on Friday and Saturday until 22:00 CEST and on Sunday until 21:00 CEST. Entry can be refused after those times even where the visitor holds a valid entry ticket. [S1][S8]

Therefore:

Valid Event Entitlement
+
Wrong Arrival Time
=
No Admission.

## DreamVille arrival has a different schedule

DreamVille operates from Thursday through Monday for each festival weekend. [S1]

DreamVille check-in has its own window and is not available on Sunday. [S1][S8]

Therefore:

Festival Arrival Window != DreamVille Arrival Window.

## DreamVille requires separate access rights

Only visitors whose Bracelet contains DreamVille access rights can enter DreamVille. [S1]

A Festival Pass or Full Madness Pass alone does not provide DreamVille access. [S17]

Therefore:

Festival Admission != Accommodation Admission.

## DreamVille zone access remains package-specific

Within DreamVille, restricted accommodation zones require the corresponding package. [S7]

Therefore:

DreamVille Admission != Universal DreamVille Movement.

## Missing physical credential can be recovered at arrival

If a Bracelet was not received before the event, the visitor can use an e-ticket and valid photo ID to obtain a Bracelet at designated pickup desks. [S12]

Therefore:

Physical Credential Missing != Admission Entitlement Missing.

## Confirmation email is not itself admission

Official guidance states that a confirmation email is not a valid Pass or Package for entry. [S2]

Therefore:

Proof of Purchase != Proof of Admittable State.

## Re-entry is service-specific

Same-day re-entry to Tomorrowland is generally not available after leaving the festival site, including for Full Madness visitors. DreamVille supports leaving and returning under its own rules and time windows. [S8]

Therefore:

Festival Re-Entry State != DreamVille Re-Entry State.

## Stage availability is another arrival layer

The 2026 terms state that stage areas can be inaccessible outside opening hours and may be changed or closed for weather, security or artist cancellation while the festival ticket remains valid. [S1]

Therefore:

Festival Admitted != Every Stage Available.

Valid Event Entitlement != Specific Experience Guaranteed.

## Accessibility arrival

Tomorrowland publishes an alternative DreamVille check-in desk plus adapted parking/camping support. [S14]

Therefore:

One Entrance Process != One Arrival Path for Every Visitor.

## Arrival and payment state

The Bracelet is also the cashless payment instrument. [S5]

A visitor can arrive with valid access but no preloaded Pearls. [S20]

Therefore:

Admitted Guest != Payment-Ready Guest Automatically.

## Global Journey handoff

Global Journey can move visitors through external carrier → city/airport/station → accommodation or DreamVille → festival transfer → festival admission. [S9][S10][S11][S18]

Therefore:

Arrival in Belgium != Arrival at Accommodation.

Arrival at Accommodation != Festival Admission.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

1. valid ticket versus valid arrival time;
2. festival arrival versus DreamVille arrival;
3. festival access versus DreamVille access;
4. DreamVille access versus zone access;
5. identity entitlement versus physical Bracelet;
6. proof of purchase versus admittable credential;
7. festival re-entry versus DreamVille re-entry;
8. festival admission versus stage availability;
9. admitted guest versus payment-ready guest;
10. arrival in Belgium versus completion of the Tomorrowland journey.

## Competing explanation

A temporary festival must enforce ordinary entry, accommodation and security controls.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS question is whether one guest relationship requires continuity across these separately validated states and recovery paths.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish queue performance, ID-check frequency, Bracelet failure rate, pickup-desk wait time, denied-entry recovery, accommodation-zone dispute recovery, accessibility fulfilment, carrier-to-festival handoff or live entrance capacity.
