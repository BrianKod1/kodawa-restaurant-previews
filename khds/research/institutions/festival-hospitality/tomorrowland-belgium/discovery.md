# Discovery

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How clearly can a prospective Tomorrowland Belgium visitor understand the difference among festival passes, DreamVille accommodation, Comfort access, Global Journey transport/accommodation packages, cashless payment and zone-specific rights before committing to the experience?

## Institution discovered as a package ecosystem

Tomorrowland Belgium is not presented publicly as one ticket product.

The inspected 2026 source set exposes several distinct discovery paths:

- festival-only Passes;
- Full Madness weekend access;
- Pleasure and Comfort variants;
- DreamVille accommodation packages;
- Global Journey flight packages;
- Global Journey train packages;
- Global Journey bus packages;
- Global Journey hotel packages;
- add-ons and ancillary experiences. [S2][S3][S9][S10][S11][S15]

Therefore:

Festival Brand != One Product.

The guest must discover not only whether Tomorrowland is available, but which combination of admission, accommodation, transport and access rights is being offered.

## Pre-registration is not purchase entitlement

The 2026 sales page states that pre-registration was required for access to relevant ticket sales but did not guarantee that a visitor would be able to purchase a ticket. [S15]

Therefore:

Pre-Registration != Purchase Entitlement.

Discovery begins before inventory is secured.

The evidence does not establish the probability that a pre-registered visitor could purchase a desired product.

## Festival pass does not include the whole hospitality ecosystem

The current pass comparison states that general Passes grant festival access, while Pleasure and Comfort variants add different Comfort-area rights. DreamVille, parking, lockers, food and drinks are not automatically included in those festival passes. [S16]

Therefore:

Festival Admission != Full Hospitality Entitlement.

A visitor can hold a valid festival pass without holding:

- DreamVille accommodation;
- DreamVille zone access;
- Comfort access;
- food and beverage;
- parking;
- locker;
- transport.

## DreamVille is a linked accommodation entitlement

DreamVille is the official festival camping ground and operates as a multi-day living environment from Thursday through Monday. [S6]

DreamVille access requires either a DreamVille Package or a Global Journey Package that includes DreamVille accommodation. [S6][S17]

A regular Festival Pass or Full Madness Pass alone does not grant DreamVille access. [S17]

Therefore:

Festival Access != DreamVille Access.

And:

DreamVille Accommodation != Separately Addable to Any Ticket.

The public FAQ states that DreamVille cannot be bought separately or combined with a one-day ticket. [S6]

## DreamVille contains internal access partitions

All DreamVille Packages grant access to common spaces such as The Gathering, Marketplace and Chill Out Area. [S7]

But Camp2Camp, Friendship Garden, Easy Tent and Montagoe are restricted to visitors holding the corresponding package. [S7]

Therefore:

DreamVille Access != Access to Every DreamVille Zone.

This is one of the strongest early Case #42 entitlement boundaries.

## Global Journey changes the object being purchased

Global Journey packages can combine festival admission with return flight, train or bus, DreamVille or hotel/hostel accommodation, transfers and Global Journey events. [S9][S10][S11]

Hotel packages can combine accommodation, breakfast, festival admission and daily transfers while the visitor arranges the inbound journey independently. [S18]

Therefore:

Tomorrowland Purchase != Always Festival Ticket Purchase.

For some visitors the commitment is a travel-and-hospitality bundle.

## Discovery depends on package composition

The Global Journey terms state that package characteristics and essential information are shown during booking, that prices are per person/package, and that availability and pricing can change during the sales period. [S1]

Therefore:

Displayed Package Option != Guaranteed Future Availability.

## Identity is already part of discovery

Official ticket channels require a Tomorrowland Account, and the public sales guidance warns that unofficially obtained tickets can be cancelled or invalid. [S2]

Discoverable Ticket != Anonymous Transferable Entitlement.

## Age eligibility

The 2026 policy states a minimum festival age of 18, with people born in 2008 permitted for the 2026 edition even if their birthday falls after the festival. [S13]

Therefore:

Inventory Available != Visitor Eligible.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

The public ecosystem presents festival-only admission, premium access, temporary accommodation, hotel accommodation, transport bundles and add-ons. [S9][S10][S11][S18]

The current evidence does not establish acquisition cost, conversion, attachment rate, contribution margin or lifetime value.

Commercial Variety != Proven Commercial Contribution.

## Strongest discovery boundaries

1. pre-registration versus purchase entitlement;
2. festival access versus DreamVille access;
3. DreamVille access versus zone access;
4. festival pass versus Comfort access;
5. admission versus accommodation;
6. admission versus travel package;
7. inventory versus visitor eligibility.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that a large festival naturally sells multiple ticket and travel products.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that these product distinctions materially change identity, access, recovery, responsibility or relationship continuity across the guest journey.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish representative buyer comprehension, conversion, sale success rates, accessibility of the purchase journey, mobile/desktop parity or secondary-market failure frequency.

## Next evidence

Inspect the reservation and personalization chain, then test how product choice becomes identity-bound access, DreamVille zone entitlement, transport responsibility and arrival eligibility.
