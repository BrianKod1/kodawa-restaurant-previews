# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What ends when a Tomorrowland Belgium visitor leaves the festival or DreamVille, and which transport, cashless, refund, lost-property, identity or account relationships can remain active after physical departure?

## DreamVille has a hard physical checkout

The 2026 practical guidance states that all DreamVille visitors must leave before Monday 12:00 and that no exceptions can be made. [S8]

Therefore:

Festival Weekend Complete != Indefinite Accommodation Entitlement.

Temporary hospitality has an explicit termination time.

## Physical checkout does not close the cashless relationship

Unspent Pearls can remain subject to automatic or manual refund after the visitor has left. [S21][S31][S32]

Therefore:

Physical Departure != Financial Relationship Closure.

This is one of the strongest Case #42 departure boundaries.

## Refund path depends on earlier payment behavior

Visitors who topped up online at least once through supported methods can qualify for automatic refund, while onsite-only top-up requires a manual refund request. [S21][S31]

Therefore:

Same Remaining Balance != Same Post-Departure Workflow.

A state created before or during the event determines the departure/closure path.

## Manual refund has a deadline

The 2026 FAQ states that the manual refund form is available only during a defined post-event window and closes on August 14, 2026 at 23:59 CEST. [S31]

Therefore:

Refund-Eligible Value != Indefinite Claim Entitlement.

Relationship closure is time-bounded.

## Refund processing can continue for weeks

Tomorrowland states that manual refunds begin processing after the request window and can take up to six weeks to become visible. [S33]

Automatic refunds can also appear later on the visitor's payment account after processing. [S32]

Therefore:

Refund Approved/Triggered != Funds Immediately Received.

The financial journey persists beyond the event.

## Lost property persists after exit

Lost-property claims can be submitted through the iLost process, and items may appear only after collection, sorting and registration. [S30]

Therefore:

Item Missing at Departure != Recovery Relationship Closed.

The visitor can leave the institution while a property-recovery relationship remains active.

## Lost-property verification creates a claimant state

The claimant must provide information sufficient to verify ownership before collection. [S30]

Therefore:

Reported Lost Item != Proven Claimant Entitlement.

Recovery includes an identity/ownership gate after departure.

## Global Journey outbound transport can remain active

For Global Journey visitors, the event journey can include return flight, train or bus after festival/accommodation departure. [S9][S10][S11]

Therefore:

Festival Exit != Global Journey Completion.

One bundled package can remain operational after the festival itself is over.

## Flight segment coupling

For flight packages, failure to take the inbound flight can cause the outbound segment to be cancelled automatically. [S9]

Therefore:

Outbound Travel Entitlement != Independent of Prior Journey State.

Departure rights can depend on a state created days earlier.

## Re-entry and final exit differ

DreamVille supports temporary leave-and-return during the weekend under stated rules, but Monday checkout is final. [S8]

Therefore:

Exit Event != Always Relationship Termination.

The system must distinguish temporary exit from final accommodation closure.

## Bracelet/account persistence

The Tomorrowland Account remains relevant after the event for manual refunds and Bracelet-related cashless administration. [S31]

Therefore:

Physical Bracelet Use Ends != Digital Account Relationship Ends.

The temporary physical institution can disappear while the digital relationship persists.

## Multiple-weekend state

Pearls are valid per weekend, and visitors with Bracelets for different weekends must treat the balances/refunds separately. [S34]

Therefore:

Same Person
+
Same Brand
!=
One Continuous Cashless Balance Across Weekends.

The temporary institution has a time-scoped commercial boundary.

## Responsibility partition after departure

Potential post-departure owners include:

- Tomorrowland cashless/refund support;
- lost-property process;
- external bank/card issuer;
- Global Journey transport provider;
- airline/train/bus operator. [S9][S10][S11][S30][S31][S32]

One Event Journey != One Closure Owner.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Departure can influence:

- refund fees;
- unspent Pearl recovery;
- transport completion;
- lost-property service burden;
- unresolved customer support. [S21][S31][S33]

The public evidence does not establish breakage, refund cost, transport recovery cost, complaint cost or lifetime value.

Physical Departure != Economic Endpoint.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. festival end versus DreamVille checkout;
2. temporary exit versus final exit;
3. physical departure versus financial closure;
4. refund-eligible value versus indefinite claim entitlement;
5. refund processing versus funds received;
6. physical departure versus lost-property recovery;
7. festival exit versus Global Journey completion;
8. outbound travel versus independent journey state;
9. physical institution closure versus digital account persistence;
10. same visitor versus one continuous balance across event windows.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that these are normal event settlement, lost-property and travel processes.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance becomes stronger if repeated temporary-hospitality cases show that closure requires coordination across physical, digital, financial and transport states after the venue relationship appears to end.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- refund success rates;
- chargeback handling;
- lost-property recovery rates;
- Global Journey missed-return recovery;
- post-event complaint ownership;
- identity-data retention duration by service;
- transport-provider handoff quality;
- financial contribution from unspent value.
