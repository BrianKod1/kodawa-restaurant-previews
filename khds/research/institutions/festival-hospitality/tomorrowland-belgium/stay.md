# Stay / Participation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does the visitor relationship operate after admission when festival access, DreamVille accommodation, internal zones, stages, Comfort areas, cashless payment, health support, shops, services and temporary facilities each have different rules, capacities or operating states?

## One admitted visitor can occupy multiple service states

Tomorrowland Belgium and DreamVille expose multiple simultaneous visitor relationships:

- festival attendee;
- DreamVille resident;
- accommodation-zone resident;
- stage participant;
- Comfort-area visitor;
- cashless customer;
- Marketplace customer;
- sports/wellness participant;
- shower/sanitary user;
- medical-support user;
- lost-property claimant. [S1][S5][S16][S24][S25][S26][S27][S28][S30]

Therefore:

Festival Admitted != One Uniform Participation State.

Admission establishes access to some services, not every service.

## DreamVille is a temporary living environment

DreamVille is described as the official campsite and includes accommodation areas, The Gathering, Marketplace, Chill Out Area and other services. [S6][S24]

Therefore:

Temporary Accommodation != Tent Space Only.

The visitor's stay relationship extends into food, retail, sanitation, recreation, information and support.

## Shared DreamVille experiences

The Gathering and Chill Out Area are available to DreamVille visitors subject to their stated operating rules. [S24]

The Marketplace provides food stands, retail, supermarket, bakery, hair salon, phone services and other facilities. [S25]

Therefore:

Accommodation Entitlement Can Unlock Shared Hospitality Infrastructure.

But this shared layer sits alongside area-specific inventory.

## Area-specific service entitlement

Easy Tents has sanitary facilities accessible only to Easy Tents visitors and food/drink options specifically available to that area. [S26]

Friendship Garden similarly has its own sanitary facilities and District House services for Friendship Garden guests. [S28]

Therefore:

DreamVille Access != Identical Service Inventory.

And:

Shared Institution != Uniform Local Facility Entitlement.

This is a strong Case #42 service-state boundary.

## Free versus paid facility state

In Easy Tents and Friendship Garden, open-air showers are described as free and continuously available, while covered hot showers are paid facilities with their own opening hours. [S26][S28]

Therefore:

Sanitary Facility Present != Every Sanitary Service Included.

One service category contains different commercial and temporal states.

## Magnificent Greens support layer

Magnificent Greens includes distributed sanitary facilities and a dedicated Aqua Care & Support area for visitors who require accommodation for medical devices or other needs. [S27]

Therefore:

General Accommodation Area != One Support Model.

Support can be layered inside a broader accommodation environment.

## Safety and medical state

The 2026 terms state that medical aid is available at Festival and DreamVille and that visitors may be transferred to a regional hospital when medically necessary. [S1]

Therefore:

On-Site Hospitality Responsibility != Every Medical Outcome Resolved On Site.

A visitor can remain inside one overall event journey while responsibility moves from festival support to external healthcare.

## Bracelet as multi-state object

The Bracelet can represent:

- admission;
- DreamVille access where included;
- zone-specific access indirectly through entitlement;
- cashless payment. [S1][S5][S7]

Therefore:

One Physical Bracelet != One Operational State.

The same token participates in different access and commercial relationships.

## Lost Bracelet state

The 2026 terms allow the visitor to deactivate a lost cashless carrier through the Cashless Account or seek support at cashless customer service. [S1]

Therefore:

Physical Token Lost != Every Underlying Relationship Lost Automatically.

But the public terms also limit organiser liability for lost or stolen carriers. [S1]

Recovery capability != Unlimited Recovery Liability.

## Stage and programme state

Programme and stage availability can change for weather, security or artist cancellation while the festival ticket remains valid. [S1]

Therefore:

Festival Participation Entitlement != Specific Stage/Artist Entitlement.

The event can remain active while one experience becomes unavailable.

## Comfort-area state

Pleasure and Comfort products add different premium-area access rights beyond general festival admission. [S16]

Therefore:

Festival Access != Premium Hospitality Access.

Premium participation is a separate entitlement state.

## Bring-your-own versus provided services

DreamVille permits visitors to bring their own food and specified quantities of drinks, while Marketplace and area-specific food services are also available. [S29]

Therefore:

Resident Food Need != Mandatory On-Site Purchase.

The temporary hospitality institution combines self-provisioning and commercial service delivery.

## Service discovery after arrival

Several services can only be arranged onsite or have onsite-specific operating rules.

Examples include Marketplace services, District House assistance and area-specific shower hours. [S25][S28]

Therefore:

Pre-Arrival Discovery != Complete Live Service State.

Some service information becomes materially relevant only after arrival.

## Responsibility continuity

During participation, responsibility can move among:

- festival organiser;
- DreamVille operations;
- accommodation-zone staff;
- security;
- medical teams;
- cashless support;
- retail/food vendors;
- external hospital care. [S1][S24][S25][S28]

One Visitor Experience != One Operational Owner.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

The stay contains both included and separately monetised relationships.

Examples include:

- included accommodation-area facilities;
- paid covered showers in some zones;
- food/drink purchases;
- add-on breakfast;
- retail;
- cashless top-ups;
- premium access. [S16][S25][S26][S28]

The evidence does not establish unit economics, vendor contribution, utilisation, labour cost or attachment rate.

Service Availability != Proven Net Contribution.

## Strongest stay boundaries

1. festival admission versus uniform participation;
2. DreamVille access versus identical local service inventory;
3. shared-area access versus restricted-area service access;
4. facility present versus facility included;
5. Bracelet possession versus one operational state;
6. festival entitlement versus specific stage availability;
7. on-site support versus external medical responsibility;
8. temporary residence versus mandatory commercial consumption.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that a large temporary festival naturally needs tiered camping, facilities and operating rules.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires repeated evidence that service-state separation materially changes entitlement, recovery, responsibility or guest continuity.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- live stage capacity;
- shower wait times;
- service utilisation;
- accommodation-zone dispute frequency;
- medical response outcomes;
- cashless support resolution time;
- vendor-level service recovery;
- live information synchronization.
