# Dining

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does food and beverage operate across Tomorrowland and DreamVille when visitors can self-provision at DreamVille, buy from shared Marketplace vendors, use area-specific dining, purchase add-on breakfast, and pay through a cashless Bracelet whose balance has its own lifecycle?

## Dining is not one service relationship

The public evidence exposes several dining modes:

- self-provided food at DreamVille;
- Marketplace food and drink stands;
- DreamVille supermarket and bakery;
- area-specific food booths;
- separately purchased breakfast;
- bars;
- festival food/drink purchases paid through the Bracelet. [S5][S25][S26][S29]

Therefore:

Festival/DreamVille Dining != One Dining Product.

## Self-provisioning is permitted at DreamVille

Visitors may bring their own food into DreamVille, while drinks are subject to defined quantity/container restrictions. [S29]

Therefore:

DreamVille Stay != On-Site Dining Purchase Requirement.

This distinguishes DreamVille from many conventional accommodation environments.

## Marketplace dining

The Marketplace includes food stands, a supermarket, bakery and beverage offerings. [S25]

Therefore:

DreamVille Accommodation != One Central Restaurant Model.

Dining inventory is distributed across a temporary village environment.

## Area-specific dining entitlement

Easy Tents provides food/drink booths available only to Easy Tents visitors. [S26]

Therefore:

DreamVille Food Available != Every DreamVille Guest Eligible for Every Dining Outlet.

Dining eligibility can inherit accommodation-zone entitlement.

## Breakfast as add-on state

Easy Tents breakfast is sold through an Add-On sale closer to the festival. [S26]

Therefore:

Accommodation Entitlement != Breakfast Entitlement Automatically.

A meal can be physically located inside the accommodation zone while remaining a separate commercial commitment.

## Cashless payment layer

Tomorrowland is cashless, and the Bracelet is used as the payment method at Tomorrowland and DreamVille through Pearls. [S5]

Therefore:

Dining Access != Dining Payment Readiness.

A visitor can be entitled to enter a dining area while lacking sufficient Pearls to complete a purchase.

## Same Bracelet, separate access and value states

The Bracelet carries both access and cashless value. [S5]

Therefore:

Access Credential State != Stored-Value State.

A valid Bracelet can have:

- admission rights;
- DreamVille rights;
- a zero Pearl balance;
- a positive Pearl balance;
- a blocked cashless state after loss.

These should not be collapsed conceptually.

## Top-up channel changes later refund workflow

Online top-up can qualify for automatic refund of eligible unspent Pearls, while onsite-only top-up requires manual refund. [S20][S21]

Therefore:

Same Dining Spend Instrument != Same Relationship-Closure Path.

The payment channel chosen before or during the event changes the post-event financial workflow.

## Bonus/non-refundable state

The terms distinguish refundable Pearls from certain non-refundable Bonus Pearls or organiser-preloaded Pearls. [S1]

Therefore:

Pearl Balance != Fully Refundable Balance.

Stored value contains sub-states.

## Lost Bracelet payment risk

A lost Bracelet can be deactivated through the visitor's account or with customer-service support. [S1]

Therefore:

Dining Payment Token Lost != Automatic Loss of Every Recoverable Value Relationship.

However, organiser liability is limited under the terms. [S1]

## Dining and zone partition

Area-restricted services show that dining cannot be modelled only as vendor availability.

Relevant state can include:

guest identity
+ DreamVille access
+ accommodation zone
+ outlet access
+ Bracelet state
+ Pearl balance
+ time.

Therefore:

Food Vendor Operating != Guest Able to Consume.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Potential commercial layers include:

- Marketplace purchases;
- supermarket;
- bakery;
- bars;
- area-specific food;
- breakfast add-ons;
- cashless top-ups. [S25][S26]

Public evidence does not establish:

- vendor revenue share;
- average spend;
- food margin;
- breakfast attachment;
- Pearl breakage;
- refund cost;
- contribution by accommodation zone.

Gross Spend != Net Contribution.

## Strongest dining boundaries

1. accommodation versus dining purchase;
2. DreamVille dining versus one central restaurant;
3. DreamVille access versus every dining outlet;
4. accommodation entitlement versus breakfast entitlement;
5. dining access versus payment readiness;
6. Bracelet access state versus Pearl balance;
7. Pearl balance versus refundable balance;
8. payment channel versus refund workflow.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that this is ordinary cashless event food service with camping options.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance lies in the coupling of accommodation-zone rights, cashless value, temporary dining infrastructure and post-event financial recovery.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- menus across all outlets;
- dietary-allergy handling;
- live queue/capacity;
- outlet opening-hour consistency;
- failed-payment recovery;
- cashless outage handling;
- vendor-level responsibility;
- profitability.
