# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a Tomorrowland Belgium visitor resolve after purchase but before arrival, and how do personalization, Bracelet delivery, activation, cashless setup, travel-provider rules, identity, accommodation, accessibility and schedule information affect whether the purchased experience is actually usable?

## Personalization is a pre-arrival gate

All standard Passes and Packages must be personalized before the stated deadline. [S4]

The name linked to the Tomorrowland Bracelet must match the visitor's identity; otherwise access can be denied. [S2][S4]

Therefore:

Reservation Confirmed != Arrival-Ready Entitlement.

## Bracelet delivery is a separate operational state

The Main Buyer normally receives the personal Tomorrowland Bracelets at home in Treasure Cases. [S5]

Incorrect address data or postal non-delivery can prevent normal Bracelet delivery. [S5]

Therefore:

Ticket Purchased != Physical Credential Delivered.

## Failed delivery has an alternate fulfilment path

If the Bracelet does not arrive in time, the visitor can collect it at DreamVille Entrance 1 or the Tomorrowland Main Entrance using the confirmation e-ticket and valid photo ID. [S12]

Therefore:

Bracelet Not Delivered != Entitlement Lost Automatically.

## Bracelet activation adds another state

Official sales guidance states that visitors need to personalize their Passes or Packages and activate their Tomorrowland Bracelets. [S2]

The Bracelet is both an access credential and the cashless payment method at Tomorrowland and DreamVille. [S5]

Therefore:

Bracelet Possession != Every Bracelet Function Active.

Access State != Payment State.

## Cashless preparation changes post-event recovery

Visitors can top up the Bracelet with Pearls online before the festival. [S20]

An online top-up through supported methods can make unspent Pearls eligible for automatic refund, while first topping up onsite can require a manual refund process. [S20][S21]

Therefore:

Same Cashless Balance != Same Refund Workflow.

A pre-arrival action can change the post-event closure path.

## Global Journey travel information

For Global Journey, the Main Buyer is sent necessary travel information before departure. [S22]

External carrier luggage and operating rules still apply. [S1]

Therefore:

Global Journey Purchase != One Uniform Travel Rule.

## Flight schedule risk

The flight-package page states that visitors should monitor schedules because schedules can change and Global Journey is not responsible for changes in the original travel schedule. It also states that failure to take the inbound flight can cause the outbound flight to be cancelled automatically. [S9]

Therefore:

Travel Package Confirmed != Original Transport Schedule Guaranteed.

Unused Inbound Segment Can Change Outbound Entitlement.

## Train and bus luggage state

Train and bus packages publish luggage limits and warn that extra luggage can be denied. [S10][S11]

Therefore:

Transport Entitlement != Unlimited Baggage Entitlement.

## Accommodation preparation differs by package

Global Journey visitors can stay in DreamVille, hotels/hostels, or in some package combinations both. [S9][S10]

Hotel transfer mode differs by accommodation city. [S9][S10][S18]

Therefore:

Global Journey Guest != One Arrival Path.

## Age and identity preparation

The 2026 festival policy makes age eligibility strict. [S13]

Because access uses personalized credentials, identity accuracy is operationally material before arrival. [S4][S13]

Therefore:

Named Ticket != Eligible Visitor Automatically.

## Accessibility preparation

Tomorrowland publishes accessible parking reservation, an alternative DreamVille check-in desk and adapted DreamVille camping support. [S14]

Therefore:

Accessibility Need != Arrival-Day Issue Only.

## Practical communications

Tomorrowland's privacy information states that practical communications can include access conditions, accessibility, payment options, weather and public-transport strikes. [S23]

Communication Sent != Visitor Prepared.

## Strongest pre-arrival boundaries

1. reservation confirmed versus personalized entitlement;
2. personalized entitlement versus delivered Bracelet;
3. delivered Bracelet versus activated functions;
4. access state versus cashless state;
5. package confirmed versus transport schedule;
6. transport entitlement versus luggage entitlement;
7. Global Journey guest versus one arrival path;
8. accessibility need versus arrival-day-only support.

## Competing explanation

These may be normal event logistics.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that separate pre-arrival states materially alter access, recovery and hospitality continuity.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish delivery success rate, activation failure rate, communication comprehension, carrier-change recovery, accessibility fulfilment quality or synchronization between external carrier changes and festival/accommodation records.

## Next evidence
