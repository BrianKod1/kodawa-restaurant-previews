# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How do room occupancy, waterpark access and optional product entitlements end relative to one another on departure day?

## Evidence

The published waterpark access window extends through waterpark closing on departure day. [S2]

This means the waterpark entitlement can continue beyond the point at which the overnight accommodation relationship is ending.

Late checkout is available as an optional purchasable product. [S5]

That creates another timing distinction because room-possession duration can be extended separately from the standard accommodation period.

The RFID wristband can function as room key and waterpark ticket. [S7]

The public evidence therefore shows that one physical credential can potentially represent entitlements whose end times are not identical.

## Interpretation

Departure is not one synchronized endpoint.

At minimum:

- room possession has one end condition;
- waterpark access has another;
- late checkout can modify the room-use period;
- separately purchased dining, attraction or cabana products may have their own validity periods.

This supports:

Checkout != end of waterpark access.

Room-access duration != waterpark-access duration.

Late checkout != extension of every resort entitlement.

One wristband != one universal expiration time.

Departure from accommodation != immediate departure from the resort experience.

## Architecture significance

This is analytically useful because the same guest identity may retain some rights after losing others.

If the wristband continues to support waterpark admission after room-key functionality ends, it would demonstrate relationship-specific entitlement timing behind a shared credential.

However, the current public evidence does not establish the actual credential-expiry mechanics.

## Competing explanation

This may simply be a guest-friendly conventional resort policy.

Hotels commonly allow use of amenities before check-in and after checkout.

Late checkout is also a standard ancillary hotel product.

These timing differences alone therefore do not establish a distinct architecture.

## Limits

The evidence does not establish:

- the exact checkout time in relation to credential deactivation;
- when room-key functionality ends;
- whether payment functionality continues after checkout;
- how attraction passes expire;
- how meal entitlements expire;
- whether cabana reservations can occur after room checkout;
- whether late checkout affects any non-room entitlement.

No checkout was observed.

No wristband expiration was tested.

No final settlement was inspected.
