# Dining

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does food-and-beverage access differ across General Admission concessions, included Premium dining, paid enhancements, dietary requirements and event-specific hospitality packages?

## Dining is not one service object

Food and beverage at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium exists across materially different states.

These include:

- General Admission concessions;
- bars;
- Marketplace-style outlets;
- Premium shared hospitality;
- suite dining;
- private dining;
- included package food;
- paid enhancements. [S4][S5][S6][S14][S15]

Therefore:

Stadium Dining != One Dining System.

## General Admission dining

The official food-and-drink material describes a broad concession network and multiple food formats. [S14]

A General Admission guest may purchase food independently of the ticket price.

Therefore:

Event Admission != Food Entitlement Automatically.

The public evidence does not establish availability, queue times or sell-out conditions.

## Premium included dining

Premium products can include different food-and-beverage propositions. [S4][S5][S6]

These may include forms such as:

- buffet;
- bowl food;
- private dining;
- three-course service;
- included drinks;
- lighter hospitality formats.

Therefore:

Dining Included != One Standard Dining Entitlement.

## Paid enhancements

Premium suite guests may purchase enhancements beyond the base package. [S15]

This means a hospitality relationship can contain:

included service
+
optional paid service.

Therefore:

Included Hospitality != Closed Commercial State.

Additional spend can occur after the original package purchase.
