# Arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Where does responsibility shift across perimeter approach, security screening, digital-ticket validation, assigned entrance, turnstile access, accessible entry and Premium-space admission?

## Arrival is a sequence of states

Arrival at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium is not one threshold.

The public evidence supports several distinct states:

- approach to the venue;
- perimeter ticket or pass check;
- bag and prohibited-item control;
- assigned entrance;
- turnstile validation;
- internal circulation;
- seat access;
- Premium hospitality-space access where applicable. [S8][S9][S10]

Therefore:

Arrival != One Door.

## Valid ticket versus successful entry

A guest can possess a valid digital ticket while still depending on:

- correct entrance;
- usable device;
- successful ticket presentation;
- security compliance;
- correct event;
- correct access state. [S8][S9][S16]

Therefore:

Valid Ticket != Completed Stadium Entry.

The public evidence does not establish the full recovery path for every failed-entry state.

## Assigned entrance

The official entry guidance states that the digital ticket identifies the entrance to use. [S8]

This creates a spatial entitlement before the guest reaches the turnstile.

Correct Event
+
Valid Ticket
does not automatically imply
Correct Entrance.

Therefore:

Ticket Validity != Route Correctness.

## Perimeter and security

Ticket or pass checks can occur at the perimeter before turnstile validation. [S8]

Bag checks and prohibited-item restrictions create a further security state. [S9]

These are operationally distinct.

A guest may therefore be:

- ticket-valid;
- security-noncompliant;
- at the wrong entrance;
- or not yet admitted.

Therefore:

Security Clearance != Ticket Entitlement.

## Turnstile state

The official entry guidance states that each ticket normally works once at the turnstile. [S8]

This creates a one-time admission transition.

Before scan:
entitled but outside.

After successful scan:
admitted inside.

This supports:

Entitlement State != Admission State.

The current evidence does not establish how re-entry, accidental scan, duplicate scan or failed scan are recovered.

## Premium and General Admission timing

Premium and General Admission can have different gate-opening times. [S8]

Premium packages may also include extended pre-event hospitality. [S4][S5][S6]

Therefore:

Venue Opening != One Universal Guest Opening Time.

A Premium guest may have a legitimate earlier arrival state than a General Admission guest.

## Stadium entry versus hospitality-space entry

A Premium package can include access to a lounge, suite or other hospitality environment in addition to stadium entry and a seat. [S3][S4][S5][S6]

This suggests at least two possible access checks:

1. admission to the venue;
2. admission to the Premium environment.

The current evidence does not establish the exact credential check at each Premium space.

Therefore:

Stadium Entry != Access to Every Hospitality Space.

This is one of the strongest Case #39 boundaries.

## Accessibility

Accessibility can alter the arrival route.

The public source set identifies accessible facilities, accessible ticketing and the Disability Access Scheme. [S12][S13]

The Disability Access Scheme is not represented as universally applicable across all non-football events. [S13]

Therefore:

Accessible Arrival != One Universal Event Process.

The evidence establishes the published structure.

It does not establish actual fulfilment quality.

## Internal circulation

Once admitted, the guest may still need to navigate:

- level;
- concourse;
- lift;
- seat;
- hospitality space. [S10]

Lift use can be prioritised or controlled for eligible accessibility users. [S10]

This demonstrates that admission to the building does not imply unrestricted use of every route.

Therefore:

Inside Stadium != Unrestricted Internal Access.

## Responsibility layers

Potential arrival responsibility owners include:

- ticketing;
- stewards;
- security;
- Premium hospitality staff;
- accessibility staff;
- venue operations.

The current public evidence does not establish one universal owner across the entire chain.

That supports the research question:

One Guest Arrival
!=
One Responsibility Owner Automatically.

## Failure states

Material arrival failures include:

- wrong entrance;
- invalid or unreadable ticket;
- unavailable phone;
- identity mismatch;
- duplicate or already-used ticket;
- prohibited bag or item;
- inaccessible route;
- missing accessibility state;
- Premium entitlement not recognised;
- seat or section mismatch.

The current evidence does not establish complete recovery ownership for each.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

Arrival != One Door

Valid Ticket != Completed Stadium Entry

Ticket Validity != Route Correctness

Security Clearance != Ticket Entitlement

Entitlement State != Admission State

Stadium Entry != Access to Every Hospitality Space

Inside Stadium != Unrestricted Internal Access

## Competing explanation

These states may be entirely conventional for a large stadium.

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that guest identity, entitlement and responsibility must remain coherent across otherwise separate operational systems.
