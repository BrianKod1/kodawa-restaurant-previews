# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Where does the hospitality relationship end when Premium access can continue after full-time, transport systems change by event phase, billing questions persist and seasonal or future-event entitlements remain active?

## Event end is not necessarily relationship end

For many attendees, the visible event ends at full-time or final performance.

But several guest relationships can continue beyond that moment. [S4][S5][S6][S11][S15][S17]

Therefore:

Event End != Immediate Hospitality Relationship End.

## Post-event Premium access

Some Premium products include hospitality access after full-time. [S4][S5][S6]

A Premium guest may therefore remain in an authorised hospitality state after ordinary event participation has ended.

This supports:

Full-Time != Hospitality Closure.

## Egress

Guests eventually transition from:

- seat;
- hospitality space;
- internal concourse;
- stadium exit;
- surrounding transport network.

These states may involve different operating teams.

The current evidence does not establish exact egress ownership.

Therefore:

Leaving Seat != Leaving Institution.

## Transport phase change

The surrounding transport environment changes around event conclusion. [S11]

Road conditions, public transport demand, shuttle services and coach departure can differ from pre-event arrival.

Therefore:

Arrival Transport State != Departure Transport State.

The guest may leave the stadium successfully but still depend on event-linked transport systems.

## Seasonal relationship persistence

Seasonal Premium membership persists beyond one match. [S5][S6]

Membership-linked rights may also include future event priority and transfer/resale capability.

Therefore:

Single Event Closure != Relationship Closure.

## Ticket Exchange and future inventory

Ticket Exchange can allow eligible ticket holders to release future or designated seats back into controlled inventory. [S17]

This means the relationship can continue into later commercial actions even when the current event is finished.

## Billing and support

Premium billing questions can continue through the Premium Experience Team. [S15]

Therefore:

Service Consumed != Financial Relationship Closed Automatically.

The current evidence does not establish dispute-resolution timing or escalation.

## Complaint and recovery state

Potential post-event unresolved matters include:

- billing issue;
- hospitality complaint;
- accessibility complaint;
- ticketing dispute;
- reseller issue;
- lost property;
- event disruption;
- future membership concern.

The current source set does not establish a unified closure workflow.

Therefore:

Physical Departure != Operational Closure.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Departure is commercially important because the relationship can continue into:

- repeat attendance;
- seasonal renewal;
- future Premium purchase;
- transfer or resale;
- post-event service;
- retention.

That creates a future research question:

Does the institution measure the guest only as one event transaction, or as a persistent commercial relationship?

The public evidence does not answer this.

Therefore:

Repeat Relationship Potential
!=
Proven Lifetime Value.

No retention or lifetime-value conclusion should be made.

## Multiple endpoints

Case #39 suggests several possible relationship endpoints:

- event ends;
- Premium hospitality closes;
- guest exits stadium;
- transport handoff ends;
- billing resolves;
- complaint resolves;
- membership expires;
- future ticket entitlement ends.

These are not necessarily simultaneous.

Therefore:

One Guest Journey
!=
One Universal End Point.

## Strongest departure boundaries

Event End != Immediate Hospitality Relationship End

Full-Time != Hospitality Closure

Leaving Seat != Leaving Institution

Arrival Transport State != Departure Transport State

Single Event Closure != Relationship Closure

Service Consumed != Financial Relationship Closed Automatically

Physical Departure != Operational Closure

One Guest Journey != One Universal End Point

## Competing explanation

These are normal post-event and membership-service processes.

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that the institution actively coordinates these different closure states as one guest relationship.
