# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Where does the hospitality relationship end when luggage release, destination arrival, transfers, excursions, onward accommodation and complaints can continue beyond leaving the train?

## Departure is not one state

Physical disembarkation does not necessarily close the guest relationship.

Depending on journey and cabin tier, the guest may still have:

- luggage to recover;
- transfer entitlement;
- excursion participation;
- onward accommodation;
- unresolved complaint;
- baggage claim;
- payment or service issue.

Public evidence therefore supports:

Leaving the train
!=
all responsibilities ended.

## Destination transfer

Representative journey material includes destination transfers as part of some journey configurations. [S3]

Grand Suites and L'Observatoire can also attach private departure-transfer entitlements. [S7][S8]

This means the guest may leave VSOE's physical carriage while remaining inside a journey-related service relationship.

Therefore:

Disembarkation
!=
journey closure.

## Luggage release

Through luggage remains inaccessible during the journey and becomes relevant again at destination. [S5]

Its return is therefore a distinct closure event.

Guest arrives
!=
luggage relationship automatically closed.

The current evidence does not establish:

- exact baggage release;
- identity verification;
- handoff evidence;
- missing-bag escalation;
- delayed-bag recovery;
- transfer responsibility after luggage collection.

## Multi-day itinerary boundary

The Paris-Istanbul product demonstrates that the sold journey can contain off-train components and excursions within the broader itinerary. [S4]

Therefore:

Off train
!=
outside the journey.

This is important because ordinary hotel-style checkout logic cannot automatically describe the whole product.

A guest can leave the carriage while remaining inside the institutionally sold itinerary.

## Complaint continuity

The Conditions of Carriage preserve complaint and luggage-claim mechanisms beyond the physical journey. [S11]

This supports:

Service consumption ended
!=
responsibility relationship ended.

The evidence does not establish whether all complaint categories use one channel or owner.

## Delayed-arrival problem

Published timings remain subject to railway constraints. [S3][S4]

This creates potential departure problems such as:

- delayed arrival;
- missed onward transport;
- missed hotel check-in;
- changed transfer time;
- excursion disruption;
- baggage delay.

The public sources establish timing uncertainty.

They do not establish how every downstream consequence is recovered.

Scheduled arrival
!=
guaranteed connection state.

## Premium departure state

Grand Suite and L'Observatoire guests can hold premium private-transfer entitlements. [S7][S8]

This again supports:

Cabin tier
!=
accommodation difference only.

The relationship can remain differentiated after the guest leaves the carriage.

## Journey closure model

Possible closure events include:

1. cabin vacated;
2. train disembarked;
3. luggage returned;
4. destination transfer completed;
5. excursion completed;
6. onward hotel handoff completed;
7. complaint resolved;
8. baggage claim resolved;
9. payment issue resolved.

These are analytically different states.

The public evidence does not establish that all occur in every journey.

## Early exit

The current source set does not establish complete handling of:

- voluntary early departure;
- illness;
- denied entry;
- missed border requirement;
- railway disruption;
- service failure;
- abandoned journey;
- emergency evacuation.

This is a significant evidence gap for a mobile hospitality institution.

## Responsibility ambiguity

Potential departure responsibility may sit with:

- VSOE;
- railway carrier;
- transfer provider;
- hotel;
- excursion provider;
- luggage handler;
- insurer;
- guest.

The legal evidence confirms that different providers can exist. [S10][S11]

It does not establish one universal recovery hierarchy.

## Architecture pressure

Departure is one of the strongest Case #38 architecture tests.

If VSOE maintains coherent responsibility after the train portion ends, that supports a broader hospitality-relationship model.

If responsibilities simply revert to ordinary independent travel providers after each component, the conventional packaged-travel explanation becomes stronger.

Therefore:

Brand continuity
!=
responsibility continuity automatically.

## Public-evidence limitations

No luggage return was observed.

No transfer was used.

No excursion was taken.

No onward connection was missed.

No complaint was submitted.

No baggage claim was filed.

No early departure occurred.

No disruption recovery was observed.
