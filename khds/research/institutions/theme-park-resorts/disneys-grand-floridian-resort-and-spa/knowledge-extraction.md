# Knowledge Extraction

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Case purpose

Case #36 tests whether the provisional Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recurs in a Deluxe theme-park resort hotel where one lodging relationship creates hotel status and planning advantages while theme-park admission, accommodation-tier benefits, dining, Lightning Lane and premium Resort products retain distinct eligibility conditions.

Primary falsification question:

Does Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recur in a theme-park resort where lodging creates hotel-specific benefits and priority windows but theme-park admission, dining, Lightning Lane, special access periods and other experiences retain separate entitlement requirements, or are those differences fully explained by conventional destination-resort packaging?

## Extracted institutional relationships

### 1. Grand Floridian lodging relationship

The core relationship is an overnight stay at Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, a Disney Deluxe Resort. [S1][S2]

The lodging relationship establishes accommodation and Resort-guest status.

Public material also associates the Resort with amenities and complimentary Disney transportation. [S1][S3]

The evidence therefore supports:

Lodging reservation -> Grand Floridian Resort-guest status.

Grand Floridian Resort-guest status != theme-park admission.

Accommodation relationship != ownership of every Disney entitlement.

### 2. Club Level relationship

Club Level rooms and suites create narrower benefits beyond standard accommodation.

Those benefits include secured access to the Royal Palm Club lounge and Personalized Guest Services. [S2][S3]

Disney Signature Services also provides vacation-planning support to eligible Club Level guests. [S3]

The evidence supports:

Standard accommodation != Club Level entitlement.

Grand Floridian guest != Royal Palm Club access holder.

Hotel stay != identical service level for every guest.

Accommodation tier can alter service and spatial entitlement.

### 3. Theme-park admission relationship

Theme-park access remains separately governed.

Eligible Disney Resort hotel guests can receive Early Theme Park Entry, but valid theme-park admission remains separately required. [S5][S6]

### 4. Early Theme Park Entry relationship

- accommodation tier;
- theme-park admission;
- eligibility window;

One travelling party could therefore contain people with different combinations of:

- dining reservations;

The same person can also move through multiple relationships during one Disney trip without those relationships becoming interchangeable.

However, none of this establishes the underlying responsibility architecture.

## Strongest competing explanation

The strongest competing explanation is conventional destination-resort segmentation.

Under that explanation:

- lodging is the primary hotel relationship;
- Club Level is a premium accommodation tier;
- theme parks are separately ticketed attractions;
- Early Theme Park Entry is a lodging-linked benefit;
- Extended Evening Theme Park Hours are a Deluxe Resort benefit;
- Lightning Lane is a separately purchased queue-access product;
- dining reservations are ordinary restaurant capacity commitments;
- cabanas are premium pool products;
- transportation is a destination mobility amenity.

This explanation accounts for the public evidence without requiring a distinct architecture theory.

It remains a strong and sufficient explanation at current evidence scope.

## Falsification result

Case #36 does not falsify the provisional architecture.

It also does not materially strengthen it enough to justify governance movement.

The correct bounded result is:

Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa demonstrates unusually explicit layered eligibility across lodging, accommodation tier, theme-park admission, timing benefits, dining, Lightning Lane, transportation and cabanas. This structure is directionally compatible with Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture, but conventional destination-resort segmentation remains a strong and sufficient competing explanation at public-evidence scope.

The case strengthens the falsification discipline through three bounded findings:

Layered eligibility != automatically multi-relationship architecture.

Eligibility advantage != ownership of the underlying entitlement.

Shared destination identity != unified responsibility architecture.
