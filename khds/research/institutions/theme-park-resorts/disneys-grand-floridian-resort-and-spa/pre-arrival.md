# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What planning privileges, service relationships and additional entitlements become available between booking and arrival, and how are they connected to the confirmed Grand Floridian lodging relationship?

## Evidence

A confirmed Disney Resort hotel reservation can alter the guest's planning window before arrival.

Guests with a valid Disney Resort hotel reservation can make advance dining reservations at select restaurants for their entire length of stay, up to a 10-night stay, beginning up to 60 days before arrival. [S4]

This creates a timing advantage associated with the lodging relationship without making the dining reservation itself part of the room entitlement.

Eligible Disney Resort hotel guests also receive an earlier purchase window for Lightning Lane passes.

Public material states that eligible Resort-hotel guests can purchase Lightning Lane passes and choose experiences up to 7 days before the first day of their Resort stay for eligible stay dates. [S1][S5][S8]

Lightning Lane remains separately purchased, and valid theme-park admission remains separately required. [S8]

Accommodation tier can also change the pre-arrival service relationship.

Guests staying in Club Level rooms and suites are eligible for Personalized Guest Services and Disney Signature Services, which provide vacation-planning support beyond the standard accommodation relationship. [S2][S3]

The Club Level Concierge can also assist with activities such as purchasing tickets and making dining reservations. [S3]

The public evidence therefore shows that the confirmed lodging relationship can affect:

- when certain dining reservations can be made;
- when Lightning Lane passes can be purchased;
- access to personalized vacation-planning support for eligible Club Level guests;
- preparation for transportation, dining and other Disney experiences.

## Interpretation

The period between booking and arrival can materially change the guest's entitlement and planning stack.

A guest may begin with:

- a Grand Floridian room reservation;
- registered Resort-guest status;
- a selected accommodation tier.

Before arrival, the guest may then acquire or arrange:

- theme-park admission;
- dining reservations;
- Lightning Lane passes;
- Club Level planning support where eligible;
- other separately reserved experiences.

This supports several bounded distinctions:

Reservation confirmed != trip entitlement stack complete.

Hotel reservation != theme-park admission.

Advance booking privilege != underlying product entitlement.

Eligibility to purchase early != purchase completed.

Dining reservation != park-entry entitlement.

Standard accommodation != Club Level planning relationship.

Guest information held by Disney != proof that every operational unit has reconciled the full trip.

## Pre-arrival research significance

Case #36 creates a strong test of whether booking-to-arrival functions as an active coordination phase across several related but non-identical Disney relationships.

The public evidence demonstrates meaningful planning windows and service privileges.

It does not, however, establish the actual communication sequence used by Grand Floridian after a room reservation is confirmed.

The evidence therefore does not establish whether Grand Floridian systematically uses confirmation messages, app notifications, concierge outreach or other pre-arrival communication to:

- reinforce reservation confidence;
- reconcile room and park-admission status;
- explain Early Theme Park Entry eligibility;
- explain Extended Evening Theme Park Hours eligibility;
- surface dining opportunities;
- prompt Lightning Lane purchase;
- coordinate transportation;
- collect accessibility or room preferences;
- promote cabanas or other paid experiences;
- summarize the guest's complete entitlement stack before arrival.

Those remain research questions rather than findings.

## Competing explanation

The observed pre-arrival advantages may be fully explained by conventional destination-resort planning.

A large theme-park destination can give hotel guests earlier booking windows and premium-tier concierge support simply to improve vacation planning and manage demand.

Under that explanation, different planning windows and services do not demonstrate a distinct multi-relationship hospitality architecture.

The stronger architecture claim would require evidence that pre-arrival coordination materially changes:

- customer identity;
- responsibility;
- entitlement;
- operational preparation;
- cross-product recovery;
- persistence of guest information;
- ownership of unresolved requests.

## Limits

The current evidence does not establish:

- reservation-confirmation email content;
- reminder cadence;
- My Disney Experience notification cadence;
- personalization rules;
- preference collection;
- transport-preference collection;
- accessibility-request workflow;
- upsell targeting;
- pre-arrival service-recovery ownership;
- whether dining, ticket, Lightning Lane and hotel selections are proactively reconciled by Grand Floridian staff;
- whether Club Level planning support sees or owns every element of the guest's trip.

No confirmation email was inspected.

No Grand Floridian pre-arrival email sequence was systematically reviewed.

No authenticated My Disney Experience pre-arrival journey was tested.

No Disney Signature Services interaction was conducted.
