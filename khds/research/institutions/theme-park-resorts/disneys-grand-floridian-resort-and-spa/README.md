# Case #36 - Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Institution

Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa is being studied as a Deluxe theme-park resort hotel combining overnight accommodation, dining, recreation, Disney transportation, tier-specific hotel benefits and access to a larger separately ticketed theme-park ecosystem.

Sector: theme-park-resorts

Research access date: 2026-10-12.

This dossier is based on inspected public sources. It is not an on-site audit, booking-flow transaction test, park-entry test, credential-system audit, accessibility assessment, service-recovery test or proof of representative guest outcomes.

## Research role

Case #36 tests whether the provisional Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recurs where one hotel stay can create multiple eligibility advantages without automatically granting every related Disney entitlement.

The primary falsification question is:

Does Role-Partitioned / Multi-Relationship Hospitality Architecture recur in a theme-park resort where lodging creates hotel-specific benefits and priority windows but theme-park admission, dining, Lightning Lane, special access periods and other experiences retain separate entitlement requirements, or are those differences fully explained by conventional destination-resort packaging?

Case #36 deliberately tests a layered destination-resort structure:

- overnight lodging;
- standard versus Club Level accommodation;
- complimentary Resort transportation;
- Early Theme Park Entry linked to eligible hotel status but still requiring valid park admission;
- Extended Evening Theme Park Hours associated with eligible Deluxe Resort status;
- separately purchased Lightning Lane passes;
- dining reservations that do not themselves create park-entry entitlement;
- separately reservable Grand Floridian cabanas restricted to Resort guests.

## Initial boundaries under test

Resort hotel reservation != theme-park admission.

Eligible Resort-hotel status != unrestricted theme-park access.

Dining reservation != theme-park admission.

Early Theme Park Entry eligibility != park-entry entitlement by itself.

Deluxe Resort status != every Disney Resort hotel status.

Standard accommodation != Club Level entitlement.

Earlier Lightning Lane purchase eligibility != Lightning Lane entitlement without purchase.

Resort-guest eligibility != cabana reservation entitlement.

Shared Disney destination identity != one undifferentiated guest relationship.

Shared digital or physical credential != proof of unified operational responsibility.

Physical proximity to a theme park != admission to that theme park.
