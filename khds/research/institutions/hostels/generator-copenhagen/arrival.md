## Purpose

This file studies the transition from reaching Copenhagen to becoming an appropriately oriented Generator Copenhagen guest.

Arrival includes:

* destination navigation;
* finding the property;
* recognizing the entrance;
* crossing the physical threshold;
* locating reception;
* retrieving the booking;
* verifying identity and eligibility;
* resolving payment;
* confirming the correct room or bed;
* receiving access;
* understanding public and private zones;
* locating essential facilities;
* and recovering from an arrival-stage failure.

Arrival is not complete merely because the guest reaches the address.

```text
Physical Presence

≠

Hospitality Entry
```

## Core Arrival Question

> Can Generator Copenhagen move different guests from the city, through one public and socially active threshold, into the correct private or shared accommodation state without losing identity, eligibility, privacy or recovery responsibility?

## Arrival Failure States

### Property cannot be located

Recovery requires a reachable property contact and usable navigation assistance.

### Entrance cannot be identified or accessed

Recovery requires visible signage, a working call route or staff assistance.

### Booking cannot be found

Reception must investigate before asking the guest to repurchase.

### Identification is missing or rejected

The guest needs a clear explanation and a safe next step.

### Payment cannot be verified

The system needs an alternative-payment or escalation route.

### Minor is ineligible or undocumented

The institution must preserve policy while addressing immediate guest safety.

### Booked room category is unavailable

Recovery may require:

* equivalent or better accommodation;
* a different room;
* rebooking;
* refund;
* or assistance locating an alternative.

### Friends are separated

The guest must be told whether regrouping is possible and what tradeoff it requires.

### Accessible allocation is unsuitable

Recovery must prioritize functional compatibility rather than nominal room category.

### Women-only allocation is incorrect

The institution must restore the confirmed category boundary or provide an acceptable alternative.

### Bed or room is not ready

Recovery may require luggage storage, waiting space, early-access refund or updated timing.

### Night arrival was not recorded

Reception must distinguish delay from no-show and retrieve the reservation.
