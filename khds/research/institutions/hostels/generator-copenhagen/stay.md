## Core Stay Question

> How does Generator Copenhagen preserve sleep, privacy, belongings, personal agency and institutional responsibility when unrelated guests may share one room?

### Bed defect

The institution must determine whether:

* the bed can be repaired;

### Bathroom failure

Recovery depends on access to another appropriate facility.

### Incorrect room category

Recovery must address functional compatibility, not merely nominal room equivalence.

### Lost access credential

The institution must verify identity and restore access without exposing the room to an unauthorized person.

### Room change during a continuous stay

The institution should explain the reason, protect belongings and minimize disruption.
