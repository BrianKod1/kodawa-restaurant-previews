## Purpose

This file studies the period between confirmed booking and physical arrival at Generator Copenhagen.

Pre-arrival hospitality should help the guest become:

* correctly informed;
* appropriately documented;
* financially prepared;
* geographically oriented;
* compatible with the booked accommodation;
* ready for shared-space conditions;
* and able to recover if plans change.

This stage is especially important for a hostel because a confirmed booking may still depend on:

* guest eligibility;
* identification;
* payment-card presentation;
* guardian documentation;
* arrival timing;
* bed or room interpretation;
* shared-room expectations;
* accessibility confirmation;
* and group coordination.

```text
Confirmed Booking

≠

Prepared Arrival
```

## Core Pre-Arrival Question

> Does Generator Copenhagen convert a confirmed accommodation transaction into sufficient readiness for the guest’s actual room, bed, arrival time and participation conditions?

## Pre-Arrival State

The guest enters pre-arrival with a booking record.

The institution should help transform that record into an executable visit.

```text
Booking Confirmation

↓

Accommodation Understanding

↓

Eligibility Readiness

↓

Document Readiness

↓

Payment Readiness

↓

Travel Readiness

↓
```

Failure at any stage may convert a valid booking into:

* delayed access;
* refused check-in;
* unexpected payment;
* incorrect room expectations;
* group separation;
* inaccessible accommodation;
* or loss of the reservation.

## Confirmation Review

Before travel, the guest should be able to verify:

* Generator Copenhagen as the correct property;
* the street address;
* arrival and departure dates;
* number of nights;
* customer name;
* guest count;
* room or bed type;
* whether the accommodation is private or shared;
* dormitory capacity;
* whether a women-only category applies;
* the applicable rate;
* amount paid;
* amount still payable;
* cancellation conditions;
* modification conditions;
* and required arrival documents.

For shared accommodation, the confirmation must preserve:

```text
One Bed

inside

A Shared Room
```

It should not allow a guest to infer that purchasing one bed creates control over the complete dormitory.

For a private multi-bed room, the confirmation should preserve:

```text
Exclusive Room Use

with

Defined Maximum Occupancy
```

## Customer and Guest Reconciliation

The person who made the booking may not be every person who arrives.

Pre-arrival communication should distinguish:

* booking customer;
* payment-card holder;
* lead guest;
* additional guests;
* group leader;
* minor;
* guardian;
* and accessibility contact.

This matters because different people may carry different responsibilities.

```text
Customer Ready

≠

Every Guest Ready
```

A group may possess one valid confirmation while individual members remain unprepared for:

* identification;
* age rules;
* room allocation;
* or behavioural conditions.

## Identification Readiness

Generator’s published terms require valid photo identification for guests at check-in.

Accepted categories are publicly described as:

* passport;
* national identity card;
* or driver’s licence, subject to relevant location rules.

Pre-arrival communication should state:

* who must present identification;
* which documents are acceptable;
* whether copies are sufficient;
* whether the booking name must match;
* and what happens when identification is missing.

The consequence is potentially severe.

A guest may travel successfully to Copenhagen but remain unable to enter the booked accommodation.

```text
Arrival at Property

≠

Eligibility to Check In
```

## Pre-Arrival Failure States

### Booking details are incorrect

Recovery requires timely access to booking management or human assistance.

### Confirmation is missing

The guest must verify whether a booking exists before making another purchase.

### Guest cannot produce accepted identification

The strongest intervention occurs before travel.

### Original payment card is unavailable

The guest needs a documented alternative before arrival.

### Minor lacks required documentation

The institution should identify the failure before the guest travels.

### Friends expect to share one dormitory

The non-guaranteed allocation condition must be restated before arrival.

### Accessibility remains unconfirmed

The guest should not be left to discover incompatibility at reception.

### Arrival changes to after midnight

The guest needs a working contact route and confirmation that the update was received.

### Early arrival occurs before bed readiness

The guest needs a luggage and waiting plan.

### Transport route fails

The guest needs the property address, phone number and an alternate navigation route.

### Luggage does not fit available storage

The guest needs an alternative rather than an arrival-stage surprise.

### Dietary needs conflict with food restrictions

The guest requires clarification before entering a no-external-food environment.
