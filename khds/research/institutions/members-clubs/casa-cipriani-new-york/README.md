# KHDS Case #29 — Casa Cipriani New York

## Scope
Public-evidence study of Casa Cipriani New York as a private members' club with hotel accommodation, dining, events and wellness.

## Research role
Third independent comparator in the provisional Membership-Gated / Recurring-Entitlement Hospitality family after Soho House Toronto and The Arts Club London.

## Core question
Does recurring-entitlement hospitality still hold when membership identity overlaps with hotel accommodation, dining, wellness, events, prepaid value and a broader brand relationship?

## Primary boundaries
```text
Membership Continuity != Uniform Access Continuity
Membership Status != Guaranteed Service Inventory
Hotel Stay != Full Membership Entitlement
Prepaid Value != Guaranteed Eligibility or Availability
Brand Community != Verified Belonging Outcome
Physical Departure != Relationship Closure
Published Privacy Policy != Verified Privacy Outcome
```

## Evidence position
The strongest public evidence concerns membership/hotel/dining/wellness proposition, cultural programming, gift-card value and data/privacy relationships. All ten dimensions remain partial.
