# Discovery

Casa Cipriani New York presents membership, hotel accommodation, Cipriani dining, wellness and a private community under one brand relationship.

S1, S3 and S4 indicate several distinct participation routes: membership, hotel use, dining/wellness participation, editorial/cultural engagement and prepaid gift-card value.

```text
One Brand != One Access Route
```

Potential failure states include assuming hotel booking equals membership, assuming gift-card ownership guarantees member-only services, or assuming membership guarantees availability.

Public material supports proposition-level distinctions but not representative user comprehension.

Rating: **partial**

Sources: S1, S3, S4
