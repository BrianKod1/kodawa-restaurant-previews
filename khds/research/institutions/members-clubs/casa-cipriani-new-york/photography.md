# Photography / Visual Proof

Official imagery can support claims about architecture, accommodations, dining settings, wellness spaces and curated social atmosphere.

It cannot independently prove membership entitlement, live availability, privacy outcomes, accessibility, guest sponsorship, service recovery or belonging.

```text
Visible Luxury != Verified Operating Performance
Curated Image != Representative Operation
```

Case #29 reinforces KHDS treatment of photography as bounded use-case evidence rather than universal proof.
