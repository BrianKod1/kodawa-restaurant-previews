# Story / Narrative

## Public narrative

The Arts Club frames itself as a Mayfair private members' club with a creative community connected to contemporary cultural life.

Its public proposition links membership, art, cultural programming, food and drink, private events, hotel accommodation and wellness-related access.

## Organising logic

```text

Organises
Multiple Hospitality Services
```

That relationship is governed by formal rules, categories, fees, sponsorship conditions and operator discretion.

## Membership categories

Public information distinguishes multiple membership categories with different eligibility and fee structures.

## Hotel and wellness narrative

Hotel accommodation and wellness access extend the club story beyond a conventional members' lounge, but their separate booking and activation conditions reinforce that this is a network of related hospitality products rather than one uniform entitlement.
