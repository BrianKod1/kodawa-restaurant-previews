# KHDS Case #28 — The Arts Club London

## Scope

This dossier studies The Arts Club London as a private members' club with integrated hotel, dining, cultural programming and wellness access.

The evidence base is limited to public operator material recorded in `sources.json` and `coverage.json`. It does not verify representative member experience, operational performance, accessibility fulfilment, enforcement consistency, belonging outcomes, safety outcomes, dietary suitability, recovery effectiveness or causal effects.

## Research role

Case #28 is an independent replication / falsification case for membership-governed hospitality.

It follows Case #27 — Soho House Toronto — and tests whether recurring membership, guest sponsorship, role-dependent access, service-specific capacity and continuing responsibility recur in a second private-membership institution.

## Core research question

How does a private members' club preserve legible entitlement, sponsorship, privacy, responsibility and recovery when membership access overlaps with hotel stays, dining reservations, events, nightlife and wellness services?

## Evidence position

The strongest public evidence concerns membership categories and privileges, member and guest check-in, guest sponsorship and host dependency, dining reservations and cancellation rules, event RSVP conditions, dress code and access enforcement, privacy and no-press rules, hotel booking and departure conditions, wellness activation, and continuing membership obligations.

Current evidence coverage remains partial across all ten dimensions.
