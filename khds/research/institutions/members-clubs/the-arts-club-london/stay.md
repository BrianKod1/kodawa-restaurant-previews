# Stay / Service Delivery

## Recurring entitlement under conditions

Membership can continue across repeated visits while participation remains governed by membership status, guest sponsorship, space-specific rules, event capacity, dining availability, dress expectations, privacy rules, behaviour requirements and hotel-specific conditions.

## Guest responsibility

Published rules make members responsible for guest compliance.

```text
Member Responsibility
!=
Operator Responsibility Absent
```

The operator still controls access, publishes rules and can impose warnings, bans, suspension or withdrawal of membership.

## Membership continuity

Rules and Bye-Laws establish annual renewal review and consequences for overdue subscriptions, including denial of club access and possible suspension or termination.

```text
Membership Relationship
!=
Unconditional Access
```

## Service-specific variation

Hotel use introduces room occupancy conditions and staff-access rights. Events use RSVP and guest limits. Dining uses separate booking rules. Wellness access can require separate activation and a health questionnaire.

## Privacy during participation

The rules impose a no-press and confidentiality expectation concerning members, guests and events.

## Evidence boundary

Public rules establish governance mechanisms but not enforcement consistency, representative member experience, dispute resolution quality or accessibility fulfilment.
