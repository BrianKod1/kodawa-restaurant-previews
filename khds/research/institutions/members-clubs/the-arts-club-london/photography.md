# Photography / Visual Proof

## Visual evidence constraint

The Arts Club is useful for KHDS because privacy rules constrain what photography can legitimately prove.

The Rules and Bye-Laws impose confidentiality expectations around members, guests and events, including restrictions on disclosure through social and other media.

## Official imagery

Official imagery can show architecture, room configuration, finishes, dining spaces, art and selected hospitality settings.

It cannot by itself prove entitlement, sponsorship, membership status, actual availability, privacy enforcement, accessibility fulfilment, belonging or service recovery.

```text
Visible Space
!=
Verified Institutional Performance

Photographed Availability
!=
Live Access Entitlement
```

## Privacy paradox

```text
Limited Public Photography
!=
No Internal Hospitality Activity
```

## Research implication

Case #28 strengthens the KHDS requirement that photography be treated as bounded evidence rather than universal proof.
