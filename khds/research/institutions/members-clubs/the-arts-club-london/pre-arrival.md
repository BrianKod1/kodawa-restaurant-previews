# Pre-arrival

## Role-dependent preparation

Pre-arrival requirements differ by participant role.

Members and guests must account for check-in expectations, guest accompaniment, age restrictions, dress code, privacy expectations, event RSVP conditions and behaviour rules.

Hotel guests additionally face booking guarantees, cancellation and no-show rules, check-in and checkout times, room-occupancy limits, pet restrictions and room-use conditions.

Hotel guests seeking Lanserhof access must request it through Reception and complete a mandatory health questionnaire.
