# Dining

## Dining inside membership hospitality

Food and drink form part of the club proposition, but dining operates through its own reservation and capacity logic.

Members may use an online booking route using their membership ID.

```text
Membership
!=
Table Reservation

Membership Payment
!=
Guaranteed Dining Capacity
```

## Guest dining

A guest may arrive ahead of a host member for a reservation, but continued presence remains dependent on the member arriving.

```text
Dining Reservation
!=
Independent Guest Entitlement
```

## Behavioural conditions

Wider club rules, dress expectations, guest sponsorship and privacy requirements continue to apply around dining.

## Evidence gaps

Reviewed operator material does not establish live table availability, priority between membership categories, waitlist behaviour, allergy-handling performance, dietary suitability or service-recovery outcomes.
