# Departure / Closure

## Physical departure versus institutional closure

For a recurring-membership institution, leaving the building is not the same as ending the hospitality relationship.

```text
Physical Departure
!=
Membership Relationship End
```

A participant may leave while membership status, payment obligations, renewal state, conduct history, privacy responsibilities, personal data, reservations, hotel billing or unresolved complaints remain active.

## Hotel checkout

Public hotel terms establish a defined room checkout time.

```text
Hotel Checkout
!=
Full Relationship Closure
```

## Membership renewal and termination

Rules and Bye-Laws describe annual review, overdue subscription consequences, suspension or termination possibilities and resignation conditions.

## Evidence boundary

Public material does not establish complaint-resolution performance, disputed-charge recovery, post-visit communication quality or exact operational retention practices.
