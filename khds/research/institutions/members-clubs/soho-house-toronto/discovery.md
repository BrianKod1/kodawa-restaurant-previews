# Discovery

## Research question

Can a prospective member, sponsored guest or private-event customer understand what Soho House Toronto is, who it is for and which access model applies?

## Public identity

Soho House Toronto is publicly presented as a members' club rather than an ordinary open-access restaurant, bar or event venue.

The Toronto House combines dining, member events, social spaces and other club functions inside a continuing membership system. [S1]

Membership material presents access as an ongoing relationship that begins through application rather than an immediate one-time purchase. [S4]

## Multiple discovery routes

The institution is not represented through only one access route.

A prospective user may encounter:

- membership;
- member-sponsored guest access;
- member events;
- member dining and House use;
- private-event booking;
- and other House-specific services.

Toronto event-space material creates an important boundary because some spaces or uses may operate through member access while private-event material also supports commercial enquiries outside ordinary membership participation. [S7][S8]

Therefore:

```text
Private Members' Club
!=
Single Access Route
```

## Discovery burden

The public proposition asks users to understand several different questions:

1. Am I eligible to apply for membership?
2. If I am a member, what does membership permit?
3. If I am a guest, whose sponsorship governs my access?
4. If I am attending a private event, does membership matter?
5. Does access to the House imply access to every area or activity?

These are related but not identical entitlement questions.

## Membership as continuing relationship

Unlike a single admission purchase, membership may continue across many visits and may involve:

- application;
- approval;
- renewal;
- identification;
- House Rules;
- bookings;
- guest sponsorship;
- conduct obligations;
- payment relationships;
- and possible restriction or termination.

This means discovery is partly discovery of an institution and partly discovery of a governance relationship.

## Evidence boundary

Current public evidence establishes Soho House Toronto's stated identity and several access routes.

It does not establish:

- how easily prospective users understand the distinctions;
- live membership availability;
- live space capacity;
- application acceptance probability;
- or whether users correctly infer their practical access rights before arrival.

## Rating

**Partial.**

The institution and principal access models are visible, but the clarity and live operation of those access models remain untested.
