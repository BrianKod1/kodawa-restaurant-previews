# Pre-Arrival

## Research question

What must a member, sponsored guest or other authorized participant understand before arriving at Soho House Toronto?

## Preparation is role-dependent

Pre-arrival preparation differs according to the person's participation state.

A user may be:

- a member;
- a member's guest;
- an event attendee;
- a private-event organizer or guest;
- or another authorized participant.

Those states may carry different identity, sponsorship, payment and behavioural requirements.

## Membership identity

House Rules establish membership credentials as part of House access. [S3]

Membership is therefore not only an account relationship. It must be translated into a usable arrival identity.

```text
Membership Record
→
Recognizable Member Identity
→
Physical Admission
```

The public evidence does not establish every Toronto fallback when a credential is unavailable.

## Guest preparation

A sponsored guest depends on the member relationship.

House Rules place responsibility for guests on the member and restrict ordinary independent guest entry. [S3]

The guest therefore needs more than knowledge of the address.

They may need clarity about:

- who is sponsoring them;
- whether the member must be present;
- applicable House Rules;
- payment responsibility;
- photography or privacy expectations;
- and area-specific restrictions.

## Toronto-specific rules

Toronto publishes location-specific operating information, including restrictions or conditions involving laptops, calls, children and rooftop use. [S2]

These rules demonstrate that participation can vary by:

- space;
- time;
- activity;
- age;
- weather;
- and operating condition.

Therefore:

```text
House Access
!=
Uniform Use Permission
```

## Digital preparation

The Soho House app forms part of the continuing member relationship and supports booking and member functions. [S5]

The public record therefore suggests a digital preparation layer before physical arrival.

The evidence does not establish:

- app uptime;
- device failure recovery;
- whether all Toronto functions require the app;
- or whether equivalent human channels always exist.

## Privacy and data

Soho House's privacy framework describes membership, reservation, guest, visit, payment and other personal-data relationships. [S9]

This makes pre-arrival preparation partly an identity-and-data process as well as a hospitality process.

## Accessibility gap

The current Case #27 evidence set does not yet provide sufficiently detailed Toronto-specific accessibility preparation evidence.

No positive accessibility-fulfilment conclusion should be inferred from the current sources.

## Unresolved questions

The research still needs to determine:

- Toronto-specific accessibility preparation;
- exact arrival instructions;
- credential-failure fallback;
- how temporary area restrictions are communicated;
- how guests receive rules before arrival;
- and whether private-event attendees follow a distinct arrival pathway.

## Rating

**Partial.**

Pre-arrival rules and role dependencies are visible, but accessibility, communication consistency and exception recovery remain incomplete.
