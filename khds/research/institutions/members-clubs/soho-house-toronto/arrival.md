# Arrival

## Research question

How is membership or other authorized participation converted into physical admission at Soho House Toronto?

## Arrival is an identity event

For an ordinary member visit, physical admission depends on a continuing membership relationship rather than only a one-time booking.

House Rules require members to present a physical or digital membership card for entry. [S3][S6]

This creates a threshold sequence:

```text
Membership Status
→
Recognizable Credential
→
Admission Decision
→
House Access
```

A valid membership relationship therefore has to be made legible at the physical threshold.

## Guest arrival depends on sponsorship

Ordinary guest access is not equivalent to independent member access.

House Rules state that guests cannot enter without the member present under the ordinary guest route. [S3]

This means the guest's arrival right is relational:

```text
Guest Identity
+
Member Sponsorship
+
Member Presence
=
Potential Admission
```

The public evidence does not establish all Toronto exceptions to that sequence.

## Multiple arrival pathways

Soho House Toronto may receive:

- members;
- member-sponsored guests;
- event participants;
- private-event attendees;
- and other authorized users.

Private-event material establishes that non-member commercial access can exist without turning the House into an open public venue. [S7][S8]

Therefore:

```text
Non-Member Admission
!=
General Public Access
```

## Capacity and area restrictions

House Rules permit access restrictions to particular areas during busy periods. [S3]

This creates a second decision after basic admission:

```text
Admitted to House
!=
Admitted to Every Space
```

The public evidence does not establish how Toronto staff communicate or recover from a member being admitted to the House but denied a preferred area.

## Privacy and visit records

The privacy framework describes visit-related data alongside membership, reservation and payment information. [S9]

Arrival therefore creates not only a physical threshold but also a recorded institutional state.

## Accessibility gap

The current evidence set does not provide enough Toronto-specific detail to establish the accessibility pathway through the physical threshold.

No positive claim about step-free arrival, staff assistance or accessibility fulfilment should be made from the current sources.

## Unresolved questions

The evidence does not establish:

- the exact Toronto reception sequence;
- credential-failure fallback;
- how private-event attendees are identified;
- how guest exceptions are handled;
- how capacity denials are explained or recovered;
- or the accessible route through the physical threshold.

## Rating

**Partial.**

Identity and sponsorship requirements are documented, but the full Toronto arrival, exception and accessibility process remains unverified.
