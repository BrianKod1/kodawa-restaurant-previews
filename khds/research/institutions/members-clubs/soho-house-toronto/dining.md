# Dining

## Research question

How does membership affect dining access, payment and responsibility at Soho House Toronto?

## Dining is embedded inside membership

The Toronto House publicly describes a members-only restaurant. [S1]

Dining is therefore not simply a standalone restaurant transaction.

Ordinary participation depends first on the membership or guest relationship:

```text
Dining Interest
+
Valid Access State
→
Potential Dining Participation
```

This is different from an open public restaurant where discovery and table allocation may be the primary thresholds.

## Access does not prove table availability

Membership or valid guest access does not establish that a table, preferred time or preferred dining area is available.

House Rules permit area restrictions during busy periods. [S3]

The current evidence set does not establish Toronto-specific:

- reservation windows;
- walk-in rules;
- waitlists;
- table-allocation priorities;
- cancellation rules;
- or dining recovery when capacity is unavailable.

Therefore:

```text
Member Access
!=
Guaranteed Dining Capacity
```

## Guest payment responsibility

House Rules make members responsible for guest bills. [S3]

This is a distinctive governance feature because hospitality consumption by a guest can create a financial obligation for the sponsoring member.

```text
Guest Consumption
→
Potential Member Financial Responsibility
```

The evidence does not establish every Toronto payment workflow or dispute process.

## Dietary and allergen evidence gap

The current Case #27 evidence ledger does not contain sufficiently detailed Toronto-specific dietary or allergen procedures.

No conclusion should be drawn about:

- allergen controls;
- dietary accommodation;
- kitchen segregation;
- religious dietary requirements;
- or successful accommodation.

## Dining as social participation

The restaurant operates inside a broader members' institution that also includes events, art and social use. [S1][S11]

This means dining may function as both consumption and participation in the House environment.

That does not establish social outcome, belonging or satisfaction.

## Rating

**Partial.**

The members-only dining model and guest-bill responsibility are documented, but table allocation, dietary handling, exception recovery and dining outcomes remain incomplete.
