# Reservation / Membership / Participation

## Research question

How are membership, event, dining and other participation rights allocated or booked?

## Reservation is not one transaction

For Soho House Toronto, participation cannot be reduced to one reservation mechanism.

The system includes at least:

- membership application and approval;
- continuing membership entitlement;
- member-sponsored guest access;
- event booking;
- other app-supported member bookings;
- dining or space access;
- and private-event hiring.

The first important distinction is:

```text
Membership Approval
!=
Specific Service Reservation
```

A person may hold membership while still needing separate capacity, booking or area access for a particular experience.

## Membership application

House Rules and membership material describe membership as an application-based relationship rather than immediate admission. [S3][S4]

This introduces a governance stage before ordinary participation:

```text
Interest
→
Application
→
Institutional Decision
→
Membership Status
→
Individual Visit or Booking
```

The public record does not establish how individual applications are evaluated in practice.

## Continuing entitlement

Membership creates an ongoing relationship but does not establish unlimited live capacity.

House Rules allow access to areas to be restricted during busy periods. [S3]

Therefore:

```text
Valid Membership
!=
Guaranteed Access to Every Space
```

## Digital booking layer

The Soho House app supports member booking functions, including events and other services. [S5]

This creates a second entitlement layer:

```text
Membership Entitlement
+
Service-Specific Inventory
=
Potential Participation
```

The evidence does not establish that every service uses the same booking rules or inventory model.

## Guest sponsorship

Guest access operates through the member relationship.

House Rules make the sponsoring member responsible for guests and establish that ordinary guests do not simply hold an independent equivalent membership entitlement. [S3]

Therefore:

```text
Sponsored Guest
!=
Independent Member
```

## Private-event route

Toronto event-space evidence shows a separate route through which spaces can be used for private events. [S7][S8]

This matters because a private members' institution can contain non-member commercial participation without making ordinary House access public.

```text
Non-Member Event Access
!=
General Public House Access
```

## Unresolved allocation questions

Public evidence does not yet establish:

- Toronto dining reservation rules;
- waitlist behaviour;
- priority among membership types;
- live capacity allocation;
- how sold-out events are recovered;
- how private events affect normal member capacity;
- or whether denied access to one area triggers a meaningful alternative.

## Rating

**Partial.**

Membership and multiple allocation routes are documented, but live inventory, priority, exception handling and recovery remain unverified.
