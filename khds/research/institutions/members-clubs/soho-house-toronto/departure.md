# Departure

## Research question

When does responsibility end after a member or guest leaves Soho House Toronto?

## Physical departure does not end membership

Unlike a one-time venue visit, leaving the House does not normally end the underlying membership relationship.

Membership, account, renewal and future-access relationships continue beyond a single visit. [S3][S6]

Therefore:

```text
Physical Departure
!=
Membership Relationship End
```

## Financial closure

House Rules require bills to be settled and make members responsible for guest bills. [S3]

This means visit-level departure may include a financial closure requirement even while the broader institutional relationship continues.

The current evidence does not establish all Toronto-specific:

- payment-dispute procedures;
- delayed-charge handling;
- failed-payment recovery;
- or post-visit billing escalation.

## Conduct can outlive the visit

House Rules provide an institutional governance framework that includes possible restriction, suspension or termination of membership. [S3]

A conduct issue arising during one visit may therefore affect later access.

```text
Visit Ends
!=
Governance Consequence Ends
```

The public evidence does not establish how frequently or consistently those powers are used.

## Data relationship after departure

The privacy notice covers membership, visit, reservation, purchase, guest and other data categories. [S9]

This means some institutional records persist after the person physically leaves.

Published retention or processing rules do not establish individual privacy outcomes.

## Post-visit recovery gap

The current evidence set does not sufficiently establish Toronto-specific:

- lost-property procedures;
- complaint escalation;
- billing-dispute resolution;
- guest-related post-visit responsibility;
- or service-recovery timelines.

## Member versus guest closure

A guest may physically depart while the sponsoring member's continuing relationship remains active.

This creates an unresolved responsibility boundary:

```text
Guest Departure
!=
Automatic End of Member Responsibility
```

The exact post-visit transfer point is not established by the current sources.

## Rating

**Partial.**

Continuing membership, payment responsibility and persistent governance/data relationships are documented, but Toronto-specific post-visit recovery and responsibility transfer remain incomplete.
