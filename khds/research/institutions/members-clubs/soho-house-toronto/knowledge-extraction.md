## Purpose

This extraction asks what Soho House Toronto contributes to KHDS once the twelve required dimensions are read together.

The case is useful because it shifts hospitality from a one-time reservation or admission model into a continuing membership relationship.

The central issue is not whether membership exists.

It is whether identity, entitlement, guest sponsorship, conduct, privacy, access and recovery remain legible across repeated visits and different participation states.

## Core question

> How does a hospitality institution preserve legible access, responsibility, privacy and recovery when participation depends on continuing membership, guest sponsorship and behavioural rules?

## Core institutional mechanism

### Membership-Governed Hospitality Continuity

A continuing relationship in which access is mediated through membership status, credentials, guest sponsorship, service-specific capacity and behavioural rules rather than one universal admission event.

The mechanism can be expressed as:

```text
Membership Status
+
Recognizable Identity
+
Current Access Rule
+
Service-Specific Capacity
+
Behavioural Compliance
=
Potential Participation
```

None of those elements alone proves successful hospitality fulfilment.

## Institution-comparison unit

### Membership-Gated / Recurring-Entitlement Hospitality

Case #27 adds a hospitality form in which:

- the guest relationship may persist across many visits;
- admission can depend on continuing membership rather than a one-time booking;
- guests can derive access through sponsorship;
- access may vary by area, time, capacity or activity;
- conduct rules can affect future participation;
- and physical departure does not end the institutional relationship.

This comparison unit is provisional and not a formal pattern.

## Cross-dimensional extraction

### Identity

Membership identity must be legible digitally or physically at arrival.

```text
Membership Record
!=
Automatically Legible Admission Identity
```

### Entitlement

Membership creates a continuing entitlement relationship but not unrestricted access.

```text
Valid Membership
!=
Guaranteed Access to Every Space
```

### Guest sponsorship

A sponsored guest does not hold an equivalent independent member state.

```text
Member Guest
!=
Independent Member
```

### Capacity

House access, area access, dining access and event access can be distinct capacity objects.

```text
Institution Access
!=
Service-Specific Capacity
```

### Responsibility

The member may carry responsibility for guest conduct and bills, but that does not eliminate operator responsibility for rules, access decisions, service delivery or recovery.

```text
Guest Responsibility
!=
Operator Responsibility Absent
```

### Privacy

Privacy is both a behavioural rule and a data-governance issue.

Published privacy rules do not establish privacy outcomes.

### Recovery

The current public evidence describes rules and authority more clearly than it describes failure recovery.

Credential failure, area denial, booking conflict, payment dispute, accessibility failure and post-visit recovery remain incompletely evidenced.

## Strongest finding

> In membership-based hospitality, entitlement is persistent but access is conditional.

The strongest bounded relationship is:

```text
Membership Continuity
!=
Uniform Access Continuity
```

A member may retain the relationship while access to a particular space, time, activity or service changes.

## Discovery

Discovery is partly discovery of an institution and partly discovery of a governance relationship.

The user must distinguish membership, sponsored guest access, events and private hire.

### Pressure

**Access-Route Legibility**

Would strengthen if users can reliably distinguish which participation route applies before commitment.

Would weaken if the institution's routes are already operationally equivalent or universally interchangeable.

## Reservation

Membership approval and service reservation are separate allocation events.

### Pressure

**Recurring Entitlement / Local Inventory Separation**

Would strengthen if repeated cases show that continuing status must still be translated into service-specific capacity.

Would weaken if membership consistently creates unrestricted or effectively unlimited access.

## Pre-arrival

Preparation includes role, sponsorship, credentials, behavioural rules and changing House-specific conditions.

### Pressure

**Role-Dependent Preparation**

Would strengthen if member, guest and event-attendee preparation repeatedly requires distinct instructions.

## Arrival

Arrival converts a continuing relationship into a current physical access state.

### Pressure

**Identity-to-Access Conversion**

Would strengthen if membership-based institutions repeatedly require a distinct threshold check before physical participation.

## Stay / participation

The visit is governed by membership rules, guest responsibility, area-specific conditions and privacy expectations.

### Pressure

**Rule-Governed Participation**

Published rules are visible; operational enforcement remains unverified.

## Dining

Dining is embedded inside the membership relationship.

### Pressure

**Access Before Consumption**

Dining entitlement may depend on both membership state and table/service capacity.

## Departure

Physical departure ends the visit but not necessarily membership, data, billing or conduct consequences.

### Pressure

**Persistent Relationship After Visit**

Would strengthen if post-visit obligations and future-access consequences recur across membership institutions.

## Atmosphere

Atmosphere is produced partly through explicit restrictions on behaviour, devices, privacy and use of space.

### Pressure

**Atmosphere as Governed Participation**

Would weaken if atmosphere proves independent of these rules or if rules are not materially connected to use.

## Photography

Visual evidence can document design but not access, belonging, privacy or enforcement.

### Pressure

**Visible Place / Invisible Governance Boundary**

## Story

The narrative is organized around recurring membership and creative community.

### Pressure

**Community Narrative / Outcome Separation**

The operator's story is documented; belonging and community outcomes are not.

## Falsification requirements

The Case #27 directions should weaken if future evidence shows that:

1. membership status automatically produces unrestricted practical access;
2. guest sponsorship creates no meaningful dependency;
3. area, dining and event capacity are operationally identical to general House access;
4. behavioural rules do not materially affect participation;
5. published privacy rules reliably prove privacy outcomes without further evidence;
6. physical departure reliably ends all meaningful institutional responsibility;
7. repeated membership institutions do not show the same identity, entitlement or responsibility pressures;
8. or existing KHDS architecture already explains these observations without a new comparison architecture.
