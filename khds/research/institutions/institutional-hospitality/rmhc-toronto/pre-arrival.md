# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a family resolve before physically arriving when House eligibility can be approved without a guaranteed room, adult guests require clearance, communicable illness can block admission and Family Room services vary by hospital and unit?

## Approval does not eliminate accommodation uncertainty

Families are told to make alternate accommodation arrangements because a room cannot be guaranteed for the requested arrival date. [S1]

Therefore:

Eligibility / Wait-List Approval != Arrival Accommodation Certainty.

## Families are encouraged to check availability shortly before need

Families are encouraged to contact Family Services one day before the referral request date to check room availability and remain on the wait list until a room becomes available. [S1]

Therefore:

Pre-Arrival Plan != Fixed Room Assignment.

## Daily reconfirmation is not required

RMHC Toronto states that families do not need to call daily while remaining on the wait list. [S1]

Therefore:

Wait-List Membership != Continuous Guest Reconfirmation.

## Adult clearance is a pre-arrival dependency

After referral, families receive links for criminal background checks; all adult guests must complete the required checks. [S1]

Therefore:

Child Treatment State != Adult Hospitality Readiness.

## Communicable illness can block admission

Anyone suffering from or recently exposed to a communicable illness cannot be admitted. [S1]

Therefore:

Room Availability + Eligibility != Admission If Health-Safety Condition Fails.

## Family composition matters

Siblings and other family members may accompany the parents, while parents under 18 must be accompanied by a legal guardian for the entire stay. [S1]

Therefore:

Eligible Child != Fixed Adult-Only Hospitality Party.

And:

Minor Parent Role != Independent Adult Guest Role.

## One bedroom per family shapes preparation

The House provides one bedroom per family. [S1]

Therefore:

Family Composition != Unlimited Room Allocation.

## Family Room access must be checked locally

Families are directed to check the specific Family Room page or inquire within the hospital unit because eligibility varies by partner hospital and unit. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Hospital Destination Known != Family Room Eligibility Known Automatically.

## Family Room hours and services can vary

RMHC Toronto warns that Family Room hours may vary and asks families to check with the local Family Room team for current hours and services. [S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Family Room Exists != Every Service Available at Every Time.

## Sleep-space planning remains uncertain

Some Family Room sleep rooms are assigned by hospital staff and some pods operate first-come, first-served. [S3]

Therefore:

Need for Overnight Respite != Pre-Reserved Sleep Space.

## House proximity reduces travel burden but does not replace hospital state

The House is located close to SickKids, Mount Sinai and other downtown hospitals. [S2]

Therefore:

Hospitality Proximity != Clinical Care Integration.

## Strongest pre-arrival boundaries

1. eligibility versus accommodation certainty;
2. pre-arrival plan versus fixed room assignment;
3. wait-list membership versus daily reconfirmation;
4. child treatment state versus adult hospitality readiness;
5. eligibility plus room availability versus admission after health-safety failure;
6. eligible child versus fixed hospitality party;
7. family composition versus unlimited room allocation;
8. hospital destination versus Family Room eligibility;
9. Family Room existence versus all-time service availability;
10. need for respite versus reserved sleep space;
11. proximity versus clinical integration.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish exact wait-list communication cadence, how last-minute room offers are delivered, arrival deadlines after a room offer, health-screening procedure at arrival, accessibility planning, language-support arrangements or transport assistance.

## Next evidence

Inspect Arrival to determine when a family becomes a House guest or Family Room user, who confirms access, how local capacity is assigned and where clinical and non-clinical responsibility separate.
