# Stay / Participation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does an RMHC Toronto stay function when the family's medical journey continues to change while accommodation, school, meals, laundry, wellness, activities and caregiver respite operate as separate but related non-clinical services?

## The House is structured as a temporary family home, not only a bedroom

The House provides private family bedrooms plus shared kitchens, dining areas, laundry, family rooms, a library, media room, business centre and activity spaces. [S11][S14]

Therefore:

House Stay != Bedroom Occupancy Only.

The hospitality relationship includes daily living infrastructure.

## Families retain household agency

The House kitchen is available 24/7. Families receive dry-goods storage and refrigerated space, while pantry and common-fridge items are also available. [S14]

Therefore:

House Accommodation != Total Dependence on Catered Meals.

Families can continue parts of ordinary household life while away from home.

## Laundry is continuous infrastructure

Laundry facilities are available 24/7 without charge and detergent is provided. [S14]

Therefore:

Long Stay != Repeated External Laundry Dependency.

This supports continuity for extended medical journeys.

## The institution supports family roles beyond caregiving

RMHC Toronto offers more than 20 programs including activities, wellness programs, child-care loan items, camps and school. [S11]

Therefore:

Caregiver Role != Only Role Supported During Stay.

Parents and children can occupy educational, recreational and household roles alongside the medical journey.

## Education can become part of the stay state

RMHC Toronto School serves Junior Kindergarten through Grade 12 students staying at the House and offers rolling admission. [S11][S12]

Patients and siblings can participate, subject to school registration requirements. [S12][S15]

Therefore:

House Guest != School Student Automatically.

And:

Medical Stay != Automatic Education Interruption.

Education becomes a separate service state inside the hospitality relationship.

## School participation can adapt to treatment state

The school offers full-day learning plus EASEL sessions for students unable to participate in a full day; EASEL may occur in person or virtually. [S12][S13]

Therefore:

Student Enrolled != Fixed Attendance Mode.

Education can change as health, energy and treatment schedules change.

## The House can support different family configurations

First-party family stories document situations where the entire family stayed together, while other families split between Toronto and home to preserve schooling or other household continuity. [S16][S17]

Therefore:

One Child's Medical Journey != One Fixed Family Co-Location Pattern.

These stories are illustrative, not representative prevalence evidence.

## Treatment state can change while the House relationship continues

A first-party family story describes a child becoming semi-discharged from hospital on the condition that she remain close at RMHC Toronto. [S17]

Therefore:

Hospital Inpatient State != Only Clinical State Compatible With House Stay.

This is illustrative evidence that the medical state can change without ending the hospitality relationship.

## Family Rooms support the bedside-adjacent stay differently

Family Rooms offer rest, meals, showers, laundry and quiet space inside hospitals, close to the child's care. [S19][S20]

Therefore:

House Stay != Only RMHC Hospitality Mode During Treatment.

The family may use a hospital-based respite relationship in addition to or instead of House accommodation.

## Clinical responsibility remains separate

RMHC Toronto describes Family Rooms as non-clinical and its House as family support close to care rather than a provider of treatment. [S19]

Therefore:

Extended Hospitality Stay != RMHC Clinical Care.

## Strongest stay boundaries

1. House stay versus bedroom occupancy only;
2. accommodation versus total catered-meal dependence;
3. caregiver role versus only role supported;
4. House guest versus school student automatically;
5. medical stay versus automatic education interruption;
6. student enrolment versus fixed attendance mode;
7. one medical journey versus one family co-location pattern;
8. hospital inpatient state versus only state compatible with House stay;
9. House stay versus only RMHC hospitality mode;
10. extended hospitality stay versus clinical care.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that RMHC Toronto provides ordinary family support services around temporary accommodation.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance strengthens if these services must remain coordinated as treatment, family composition, education and caregiver needs change.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- exact maximum House stay duration;
- daily occupancy by family type;
- service-utilization rates by program;
- whether school, meal and Family Room records share a family identifier;
- how hospital discharge or transfer is propagated to House operations;
- case-management workload;
- family-level service orchestration technology.

## Next evidence

Inspect Dining and Departure to determine how food support and family routines continue during treatment and how hospitality, school and Family Room relationships close or reset when appointments, treatment status or location change.
