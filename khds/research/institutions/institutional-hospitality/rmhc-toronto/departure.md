# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What ends when a family's immediate treatment phase changes, and which House, Family Room, school, referral and support relationships must close, transfer or restart?

## Treatment scheduling can change expected arrival before the stay begins

RMHC Toronto's FAQ states that if surgery or an appointment is cancelled, the family must contact the social worker and RMHC Toronto requires confirmation of the new date before changing the expected arrival date. [S13]

Therefore:

Medical Schedule Change != Hospitality Date Change Automatically.

A clinical change requires a cross-institution administrative handoff.

## Repeat House stays require renewed authorization

RMHC Toronto states that a new referral is required for every House visit. [S1]

Therefore:

Prior House Stay != Persistent Future Accommodation Entitlement.

The hospitality relationship can close and later reopen through a new referral.

## Clinical discharge can change capacity for other families

RMHC Toronto explains that House wait-list priority changes as children are discharged from hospital. [S2][S14]

Therefore:

One Family's Clinical Transition Can Change Another Family's Hospitality Opportunity.

This is direct evidence that clinical state affects accommodation allocation at system level.

## Hospital discharge does not always mean immediate departure from RMHC

A first-party family story describes a child becoming semi-discharged while remaining close at RMHC Toronto as part of the continuing medical plan. [S17]

Therefore:

Hospital Discharge State != House Departure Automatically.

This is illustrative evidence and should not be generalized to all families.

## School departure has an explicit continuity handoff

When a student leaves RMHC Toronto School, school staff send a short-term report, Ontario Report Card or summary of accomplishments to the student's hometown school, depending on grade and length of stay. [S13]

Therefore:

RMHC School Departure != Education Record Closure Without Handoff.

The education relationship includes a continuity step back to the home institution.

## School enrollment can therefore end differently from House occupancy

School registration is its own process and learning mode can vary throughout the stay. [S12][S13]

Therefore:

House Departure State != School State in Every Moment.

The two relationships are connected but not identical.

## Family Room eligibility is tied to hospital treatment context

Family Room access depends on the child being treated in specified participating hospital units. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Clinical Unit Change Can Imply Family Room Eligibility Change.

This is an architecture inference from published eligibility rules; the public evidence does not establish the operational handoff procedure.

## Family Room departure can precede overall medical-journey closure

A family can stop using one Family Room because the child moves units, is discharged or changes hospital while the broader medical journey continues. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Family Room Relationship End != Medical Journey End.

This is a bounded inference from unit-specific eligibility.

## Returning families can re-enter the system

First-party family stories describe families using RMHC Toronto repeatedly across multi-stage treatment journeys. [S17]

Combined with the published rule requiring a referral for each House visit: [S1]

Therefore:

Medical Journey Continuity != Continuous Single Hospitality Stay.

A longitudinal care journey can contain multiple separate hospitality episodes.

## Physical departure does not erase institutional continuity

School records can be transferred to the hometown school, and families may later return through a new referral. [S1][S13]

Therefore:

Physical House Departure != End of Every RMHC-Related Continuity State.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. medical schedule change versus automatic hospitality-date change;
2. prior House stay versus persistent future accommodation entitlement;
3. one family's clinical transition versus another family's capacity opportunity;
4. hospital discharge versus House departure automatically;
5. school departure versus education-record closure;
6. House state versus school state;
7. Family Room relationship end versus medical-journey end;
8. medical journey continuity versus one continuous hospitality stay;
9. physical departure versus end of all continuity states.

## Multi-owner handoff pressure

Departure exposes at least three institutional owners:

- hospital/social worker for treatment and referral confirmation;
- RMHC Toronto for House and Family Room hospitality;
- hometown school / RMHC Toronto School for educational continuity. [S1][S13]

Therefore:

One Family Transition != One Institutional Owner.

This is directional pressure toward Multi-Owner Recovery / Responsibility Continuity.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that separate organizations naturally need separate discharge and referral processes.

That remains plausible.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that failures at those handoffs materially disrupt family continuity.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- formal House checkout rules;
- exact required departure time;
- how rapidly discharge information reaches RMHC Toronto;
- whether room release is automated or manual;
- discharge-to-wait-list turnover time;
- Family Room exit procedures;
- transfer handling between hospital units;
- post-stay family follow-up;
- failed school-record handoff rates.
