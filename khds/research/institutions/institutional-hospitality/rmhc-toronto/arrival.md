# Arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What changes when a family physically reaches the House or a hospital Family Room, and how do room availability, local allocation, unit eligibility, health-safety rules and patient/family roles determine whether hospitality service actually begins?

## Physical arrival is not sufficient for House admission

House eligibility requires the referral/application process, adult background checks and room availability. Communicable illness can also block admission. [S1]

Therefore:

Physical Presence at House != Admitted House Guest.

## A wait-listed family may arrive in Toronto without a House room

Because rooms cannot be booked in advance and availability is not guaranteed, families are told to maintain alternate accommodation plans. [S1]

Therefore:

Arrival in Toronto != Arrival Into RMHC Accommodation.

## House admission remains distinct from hospital admission

A child can be receiving eligible treatment while the family remains waiting for House accommodation. [S1][S2]

Therefore:

Hospital Admission != House Admission.

## Room type is a local allocation state

The House has standard family bedrooms and apartment suites, with apartment suites assigned on a priority basis. [S2]

Therefore:

House Admission != Any Room Type Entitlement.

## Family Room arrival occurs inside the clinical institution

Family Rooms are located inside partner hospitals, close to where the child receives care. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Hospital Arrival Can Precede Hospitality Arrival by Only a Local Handoff.

## Family Room access is unit-specific

At SickKids, Family Rooms are open to family members of children in specified intensive-care units plus certain other qualifying units. Other sites publish their own unit-level eligibility. [S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Hospital Visitor != Family Room Guest Automatically.

## The patient is not the Family Room guest

RMHC Toronto describes Family Rooms as clinical-free spaces and asks that patients not use them. [S3]

Therefore:

Child Receiving Care != Family Room User.

## Family Room sleep-space activation can require another handoff

Sleep rooms are assigned by hospital staff, while some sleeping pods are first-come, first-served. [S3]

Therefore:

Family Room Admitted != Sleep Space Assigned.

And:

Hospitality Access != Overnight Respite Capacity Guaranteed.

## Local sites expose different arrival states

SickKids Family Rooms are open 24 hours; Credit Valley has unit-specific access and overnight sleep spaces; Markham Stouffville has a 24-hour napping area while staffed hours may vary; Scarborough sites have unit-specific rooms and overnight sleep options. [S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

Family Room Arrival != Uniform Service State Across Hospitals.

## Arrival activates non-clinical support without changing clinical responsibility

Family Rooms provide rest, snacks, showers, laundry and respite but are explicitly non-clinical. [S3]

Therefore:

Hospitality Service Begins != RMHC Assumes Clinical Responsibility.

## Family role determines experience after arrival

A single medical case can involve patient, parent/legal guardian, sibling, other accompanying family member and minor parent with legal guardian. [S1][S3]

Therefore:

One Medical Case != One Arrival Role.

## Strongest arrival boundaries

1. physical presence versus House admission;
2. arrival in Toronto versus arrival into RMHC accommodation;
3. hospital admission versus House admission;
4. House admission versus room-type entitlement;
5. hospital visitor versus Family Room guest;
6. patient versus Family Room user;
7. Family Room admission versus sleep-space assignment;
8. hospitality access versus guaranteed overnight respite capacity;
9. Family Room arrival versus uniform service state;
10. hospitality service versus clinical responsibility;
11. one medical case versus one arrival role.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish House check-in hours, identity verification at physical check-in, exact room-allocation logic beyond published priorities, how hospital discharge affects an active House stay, Family Room occupancy limits, queue handling at first-come sleep pods or escalation when a family arrives but cannot be accommodated.
