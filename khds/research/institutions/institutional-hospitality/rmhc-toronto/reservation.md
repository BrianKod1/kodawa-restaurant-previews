# Reservation / Eligibility

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How does a family move from medical need to hospitality eligibility when House access requires referral, application, background checks and wait-list prioritization, while Family Room sleep space can be locally assigned or first-come, first-served?

## House access begins with qualifying context, not open reservation

RMHC Toronto accepts referrals from specific qualifying hospitals and directs families to their Toronto hospital social worker. [S1]

Therefore:

Hospitality Need != Direct Self-Booking.

## Referral is necessary but not sufficient

The House path includes hospital social-worker referral, application, criminal background checks for adult guests, wait-list confirmation and room availability. [S1]

Therefore:

Referral Received != House Room Secured.

## Adult guests have an additional admission requirement

All adult guests must complete a criminal background check. [S1]

Therefore:

Family Eligible in Principle != Every Adult Guest Cleared Automatically.

## The House cannot be booked in advance

If a room is unavailable on the approved arrival date, the family is placed on a wait list. [S1]

Therefore:

Approved Arrival Need != Reserved Room.

## Wait-list priority is care-state sensitive

RMHC Toronto says the wait list is prioritized first by inpatient care, then outpatient care, then clinic visit, and then by distance with families living furthest away receiving higher priority. [S2]

Therefore:

Wait-List Position != Simple First-Come Order.

## Wait-list position can change

The priority list may change daily as new families are referred or children are discharged. [S2]

Therefore:

Current Priority != Fixed Future Priority.

## Repeat use requires a new referral

Referrals are required for every House visit. [S1]

Therefore:

Previous House Guest != Automatically Eligible for Future Stay.

## One bedroom is assigned per family

The House offers one bedroom per family. [S1]

Therefore:

Family Allocation != Individual Room Allocation.

## Specialized suites are separately prioritized

The House has 66 standard bedrooms and 15 apartment suites, with apartment suites assigned on a priority basis. [S2]

Therefore:

House Admission != Any Room Type Entitlement.

## Family Room access is not the same reservation process

Family Rooms are available only to specified hospital units or other locally qualifying families. [S3][S4][S5][S6][S7]

Therefore:

House Referral Process != Family Room Access Process.

## Family Room sleep space uses multiple allocation models

There is no general reservation system for Family Room sleep rooms and pods. Sleep rooms are assigned by hospital staff, while sleep pods at specified hospitals operate first-come, first-served. [S3]

Therefore:

Family Room Access != Sleep-Space Reservation.

And:

Eligible Family Room User != Sleep Space Assigned Automatically.

## Hospital staff can become hospitality allocators

Where hospital staff assign sleep rooms, hospital staff participate in access to a non-clinical hospitality resource. [S3]

Therefore:

Hospital Role != Clinical Care Only.

This creates cross-institution responsibility pressure without proving shared architecture.

## Payment occurs after eligibility

House families are asked to contribute $15 per night, but eligibility is defined by distance, child treatment, family role and referral. [S1]

Therefore:

Payment != Admission Qualification.

## Strongest reservation / eligibility boundaries

1. hospitality need versus direct self-booking;
2. referral versus secured room;
3. family eligibility versus individual adult clearance;
4. approved arrival need versus reserved room;
5. wait-list position versus first-come order;
6. current priority versus fixed future priority;
7. previous guest versus future eligibility;
8. family allocation versus individual room allocation;
9. House admission versus room-type entitlement;
10. House referral process versus Family Room access;
11. Family Room access versus sleep-space reservation;
12. payment versus admission qualification.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish referral turnaround time, background-check turnaround time, current average wait-list duration, how priority ties are resolved, how room assignment considers accessibility or family composition, or shared data permissions between hospital and RMHC Toronto.
