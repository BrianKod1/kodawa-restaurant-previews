# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What ends when a Yas Island visitor checks out or leaves the destination, and which park-ticket, transport, support, refund, payment, account or complaint relationships can remain active afterward?

## Hotel checkout and attraction entitlement can share a time boundary

Stay & Play general admission park tickets are valid only during the booked stay dates. [S25]

Therefore:

Hotel Stay Period
Can Bound
Attraction Entitlement Period.

But:

Hotel Checkout != Every Destination Relationship Closed.

Other support and account relationships can persist.

## Premium access is also time-bounded to the stay

Premium Stay & Play states that the Premium Pass is valid only during the hotel stay and cannot be transferred or shared. [S23]

Therefore:

Premium Entitlement != Persistent Post-Stay Benefit.

The hotel dates participate directly in attraction entitlement.

## Departure can expire unused service rights

Because included park admission is constrained to the stay period, an unused ticket can lose practical value when the stay ends. [S23][S25]

Therefore:

Purchased/Included Entitlement != Indefinite Redeemability.

Time-bound package design can convert physical departure into entitlement expiration.

## Local problem resolution comes first

Yas Island's general terms recommend contacting the relevant hotel or attraction directly when an issue occurs during the stay. [S15]

Therefore:

Service Failure != Destination-Level Recovery Owner Automatically.

The first recovery owner is often the local institution.

## Destination support remains available after the stay

If the local issue is not resolved, Yas Island support can be contacted during the stay or within two weeks after the booking dates. [S15]

Therefore:

Physical Departure != Destination Support Closure.

This is one of the strongest Case #43 departure boundaries.

## Booking disputes can outlive the trip

The terms provide a dispute process and establish that complaints/feedback can continue after the booking dates. [S15]

Therefore:

Service Consumption Complete != Commercial Relationship Closed.

The destination-level contract can persist after local service delivery.

## Provider cancellation can create a destination-level refund

If Yas Island completely cancels a booking, the general terms state that a full refund is issued, while associated expenses such as travel, insurance or visa costs remain outside that refund responsibility. [S15]

Therefore:

Destination Booking Refund != Whole-Journey Financial Recovery.

External travel loss can remain with the guest.

## External trip costs remain outside destination ownership

The booking terms place travel arrangements, travel insurance and visa responsibility on the guest. [S15]

Therefore:

Destination Package Completion/Failure != End-to-End Travel Responsibility.

The destination contract does not absorb every journey cost.

## FacePass/FacePay can be disabled after use

The Yas Island mobile terms allow a user to withdraw consent or disable FacePass/FacePay and review purchase history in the account. [S26]

Therefore:

Physical Visit Ends != Digital Identity/Payment Relationship Ends Automatically.

The account state can persist beyond the visit.

## Transport departure remains independently stateful

Airport/Dubai shuttle services remain first-come, first-served and timetable-dependent. [S5][S22]

Therefore:

Hotel Checkout != Guaranteed Outbound Shuttle Capacity.

A guest can finish the hotel relationship while still depending on a separate transport service.

## Multi-Park entitlement can outlive one local service but not its window

Multi-Park tickets operate across a defined six-calendar-day period after first use. [S4]

Therefore:

Exit From One Park != Destination Attraction Relationship Closed.

But:

Multi-Park State != Indefinite Post-Visit Entitlement.

## One destination journey can close at different times

Potential closure moments include:

- hotel checkout;
- final included park visit;
- Premium Pass expiry;
- final shuttle use;
- final dining voucher;
- end of Multi-Park window;
- end of destination-support feedback window;
- closure of digital identity/payment state. [S4][S15][S19][S23][S25][S26]

Therefore:

One Destination Journey != One Closure Time.

## Multi-owner recovery

Public terms expose at least three recovery layers:

1. local hotel/attraction;
2. Yas Island destination support;
3. external travel/insurance/payment actors. [S15]

Therefore:

One Guest Problem != One Recovery Owner.

This is direct Case #43 pressure toward Multi-Owner Recovery.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Departure and recovery can affect:

- unused included entitlements;
- cancellation refunds;
- associated travel losses;
- support cost;
- transport reliance;
- repeat value. [S15][S23][S25]

The evidence does not establish:

- unused ticket breakage;
- refund rate;
- support cost;
- recovery compensation;
- repeat-purchase impact.

Physical Departure != Economic Endpoint.

## Strongest departure boundaries

1. hotel stay period versus attraction entitlement period;
2. hotel checkout versus destination-relationship closure;
3. included entitlement versus indefinite redeemability;
4. service failure versus destination-level first-line ownership;
5. physical departure versus destination support closure;
6. destination refund versus whole-journey financial recovery;
7. destination package versus end-to-end travel responsibility;
8. physical visit versus digital identity/payment closure;
9. hotel checkout versus guaranteed outbound transport;
10. exit from one attraction versus destination-attraction closure;
11. one destination journey versus one closure time;
12. one guest problem versus one recovery owner.

## Competing explanation

A simpler explanation is that normal package travel separates hotel, attraction, transport and after-sales responsibilities.

That remains plausible.

The KHDS relevance becomes stronger if destination branding and bundling create a continuity expectation that crosses those otherwise separate recovery domains.

## Evidence limitations

The public record does not establish:

- actual checkout timing by hotel;
- missed final-park-use recovery;
- shuttle denied-boarding recovery;
- complaint resolution rates;
- refund processing time;
- cross-operator compensation;
- digital account retention outcomes;
- destination-wide service-recovery ownership.
