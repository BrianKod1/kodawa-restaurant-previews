# Pre-arrival

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What must a passenger resolve before reaching a Changi hospitality service, including flight status, terminal, itinerary, baggage, immigration, accessibility, onward boarding pass and service eligibility?

## Pre-arrival is state preparation

For Case #40, successful pre-arrival does not mean only knowing:

- flight number;
- departure time;
- terminal.

A traveller may also need to understand:

- whether they are arriving, departing or transiting;
- whether baggage is through-checked;
- whether an onward boarding pass is available;
- whether immigration must be cleared;
- whether immigration must not be cleared;
- which terminal is relevant;
- whether a hospitality service is public-side or transit-side;
- whether assistance requires airline coordination. [S2][S3][S4][S7]

Flight Known != Hospitality Journey Ready.

## Transit preparation

The transit guide connects normal transfer status to:

- same-itinerary conditions;
- through-checked baggage;
- onward boarding arrangements. [S2]

This means:

Connecting Flight Confirmed != Transfer Process Fully Resolved.

A traveller can possess a valid onward flight while still needing to solve:

- baggage collection;
- immigration;
- check-in;
- transfer-desk activity.

## Immigration-state preparation

Case #40 contains an unusual but important contradiction.

A passenger seeking Jewel may need to clear arrival immigration. [S3]

A passenger seeking an airside transit hotel may need to avoid clearing immigration. [S4]

Therefore:

Hospitality Choice Can Change Required Immigration State.

This makes pre-arrival decision quality especially important.

Wrong Immigration Decision Can Remove Intended Hospitality Access.

The evidence does not establish how often this failure occurs.

## Terminal preparation

Terminal location changes:

- transfer route;
- transport method;
- available hospitality;
- transfer time. [S3]

T1-T3 movement and T4 movement do not operate identically.

Airport Destination Known != Terminal Journey Known.

## Boarding-pass readiness

A transit passenger without an onward boarding pass may need a transfer desk before continuing toward the next flight. [S2]

Onward Flight Exists != Boarding Credential Ready.

This is similar to broader KHDS distinctions between purchased entitlement and usable access credential.

## Baggage preparation

Baggage state can materially affect the passenger journey.

Through-checked baggage can support a normal transit process.

Other baggage conditions may require:

- collection;
- immigration;
- re-check;
- different terminal movement. [S2]

Passenger Owns Baggage != Passenger Controls Its Current Operational State.

## Assistance preparation

The special-assistance guidance creates a distributed preparation model.

Changi provides airport-side accessibility infrastructure and Care Ambassadors. [S7][S8]

But some support should be arranged through the airline before travel. [S7]

Therefore:

Airport Assistance Available != Every Assistance Need Owned by Airport.

The passenger may need to know which organisation must be contacted before arrival.

## Digital preparation

The Changi App can surface:

- flight information;
- saved flights;
- baggage tracking;
- attraction booking;
- dining reservations;
- payments;
- rewards. [S11][S12]

This may improve continuity.

However:

Information Visible in One App != One Responsibility Owner.

The evidence does not establish complete real-time synchronization among all underlying systems.

## Flight disruption preparation

Changi publishes flight information and real-time status. [S14]

For rescheduling or compensation, passengers are directed toward their airline. [S14]

Therefore:

Airport Displays Disruption != Airport Owns Airline Recovery.

This responsibility distinction should be legible before the passenger attempts a hospitality service whose timing depends on the flight.

## Jewel preparation

Jewel hospitality may involve:

- attractions;
- dining;
- YOTELAIR;
- baggage storage;
- early check-in. [S15]

Each service can have its own eligibility or participation conditions.

Jewel Destination != One Service Relationship.

## Early check-in

Jewel early check-in depends on participating airlines and relevant conditions. [S15]

Airport Service Exists != Every Airline Passenger Eligible.

This is another example of service availability being separate from passenger eligibility.

## Free Singapore Tour preparation

The tour requires alignment among:

- transit state;
- flight schedule;
- layover duration;
- immigration requirements;
- entry documentation;
- baggage state;
- registration;
- capacity. [S9]

This creates a high-dependency hospitality experience.

Tour Interest != Tour Readiness.

## Responsibility map

A simplified pre-arrival chain can include:

Airline booking
→ baggage arrangement
→ flight status
→ passenger state
→ immigration requirement
→ terminal
→ hospitality eligibility
→ hospitality booking
→ accessibility preparation.

Different organizations may own different parts of that chain.

One Passenger Plan != One Preparation Owner.

## Competing explanation

This may be normal airport travel preparation.

Airports necessarily require passengers to understand:

- flights;
- baggage;
- terminals;
- security;
- immigration;
- service conditions.

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that failure to coordinate these dependencies materially changes hospitality recovery and responsibility.
