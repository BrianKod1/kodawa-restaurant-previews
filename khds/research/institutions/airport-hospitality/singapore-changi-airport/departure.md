# Departure

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

Where does the airport hospitality relationship end when flight departure, baggage, terminal transfer, disruption, airline recovery, digital tracking and post-service enquiries can continue beyond one physical space?

## Leaving a hospitality service is not airport departure

A passenger can leave:

- lounge;
- hotel;
- restaurant;
- attraction

while still remaining inside the larger airport journey.

The passenger may still need:

- terminal transfer;
- security;
- gate movement;
- baggage state confirmation;
- flight departure. [S2][S3][S12]

Therefore:

Hospitality Service Exit != Airport Journey Completion.

## Gate and security continuation

A connecting traveller can leave a hospitality environment but still need to proceed through the required security and onward-gate process. [S2]

Therefore:

Hospitality Closure != Boarding Readiness.

The airport journey remains open.

## Terminal transfer during departure

A passenger may need to change terminals before reaching the onward departure point. [S3]

Public and transit routes are not identical.

Therefore:

Service Location != Departure Location Automatically.

## Baggage persistence

A passenger can depart from a lounge, hotel or terminal area while baggage remains within airport or airline-controlled handling systems.

The Changi baggage tracker supports departure, arrival and transfer states. [S12]

Therefore:

Passenger Movement != Baggage Movement.

And:

Physical Separation from Baggage != Baggage Relationship Closure.

## Flight disruption

Changi provides flight-status information. [S14]

For rescheduling or compensation matters, passengers are directed to the airline. [S14]

Therefore:

Airport Communicates Flight Status != Airport Owns Airline Recovery.

This responsibility distinction can become important after the passenger has already consumed airport hospitality.

## Air-carrier responsibility

The official Conditions of Use identifies specified air-carrier responsibilities around disruption, passenger welfare, accommodation, re-ticketing and lost-baggage arrangements. [S17]

This source is dated 2017.

It is therefore used only as bounded evidence of declared responsibility structure, not as proof that every present-day operating procedure is unchanged.

## Recovery route differentiation

The airport contact material distinguishes several enquiry routes, including:

- airline contact;
- baggage-related contact;
- airport lost-and-found;
- Jewel enquiry;
- general airport feedback. [S13]

Therefore:

Unresolved Journey Issue != One Post-Departure Recovery Route.

## Digital persistence

The Changi App can continue to expose:

- flight information;
- baggage tracking;
- account-related functions;
- other airport services. [S11][S12]

This means physical movement away from one service does not necessarily end the digital relationship.

Therefore:

Physical Service Exit != Digital Relationship Closure.

## Passenger welfare during disruption

Disruption can alter the traveller's need for:

- food;
- accommodation;
- re-ticketing;
- onward information;
- baggage resolution. [S14][S17]

This is important because hospitality demand can increase after the original travel plan fails.

However:

Disruption Creates Hospitality Need != Airport Owns Every Recovery Obligation.

The public evidence preserves responsibility boundaries.

## Relationship endpoints

Possible Case #40 endpoints include:

- lounge visit ends;
- hotel stay ends;
- dining transaction ends;
- attraction visit ends;
- passenger reaches gate;
- aircraft departs;
- baggage is loaded;
- baggage is delivered;
- disruption is resolved;
- complaint is resolved;
- digital account relationship continues.

These are not necessarily simultaneous.

Therefore:

One Airport Journey != One Universal End Point.

## Commercial Journey Economics lens

Departure can matter commercially through:

- repeat airport use;
- future lounge or hotel use;
- app engagement;
- rewards;
- future dining or attraction use. [S11]

But:

Persistent Digital Relationship != Proven Lifetime Value.

The public evidence does not establish retention or contribution economics.

## Strongest departure boundaries

Hospitality Service Exit != Airport Journey Completion

Hospitality Closure != Boarding Readiness

Service Location != Departure Location Automatically

Passenger Movement != Baggage Movement

Airport Communicates Flight Status != Airport Owns Airline Recovery

Unresolved Journey Issue != One Post-Departure Recovery Route

Physical Service Exit != Digital Relationship Closure

One Airport Journey != One Universal End Point

## Competing explanation

These are normal airport and airline closure processes.

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that Changi or its surrounding service ecosystem actively preserves guest identity, responsibility and recovery across these separate closure states.
