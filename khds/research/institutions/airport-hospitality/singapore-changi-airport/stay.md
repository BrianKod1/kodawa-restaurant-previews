# Stay / Participation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

How do public/transit location, terminal, flight state, lounge access, hotel access, attractions, assistance and digital services coexist while a traveller spends time within the Changi Airport ecosystem?

## Interpretation

Singapore Changi Airport is not one overnight institution.

For Case #40, the required KHDS stay dimension is interpreted as:

airport dwell and participation between arrival into the relevant airport state and departure from that state.

This can include:

- layover;
- waiting;
- resting;
- lounge use;
- hotel use;
- dining;
- shopping;
- attractions;
- terminal movement.

## Airport dwell contains multiple simultaneous states

A traveller may simultaneously be:

- airline passenger;
- transit passenger;
- terminal user;
- lounge guest;
- hotel guest;
- diner;
- attraction user;
- baggage owner;
- app user;
- accessibility-support recipient. [S2][S4][S5][S7][S9][S11]

Therefore:

Airport Dwell != One Participation State.

## Public versus transit participation

Public and transit areas are materially different participation environments. [S3][S4][S5][S6]

A public-area user may include:

- traveller;
- visitor;
- non-flyer.

A transit-area user may require a valid passenger state.

Therefore:

Inside Changi != Same Participation Rights.

## Transit-hotel participation

Transit-hotel use occurs inside the controlled transit environment. [S4]

The guest's hotel experience is therefore nested inside a wider passenger state.

The hotel relationship does not eliminate:

- flight timing;
- terminal;
- immigration boundary;
- onward travel requirements.

Therefore:

Hotel Guest != Independent of Passenger State.

This is a central nested-institution question for Case #40.

## Lounge participation

The representative lounge can combine:

- seating;
- food and beverage;
- showers;
- paid or credential-based access. [S5]

But lounge use remains affected by:

- transit location;
- capacity;
- entitlement.

Therefore:

Lounge Entitlement != Universal Lounge Access.

## Public-area participation

Hub & Spoke provides a contrasting public-area environment serving both travellers and non-travellers. [S6]

A traveller can receive a boarding-pass-linked benefit while the basic venue remains available outside a passenger-only transit state.

Therefore:

Hospitality Participation != Passenger-Only Participation Automatically.

## Attractions and tours

Airport participation can extend beyond waiting for a flight.

The Free Singapore Tour can become part of the layover experience when passenger, documentation, baggage, timing and capacity conditions align. [S9]

Jewel adds attractions and other public-area hospitality functions. [S15]

Therefore:

Layover != Waiting Only.

However:

Experience Available != Experience Operationally Reachable Automatically.

## Time state

Airport hospitality remains exposed to flight timing.

A passenger using:

- lounge;
- hotel;
- dining;
- attraction;
- tour

still has a departure or transfer obligation.

The current evidence establishes service and flight-state dependencies but does not establish representative time-management outcomes. [S2][S9][S14]

Therefore:

Hospitality Dwell Time != Unconstrained Time.

## Digital participation

The Changi App provides a single passenger-facing surface for several functions, including:

- flight information;
- baggage tracking;
- bookings;
- dining;
- attractions;
- payments;
- rewards. [S11][S12]

This can create apparent continuity.

However:

One Digital Surface != One Operating System.

The evidence does not establish shared internal ownership across all functions.

## Accessibility during participation

Accessibility support can involve both airport infrastructure and airline responsibility. [S7][S8]

Therefore:

Airport Accessibility Environment != One Assistance Relationship.

The evidence does not establish whether support remains continuous when the traveller moves among:

- terminal;
- lounge;
- hotel;
- airline-controlled process;
- immigration;
- other hospitality services.

## Nested hospitality institutions

Case #40 contains hospitality institutions inside a larger institution.

Examples include:

- transit hotel;
- lounge;
- restaurants;
- Jewel hotel;
- attractions. [S4][S5][S10][S15]

The guest may experience these as separate services while their usability remains dependent on the airport's controlled passenger state.

Therefore:

Nested Hospitality Institution != Operationally Independent Institution.

This remains a research direction rather than a formal pattern.

## Responsibility continuity

Potential participation owners include:

- airport operations;
- airline;
- hotel;
- lounge operator;
- dining operator;
- attraction operator;
- baggage system;
- government process;
- accessibility support. [S7][S13][S16][S17]

The public evidence does not establish whether these parties share one synchronized passenger context.

Therefore:

Shared Airport Journey != Shared Operational Context Automatically.

## Failure states

Potential participation failures include:

- wrong controlled zone;
- service capacity unavailable;

- lounge denial;
- hotel-access mismatch;
- assistance discontinuity;

The public evidence does not establish complete recovery ownership.

## Competing explanation

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that identity, entitlement, responsibility and recovery must remain continuous across those institutional boundaries.
