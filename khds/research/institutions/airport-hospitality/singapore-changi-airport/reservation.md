# Reservation

Public-evidence coverage: partial

## Research question

What exactly becomes committed when a traveller books or accesses airport hospitality whose eligibility also depends on flight, itinerary, immigration, terminal, baggage, capacity or passenger-status conditions?

## Reservation is not one booking system

Case #40 contains several potential commitment objects:

- transit hotel;
- public-area hotel;
- paid lounge access;
- programme- or card-linked lounge access;
- dining reservation;
- attraction booking;
- Free Singapore Tour reservation;
- other app-mediated hospitality commitments. [S4][S5][S9][S11][S15]

One Airport Booking != One Commitment Type.

## Transit-hotel commitment

A transit-hotel booking can create a lodging entitlement.

But the guest must also retain the correct transit-area state. [S4]

The hotel relationship therefore depends on more than:

- date;
- time;
- room;
- payment.

It also depends on where the passenger is legally and operationally permitted to remain.

Hotel Booking != Valid Access State Automatically.

## Public-area hotel relationship

YOTELAIR at Jewel exists on the public-area side of the airport relationship. [S4][S15]

For a transit passenger, access can require clearing arrival immigration before reaching the hotel or Jewel environment. [S3]

Therefore:

Airport Hotel != One Immigration Relationship.

The public evidence does not establish how booking interfaces communicate this difference at every decision point.

## Lounge commitment

Representative lounge access can be obtained through several pathways. [S5]

These may include:

- direct payment;
- travel credentials;
- participating card or programme arrangements.

However, actual access remains dependent on:

- transit location;
- capacity;
- applicable entitlement.

Paid or Eligible Access != Guaranteed Immediate Admission.

The current evidence does not establish complete denied-entry or capacity-failure recovery.

## Tour reservation

The Free Singapore Tour creates a useful reservation boundary.

A reservation or eligibility state can still depend on:

- flight schedule;
- layover duration;
- transit status;
- entry documentation;
- baggage;
- capacity;
- reporting time. [S9]

Reservation Confirmed != Every Journey Condition Satisfied.

## Dining and attraction reservation

The Changi App supports reservation or booking functions across dining and attractions. [S11]

This provides a unified digital surface.

It does not prove that:

- inventory is unified;
- cancellation rules are unified;
- merchant responsibility is unified;
- recovery is unified.

One Booking Interface != One Merchant or Recovery Owner.

## Flight reservation boundary

The underlying flight is a separate commitment relationship.

Case #40 does not treat the airport as the seller of the airline journey.

The official airport conditions and flight-information pages preserve airline responsibility for important disruption and re-ticketing states. [S14][S17]

Airport Hospitality Commitment != Airline Transport Commitment.

## Capacity state

Hospitality products can remain capacity-sensitive.

The representative lounge explicitly identifies capacity limitations. [S5]

The Free Singapore Tour also has limited participation availability. [S9]

Eligible != Capacity Reserved Automatically.

## Terminal and zone as commitment attributes

A hospitality commitment may be practically useless if the traveller misunderstands:

- terminal;
- transit/public area;
- immigration state;
- transfer time.

Therefore:

Service Reserved != Service Reachable.

The evidence does not establish whether all booking interfaces verify these conditions before commitment.

## Baggage relationship

Transit state can depend on baggage being through-checked. [S2]

That means a hospitality reservation can indirectly depend on a baggage arrangement controlled through an airline or baggage process.

Guest Has Reservation != Guest Controls Every Dependency.

## Failure states

Potential reservation failures include:

- wrong controlled zone;
- wrong terminal;
- insufficient transfer time;
- immigration incompatibility;
- baggage not through-checked;
- lounge at capacity;
- missed tour reporting time;
- flight schedule change;
- booking cancellation;
- payment failure;
- service-provider mismatch.

The public source set does not establish complete recovery for each state.

## Strongest reservation boundaries

One Airport Booking != One Commitment Type

Hotel Booking != Valid Access State Automatically

Airport Hotel != One Immigration Relationship

Reservation Confirmed != Every Journey Condition Satisfied

One Booking Interface != One Merchant or Recovery Owner

Eligible != Capacity Reserved Automatically

Service Reserved != Service Reachable

## Competing explanation

These may be ordinary airport-service booking rules.

Airport hotels, lounges, tours and restaurants naturally have separate:

- eligibility;
- capacity;
- merchants;
- operating zones.

That remains a sufficient explanation.

The stronger KHDS interpretation requires evidence that these dependencies materially change:

- responsibility;
- information continuity;
- recovery;
- entitlement;
- service ownership.
