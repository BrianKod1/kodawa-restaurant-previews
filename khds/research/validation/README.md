# New Candidate — Story Through Hospitality Model

## Status

Candidate

## Confidence

Medium-High

## Definition

> **A restaurant's operating model can function as a primary narrative carrier when the way it organizes service, participation, product, space, or guest behaviour materially explains the meaning of the hospitality experience.**

## Strongest Independent Evidence

- Haidilao
- ICHIRAN

## Why The Pair Matters

Haidilao expresses hospitality through:

- visible human service
- social participation
- active interaction
- personalization

ICHIRAN expresses hospitality through:

- privacy
- reduced interaction
- structured participation
- product concentration
- system personalization

Their surface mechanics differ.

The deeper narrative behaviour survives.

## Boundary

Do not apply simply because:

a restaurant:

has operations.

The operating model must:

materially explain:

what:

the restaurant means.

---

# New Candidate — Hospitality System Proof

## Status

Candidate

## Confidence

## Definition

> **When the guest-facing operating system materially explains how the experience works or what makes it different, the restaurant should provide sufficient evidence for the guest to understand that system.**

## Strongest Independent Evidence

- ICHIRAN

## Supporting Pressure

- Alinea

## Cross-Dimensional Support

- Arrival
- Photography / Evidence
- Reservation / Participation
- Story

## Boundary

where:

does not materially affect:

understanding

or:

participation.
