# KHDS Candidate Patterns

## Purpose

This document tracks research-derived hospitality patterns that are not yet validated enough to become permanent KHDS Principles.

It records:

- Emerging patterns
- Candidate patterns
- Partially Supported findings
- Observed research directions
- supporting behaviours
- unresolved research tensions
- evidence maturity

Independent hospitality research determines pattern maturity.

Kodawa Labs concepts may be used for implementation validation, but they do not count as independent restaurant evidence.

# 1. Emotion Before Interface

## Definition

A hospitality experience should give the guest a reason to care before asking them to process the complete functional offer.

Emotion may be communicated through:

- place
- food
- worldview
- heritage
- atmosphere
- aspiration
- concept

## Independent Evidence

- Ankole
- Cultiva
- Talisman
- Mawimbi

## Current Expressions

### Ankole

### Cultiva

### Talisman

Food, heritage, and atmosphere.

### Mawimbi

Connection, luxury, seafood, and experience.

## Status

Emerging

# 15. Human Confirmation as Hospitality

## Definition

A restaurant may preserve human involvement in the reservation process by requiring the team to confirm a booking after the guest submits a request.

Possible benefits include:

- flexibility
- operational oversight
- personal interaction
- restaurant control

Possible costs include:

- delayed certainty
- additional communication
- booking friction

## Independent Evidence

- Talisman
- Mawimbi

## Previous Status

Candidate

## Current Status

Emerging

## Confidence

Medium

## Promotion Reason

The behaviour now repeats across two independent restaurants.

## Important Limitation

Repeated use does not prove that human confirmation is superior to instant confirmation.

The behaviour is Emerging.

Its effectiveness remains unresolved. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7} :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

- approximately 5–10 or more independent restaurants
- restaurant diversity
- different hospitality categories
- different geographic markets
- different reservation models
- contradictory evidence
- implementation viability
- guest behaviour evidence where available
