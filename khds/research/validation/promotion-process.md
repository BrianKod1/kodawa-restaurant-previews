# KHDS Pattern Promotion Process

## Purpose

Not every observation becomes a KHDS Pattern.

Patterns must be repeatedly observed across multiple hospitality experiences before they become part of the framework.

---

# Promotion Pipeline

Restaurant Study

↓

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

↓

↓

Candidate Pattern

↓

Cross-Restaurant Validation

↓

Validated KHDS Pattern

↓

KHDS Experience

---

# Promotion Criteria

A pattern may be promoted only if it satisfies all of the following:

✓ Observed in multiple restaurants

✓ Supported by evidence

✓ Improves guest experience

✓ Consistent with the Twelve Foundations

✓ General enough to apply beyond one brand
