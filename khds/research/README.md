# Purpose

The KHDS research layer exists to:

study hospitality independently

extract transferable knowledge

pressure-test assumptions

document contradictions

and:

govern research maturity.

The goal is not:

to collect inspiration.

The goal is:

to understand:

how hospitality experiences work

what makes them different

what creates confidence

how guests participate

what evidence matters

how story operates

and:

where apparent best practices fail.

Research should improve:

decision quality.

It should not:

replace:

judgment.

---

# Governing Research Rule

> **Research narrows the decision space. It does not make the decision for us.**
