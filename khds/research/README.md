# Kodawa Hospitality Research

## Purpose

Kodawa Hospitality Research is the evidence layer of KHDS.

Its purpose is to study hospitality experiences, identify recurring behaviours, discover business gaps, test assumptions, and convert reliable findings into reusable hospitality knowledge.

Research exists to ensure Kodawa Labs does not design from taste or assumption alone.

---

# Mission

Study exceptional hospitality experiences.

Observe before interpreting.

Compare before generalising.

Validate before promoting.

Discover both:

- what works
- what is missing

Translate reliable knowledge into KHDS.

---

# Core Rule

Research before assumption.

Evidence before opinion.

No hospitality pattern should be promoted into KHDS simply because it appears convincing in one restaurant or because Kodawa Labs already uses it.

---

# Research Tracks

KHDS separates independent hospitality research from Kodawa Labs validation.

---

## Track A — Independent Hospitality Research

Study restaurants that were not designed by Kodawa Labs.

Current independent research set:

- Ankole
- Cultiva
- Talisman
- Mawimbi
Purpose:

Understand what existing hospitality experiences can teach KHDS.

Independent restaurant research is the primary source of evidence for pattern maturity.

---

## Track B — Kodawa Labs Validation

Study Kodawa Labs concepts and production experiences separately.

Current validation reference:

- Trunk & Tandoor Concept

Purpose:

Determine whether KHDS knowledge can be translated effectively into actual digital hospitality experiences.

Kodawa Labs concepts do not count as independent evidence.

This prevents KHDS from validating its own assumptions.
