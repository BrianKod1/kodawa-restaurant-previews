# Purpose

KHDS Research is:

the evidence layer

of:

the Kodawa Hospitality Design System.

Its purpose is to:

study real hospitality experiences

identify transferable behaviours

preserve contradictions

test assumptions

discover commercial opportunities

and:

convert reliable findings into:

structured hospitality knowledge.

KHDS Research exists so that:

Kodawa Labs does not design:

from:

taste

fashion

or:

assumption alone.

---

# Research Mission

Study hospitality carefully.

Observe before:

interpreting.

Compare before:

generalising.

Contradict before:

promoting.

Separate:

what can be observed

from:

what must be verified.

Preserve:

difference

rather than:

forcing restaurants into:

one formula.

The objective is not:

to prove KHDS correct.

The objective is:

to make KHDS:

harder to fool.

---

# Core Research Rule

> **Evidence wins.**

A finding should not gain:

research maturity

because:

- Kodawa Labs already believes it
- it appears aesthetically strong
- it sounds commercially useful
- it appears in our own production work
- several similar restaurants happen to use it

Research maturity should increase only when:

the evidence becomes:

stronger

more diverse

and:

more resistant to contradiction.

---

# Research Philosophy

The research system should repeatedly ask:

> **What restaurant would make this idea fail?**

A contradiction may reveal:

- a bad pattern
- an overly broad definition
- an overly narrow definition
- a missing boundary
- a context-specific mechanism
- a useful architecture variable
- a new research direction

Contradiction is:

not research failure.

Contradiction improves:

the model.

# Current Research Dimensions

Every independent restaurant dossier currently studies:

five primary dimensions.

---

# 1. Arrival

Primary question:

> **What does the guest need to encounter first in order to understand, trust, and enter the experience?**

Current research asks:

- What is the primary guest intent?
- What uncertainty exists?
- What creates initial meaning?
- What creates confidence?
- What utility is needed immediately?
- Does the guest understand how to participate?
- What level of commitment is required?
- How familiar is the guest with the hospitality model?

---

# 2. Atmosphere

Primary question:

> **What actually produces the feeling of the hospitality experience?**

Current research asks:

- Is atmosphere driven by place?
- worldview?
- continuity?
- escape?
- belonging?
- craft?
- service?
- ritual?
- guest behaviour?
- social interaction?
- experience architecture?

Atmosphere should not be reduced to:

aesthetics.

---

# 3. Photography / Evidence

Primary question:

> **What must be proven, and what is the best evidence medium for proving it?**

Current research asks:

- What claim is being made?
- What uncertainty exists?
- What evidence gap remains?
- Does photography prove the claim?
- Does the image demonstrate difference?
- Does it show use?
- behaviour?
- participation?
- process?
- place?
- What should remain unrevealed?
- Would motion or another medium prove the claim better?

Photography remains:

important.

It is not:

the automatic answer.

---

# 4. Reservation / Participation

Primary question:

> **How does the guest enter, experience, influence, complete, and continue the hospitality relationship?**

Current research asks:

- What access routes exist?
- Is reservation central?
- Is walk-in meaningful?
- Does waiting exist?
- What certainty is required?
- What commitment exists?
- What can self-service handle?
- Where is human assistance required?
- Where is human service itself part of the value?
- How deeply does the guest participate?
- Does participation continue after the visit?

Reservation is:

one participation route.

---

# 5. Story

Primary question:

> **What carries the meaning of the hospitality experience?**

Possible narrative carriers include:

- place
- worldview
- heritage
- biography
- product
- craft
- cultural memory
- ecology
- service
- ritual
- participation
- operating model

Story should not be reduced to:

an About page.
