# Purpose

This directory contains:

the KHDS hospitality research system.

Its purpose is to study:

real restaurant digital experiences

extract:

transferable hospitality knowledge

test:

patterns

preserve:

contradictions

and gradually build:

a research-backed hospitality design system.

KHDS does not treat:

individual restaurant examples

as:

universal rules.

The system progresses through:

evidence

comparison

contradiction

boundary definition

and

formal governance.

---

# Core Research Question

KHDS asks:

> **What hospitality behaviours remain useful across materially different restaurants, guest intents, markets, price levels, experience formats, and participation models?**

The goal is not:

to copy:

successful restaurant websites.

The goal is to identify:

why certain digital hospitality behaviours work

when they work

where they fail

and

what boundaries make them transferable.

---

# Research Philosophy

KHDS research follows several core rules.

---

## Evidence Before Preference

A design belief should not become:

research truth

because:

Kodawa Labs prefers it.

Patterns should survive:

independent evidence.

---

## Contradiction Is Evidence

A restaurant that contradicts:

a current pattern

is:

valuable research.

Contradiction should:

refine

narrow

or

challenge:

the pattern.

It should not be:

discarded.

---

## Pattern Count Is Not Progress

Research improves when:

definitions

boundaries

and

architecture

become:

more precise.

A successful research cycle may produce:

zero new patterns.

The NARISAWA cycle is:

a strong example.

---

## Website Evidence Has Limits

Public digital research may establish:

- visible navigation
- public story
- public photography
- public reservation routes
- visible policies
- public product framing
- public founder / chef prominence
- public atmosphere communication
- visible participation pathways

It must not be used to claim:

- CRM ownership
- customer-data ownership
- booking economics
- profitability
- conversion performance
- guest satisfaction
- actual emotional response
- operational efficiency
- backend automation
- staffing burden
- no-show reduction
- sustainability performance
- supply-chain performance

unless:

those facts are independently verifiable.

NARISAWA was selected:

several strong findings.

> **Can a strong worldview remain the organising force of hospitality when the restaurant is also highly chef-authored, seasonal, and operationally structured?**

not:

worldview is universally necessary.

The stronger finding was:

> **Worldview can become a strong hospitality organiser when it is materially translated into the experience.**

Franklin

and

Alinea

as:

meaningful boundary cases.
