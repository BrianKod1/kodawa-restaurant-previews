# KHDS Research

## Purpose

KHDS Research is the evidence layer behind the Kodawa Hospitality Design System.

The research exists to understand:

* how hospitality brands create meaning
* how guests are guided toward participation
* how atmosphere is communicated
* how photography builds confidence
* how reservation and access systems behave
* how story creates coherence
* where digital hospitality creates business opportunity
* which observations are strong enough to become reusable KHDS patterns

The objective is not to collect attractive restaurant websites.

The objective is to build a knowledge system that becomes more accurate through:

* repetition
* contradiction
* abstraction
* refinement
* demotion
* promotion

The research should become more precise when evidence challenges it.

---

# Promotion Discipline

A pattern should not be promoted because:

* it appears in many files
* the phrase sounds strategically strong
* Kodawa Labs already uses it
* it fits a favourite design philosophy
* the current restaurant confirms it

Promotion requires:

* repeated independent evidence
* materially different restaurants
* contradiction testing
* stable abstraction
* clear boundaries
* enough confidence that the behaviour is broader than one implementation

---

# Demotion Discipline

A pattern should be demoted when:

* independent repetition remains weak
* later evidence materially contradicts it
* the abstraction proves too narrow or context-specific
* the finding can be better explained as a mechanism inside another pattern

Dishoom’s demotion of:

Growth as Narrative

is an example.

Demotion is not failure.

It is correction.

---

# Rename Discipline

A rename should happen when:

the underlying behaviour survives

but

the existing language becomes too narrow.

Dishoom produced four major examples in participation architecture.

The behaviour survived.

The implementation-specific labels did not.

# Final Research Statement

The first KHDS research cycles were dominated by restaurants where:

reservation

was an important digital action.

Dishoom showed that this was not the deepest abstraction.

The deeper problem is:

participation.

That correction changes the language of four Emerging patterns:

* Participation Throughout the Journey
* Multi-Intent Hospitality
* Human Assistance When Complexity Requires It
* Escalating Participation Complexity

Together they create a more durable model of:

Participation Architecture.

Dishoom also strengthened:

Photography Should Demonstrate Difference

enough to promote it to Emerging.

That matters because KHDS now has repeated evidence that:

beautiful photography

and

differentiating photography

perform different commercial jobs.

At the same time:

Growth as Narrative

was demoted.

That matters just as much.

A research system should not only grow.

It should also:

remove weak assumptions.

The current research set now contains seven independent restaurants across:

* Kenya
* South Africa
* United Kingdom

with materially different:

* hospitality models
* reservation models
* atmospheric systems
* photography systems
* narrative systems

Yet KHDS still has:

0 Validated research-derived patterns

and

0 research-derived KHDS Principles.

That is deliberate.

The system is becoming:

more useful

before becoming:

more certain.

The next restaurant should not confirm the framework.

It should attack it.

Evidence wins.
