# Kodawa Hospitality Research

## Mission

Study exceptional hospitality experiences.

Extract recurring behavioural patterns.

Validate them across multiple restaurants.

Promote validated patterns into KHDS.

Never design from assumption.

Always design from evidence.

---

## Workflow

Restaurant

↓

Observation

↓

Pattern

↓

Validation

↓

KHDS

↓

Production

---

## Rule

A hospitality pattern is promoted into KHDS only after it has been independently observed in multiple restaurants.
