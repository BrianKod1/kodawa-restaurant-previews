# ICHIRAN Shibuya — Reservation / Participation

## Purpose

Study how:

ICHIRAN Shibuya

moves the guest from:

intent

to:

physical participation.

ICHIRAN is particularly valuable because:

reservation

does not appear to be:

the dominant abstraction.

The guest journey is better understood through:

- arrival
- possible waiting
- seating
- ordering
- customization
- service access

- refill
