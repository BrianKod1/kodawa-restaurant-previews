# ICHIRAN Shibuya — Arrival

## Purpose

Study how:

ICHIRAN Shibuya

introduces:

the hospitality experience

before:

the guest enters the physical restaurant.

The arrival analysis should determine:

- what the guest encounters first
- what uncertainty is reduced
- what must be understood before participation
- how much meaning and utility appear together
- whether the unusual dining system requires explanation
- whether privacy and systemisation shape the digital arrival
- how Shibuya-specific evidence differs from brand-level system evidence

The primary question is:

> **How does ICHIRAN prepare the guest for a hospitality model built around privacy, concentration, structured participation, and reduced interaction?**
