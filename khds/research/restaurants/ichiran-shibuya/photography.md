# ICHIRAN Shibuya — Photography

## Purpose

Study what visual evidence must do for:

ICHIRAN Shibuya.

ICHIRAN creates an unusual photography problem.

The restaurant is not differentiated only by:

ramen appearance

interior design

or:

social atmosphere.

Its published hospitality difference depends heavily on:

- Flavor Concentration Booths
- privacy
- structured participation
- customization
- reduced interaction
- individual dining
- product concentration
- service mechanisms

Photography therefore needs to answer:

a harder question than:

> **What does the restaurant look like?**

The primary question is:

> **What must a guest see in order to understand why ICHIRAN's unusual hospitality system is different and how they participate in it?**
