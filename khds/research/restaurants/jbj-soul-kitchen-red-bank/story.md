Primary Story Question

How can JBJ Soul Kitchen tell a coherent story of reciprocal hospitality without turning donation, pay-it-forward support or volunteer labour into proof that a guest deserves the meal—and without using celebrity, activity totals or selected success stories as substitutes for equal hospitality and social outcome evidence?
