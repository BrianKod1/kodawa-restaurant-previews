# Research Objective

Study what photography is required to prove in a restaurant where:

- worldview is explicit
- seasonality is central
- the menu changes continuously
- the exact product remains partly undisclosed
- chef authorship carries authority
- ecological and cultural meaning are important
- the digital interface remains restrained

The central question is:

> **When philosophy is a major part of the hospitality claim, what must photography actually prove?**
