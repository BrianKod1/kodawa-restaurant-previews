# Research Objective

Study how NARISAWA structures participation when:

- the restaurant is reservation-led
- the menu is omakase
- reservations open on a defined monthly schedule
- parties must contain at least two guests
- guests under 18 are not accommodated
- dietary restrictions can be communicated during reservation
- reservation inquiries have a separate contact route
- the dining experience is high-commitment
- exact future product remains partly unknown

The central question is:

> **How much uncertainty can a hospitality system preserve while still giving the guest enough clarity to commit confidently?**

# Public Reservation Evidence

NARISAWA’s official reservation page currently states that:

- reservations are not accepted on behalf of other people
- reservations for each month open at 10:00 am on the first business day of the previous month
- guests under 18 are not accommodated
- reservations are accepted for parties of two or more
- smoking is not permitted

The official menu also states that dietary restrictions, allergies, and specific vegetarian, vegan, or macrobiotic diets can be communicated during the reservation process.
