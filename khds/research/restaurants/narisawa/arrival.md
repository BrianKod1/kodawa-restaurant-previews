# Research Objective

Study how NARISAWA introduces the guest to a hospitality experience where:

- philosophy is explicit
- the menu is omakase-style
- seasonality is central
- chef authorship is visible
- participation is reservation-led
- exact product detail is intentionally limited
- commitment is meaningful

The central question is:

> **Does NARISAWA create arrival through immediate atmosphere, through philosophy, or through a combination of meaning and structured participation?**
