# Research Objective

Study how NARISAWA creates meaning when:

- philosophy is explicit
- Satoyama culture is central
- seasonality shapes the culinary proposition
- chef authorship is visible
- the restaurant is eponymous
- exact future dishes remain partly unknown

> **What actually explains NARISAWA best: the chef, the worldview, the place, the product, or the relationship between them?**
