Purpose

This file studies how Dans le Noir? London publicly uses:

photography

to create:

confidence

desire

recognition

and:

proof

for an experience whose defining guest condition is:

complete visual absence.

The research does not ask whether the photography is:

attractive

dark

premium

or:

technically polished.

It asks:

what each image can legitimately establish

what each image can only suggest

what must be proven through another evidence form

and:

whether visually simulating the experience would contradict the product.
