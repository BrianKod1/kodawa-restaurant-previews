Arrival Architecture Variables

The following remain:

architecture variables or mechanisms.

Pre-Experience Reassurance

Information and human contact that reduce fear or uncertainty before the defining threshold.

Sensory Commitment Disclosure

Clear disclosure of which normal sensory or behavioural capacities will be unavailable.

Behavioural Preparation

Instructions that make the guest compatible with the experience.

Light-Source Surrender

The removal of objects capable of breaking the defining environmental condition.

Belongings Surrender

Storage used as participation infrastructure rather than administrative convenience.

Confidence Threshold

A physical or procedural state where the guest receives enough reassurance to continue.

Human Handoff

The point where primary orientation transfers from interface or environment to a person.

Trust Transfer

The guest's shift from self-verification to guided participation.

Guide as Arrival Interface

A person acting as the primary navigation, safety and information system.

Consequence-Relevant Reconfirmation

Repeated verification justified by health, safety or operational consequence.

Re-Entry Handoff

A controlled path for guests who temporarily leave and must re-enter the experience.

Minimum Sufficient Dark-Room Arrival

The smallest set of digital, procedural, spatial and human layers required for confident entry.
