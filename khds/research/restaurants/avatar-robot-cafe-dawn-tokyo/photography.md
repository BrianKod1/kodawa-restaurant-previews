Robot as Embodiment Proof

Evidence that the robot carries human presence rather than replacing it.

Visual association of separated human, machine and process components.

Temporal Agency Proof

Evidence of live response, movement and continuity.

Sequenced proof that connects a named person with a local action.

Visible configuration showing the possibility of interaction.
