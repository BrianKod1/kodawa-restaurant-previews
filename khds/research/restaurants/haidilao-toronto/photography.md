# Photography

## Observation

Haidilao’s photography serves a broader role than:

showing attractive food

or:

showing an impressive interior.

The public visual system supports several jobs at once:

- demonstrating hot pot as a shared activity
- explaining how guests participate
- showing service interactions
- presenting food and broth
- communicating group dining
- reinforcing brand consistency across locations
- showing the scale and energy of the experience

Photography therefore works less like:

editorial atmosphere-building

and more like:

operational and participatory evidence.

---

# Evidence

Haidilao’s public materials visually support:

- communal hot pot tables
- multiple dishes and ingredients around the table
- shared dining
- service interactions
- staff activity
- cooking and table participation
- broth and ingredient variety
- restaurant interiors
- social group dining

The brand’s hot pot culture material explains the meal as a sequence of:

Choose Soup Bases

↓

Mix Dipping Sauce

↓

Cook Protein and Vegetables

↓

Add Starches

Photography and visual material can therefore reinforce:

what the guest is expected to do

rather than only:

what the finished food looks like.

---

# Interpretation

Haidilao creates a useful distinction between:

## Product Photography

and

## Participation Photography

Product photography answers:

> What will I eat?

Participation photography answers:

> What will I actually be doing?

For Haidilao:

the second question is unusually important.

The guest is not receiving:

a fully completed plate

as the complete hospitality product.

Part of the product exists through:

shared preparation

interaction

choice

and:

table activity.

Photography therefore has an opportunity to demonstrate:

the experience in motion.

# Finding 1 — Photography Can Explain Behaviour

## Observation

The Haidilao experience contains:

guest actions

that may be unfamiliar to:

first-time hot pot diners.

These include:

- choosing soup bases
- preparing dipping sauces
- cooking ingredients
- sharing food
- pacing the meal around the hot pot

## Interpretation

Photography can reduce uncertainty by showing:

how people behave

inside the experience.

That means photography can function as:

behavioural instruction

without needing:

formal instructional diagrams.

## Existing Pattern Pressure

### Photography as Use-Case Evidence

Haidilao strongly supports:

the underlying idea.

The image can demonstrate:

not merely:

who uses the restaurant

but:

how the restaurant is used.

## Potential Refinement

Use-case photography may contain at least three levels:

### Level 1 — Presence

People are visible.

### Level 2 — Occasion

The image explains:

who the experience may suit.

### Level 3 — Behaviour

The image explains:

what people actually do.

Haidilao creates strong potential evidence for:

Level 3.

## Governance Impact

Do not change maturity yet.

Flag for:

knowledge extraction.
