# Arrival

## Observation

Haidilao’s Canadian digital arrival is substantially more functional and system-led than many of the premium hospitality cases currently in KHDS.

The homepage does not depend on:

- editorial restraint
- chef authorship
- destination photography
- heritage storytelling
- atmospheric mystery

as the primary opening mechanism.

Instead, the digital experience quickly establishes:

- the Haidilao brand
- hot pot as the core product
- customer experience as a central promise
- access to restaurant locations
- reservation
- cuisine information
- membership
- service information

The arrival therefore behaves less like:

an editorial introduction to a singular place

and more like:

an entry point into a large hospitality system.

The guest is given relatively direct access to what Haidilao is, how the experience works, and how to participate.

# Contradiction 1 — Emotion Before Interface Is Not the Only Effective Arrival Mechanism

## Current KHDS Pattern

Emotion Before Interface

## Haidilao Pressure

Haidilao’s digital system contains emotional claims around:

- happiness
- connection
- personalised service
- enjoyable hot pot

but the interface does not appear to depend on emotional staging before functional information becomes available.

Operational access is visible early.

Product information is visible early.

Reservation and location functions are visible early.

## Interpretation

This does not necessarily contradict:

Emotion Before Interface.

It does challenge any interpretation that means:

> functional information should always be delayed until emotional orientation is complete.

Haidilao suggests another possibility:

> emotional meaning and practical access may be introduced concurrently when the hospitality proposition is already understandable through the service and product model.

## Governance Impact

Do not change maturity yet.

Potential boundary refinement:

Emotion Before Interface should not become:

Emotion Before Utility.

Status:

Contradiction Pressure

mandatory editorial sequencing.
