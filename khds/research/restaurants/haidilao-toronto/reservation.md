# Reservation / Participation

## Observation

Haidilao’s participation system is broader than:

reservation alone.

The guest journey may involve:

- selecting a restaurant
- reserving
- arriving without a reservation
- waiting
- receiving hospitality during the wait
- choosing broth
- choosing ingredients
- preparing sauces
- cooking at the table
- interacting with staff
- using membership or loyalty functions

This means the participation architecture extends:

before

during

and:

after

the booking decision.

The reservation itself is only:

one participation state

inside:

a larger hospitality system.

---

# Evidence

Haidilao’s Canadian digital ecosystem provides:

- store discovery
- restaurant selection
- reservation access
- contact information
- menu / cuisine information
- membership access

The Toronto Downtown location is presented within the broader store system rather than as an isolated booking experience.

Haidilao’s public materials also describe:

- waiting-area hospitality
- customer-first service
- personalized service
- hot pot participation
- guest choice
- table-side activity

These elements show that:

successful participation

does not begin only when:

a reservation is confirmed.
