# Atmosphere

## Observation

Haidilao communicates atmosphere through:

- service
- social interaction
- guest participation
- ritual
- personalization
- activity
- shared dining
- operational support

rather than relying primarily on:

- architectural spectacle
- editorial restraint
- destination imagery
- chef authorship
- visual luxury cues

The atmosphere described by the brand is fundamentally:

active.

Guests:

- choose broths
- mix sauces
- cook ingredients
- interact with servers
- share the table
- wait within an intentionally serviced environment
- participate in performances or service moments at some locations

Haidilao therefore creates atmospheric value through:

what happens

between:

people

product

service

and:

participation.
