# Research Objective

Use Haidilao Toronto Downtown as a contradiction case against the current KHDS research model.

The purpose is not:

to determine whether Haidilao has a beautiful website.

The purpose is:

to understand what happens when hospitality value is organised heavily around:

- service
- staff behaviour
- guest participation
- operational structure
- interactive dining
- standardisation
- scale
- membership
- functional access

rather than primarily around:

- editorial storytelling
- visual restraint
- destination imagery
- chef authorship
- luxury signalling

---

# Primary Research Question

> Can exceptional hospitality be communicated digitally when the restaurant's strongest differentiator is human service and participatory experience rather than visual atmosphere?

---

# Contradiction Targets

This research cycle should actively test:

- Emotion Before Interface
- Story Before Features
- Atmosphere Through Consistency
- Atmosphere as Product
- The Guest as Participant
- Show the System Behind the Plate
- Photography as Proof
- Photography Should Demonstrate Difference
- Participation Throughout the Journey
- Multi-Intent Hospitality
- Human Assistance Where Structured Self-Service Is Insufficient
- Escalating Participation Complexity
- Commitment Without Pressure

# Special Research Questions

## Service

Can staff behaviour itself function as:

hospitality proof?

Can a digital experience meaningfully communicate:

personalised service

before:

the guest experiences it physically?

Haidilao's hot-pot model requires the guest to participate in:

- broth selection
- sauce creation
- ingredient selection
- cooking
- pacing of the meal

Question:

Does this provide stronger evidence for:

The Guest as Participant

than previous KHDS cases?

---

## System

Haidilao operates at substantial scale.

Question:

Can:

standardisation

and:

personalised hospitality

coexist?

what should KHDS learn from that tension?

# Evidence Boundary

Public digital evidence may establish:

- visible brand claims
- published service philosophy
- visible menu structure
- visible participation instructions
- reservation routes
- restaurant locations
- membership routes
- published operational information

Public website observation cannot establish:

- actual service quality
- guest satisfaction
- consistency between locations
- employee autonomy in practice
- reservation conversion
- CRM ownership
- profitability
- repeat-visit behaviour

Those claims require:

operational

guest

or:

business evidence.

---

# Research Standard

Do not reward Haidilao for:

supporting existing KHDS patterns.

Look specifically for:

- contradiction
- boundary conditions
- alternative mechanisms
- patterns that become too broad
- patterns that need renaming
- assumptions that fail under scale

The purpose of Restaurant #11 is:

to make KHDS harder to fool.
