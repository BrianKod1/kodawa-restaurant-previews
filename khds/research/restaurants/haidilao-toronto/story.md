# Story

## Observation

Haidilao’s story is not carried primarily by:

- founder biography
- chef authorship
- architectural heritage
- one-location history
- luxury narrative
- destination mythology

Instead, the brand story is organised around:

- hot pot culture
- customer-first service
- personalized hospitality
- shared dining
- social connection
- employee initiative
- operational consistency
- participation
- international growth

The story is therefore expressed less as:

a singular authored tale

and more as:

a hospitality model.

The guest learns what Haidilao believes through:

how the experience is structured.

---

# Evidence

Haidilao’s public materials describe:

- customer experience as a core priority
- personalized service as central to the brand
- staff-generated service ideas
- hot pot as a social and conversational dining format
- a mission centred on creating happy hot pot experiences
- international growth from its origins in China
- formal training and organizational systems that support the brand

Its public hot pot culture material also explains the guest’s role through:

Choose Soup Bases

↓

Mix Dipping Sauce

↓

Cook Protein and Vegetables

↓

Add Starches

The service philosophy and the product ritual therefore reinforce:

the same broad narrative.

---

# Interpretation

Haidilao tells a story about:

how hospitality should work.

The narrative is not:

> Here is our history.

It is closer to:

> This is how we create a happy shared hot pot experience.

The story lives across:

- service
- ritual
- staff behaviour
- product structure
- guest participation
- social interaction

This makes Haidilao a strong test of whether KHDS has been treating:

story

too narrowly as:

editorial content.

# Contradiction 1 — Story Does Not Need to Live in a Story Section

## Current KHDS Pattern

Story Before Features

## Haidilao Pressure

Haidilao makes practical functions visible:

- restaurant search
- reservation
- membership
- cuisine
- contact

without requiring the guest to first consume:

a long narrative sequence.

Yet:

the experience still communicates:

a coherent brand idea.

## Interpretation

Story may be embedded in:

- product structure
- service promises
- participation instructions
- interaction language
- operational design

rather than:

contained in:

an About section.

## Potential Boundary Refinement

Story Before Features should not become:

Story Page Before Utility.

> Meaning should be understandable before features are experienced as disconnected functions.

Boundary Pressure

# Story Versus Operations

Haidilao introduces an important KHDS question:

> What happens when the brand story cannot be separated from the operating system?

If the story is:

personalized service

then:

the service system

must:

deliver it.

If the story is:

participation

then:

the product structure

must:

allow it.

If the story is:

social connection

then:

the experience

must:

support it.

This suggests:

some hospitality narratives are:

operational promises.

They cannot be validated through:

website copy alone.
