# Primary Research Objective

## Can KHDS survive high-volume, walk-in-first hospitality?

FYN tested KHDS against:

* high commitment
* structured reservations
* deposits
* tasting-menu logic
* explicit participation conditions

Dishoom Covent Garden creates almost the opposite pressure.

Dishoom describes itself as primarily a walk-in café.

Most tables are preserved for walk-in guests, while reservations remain available during the day and for larger groups in the evening.

This gives KHDS a direct test of whether hospitality can remain:

* emotionally rich
* operationally clear
* commercially effective
* deeply storied

without making reservation the dominant participation model.

---

# Secondary Research Objective

## Can a deeply storied restaurant remain operationally simple?

Dishoom uses extensive narrative around:

* Bombay
* old Irani cafés
* food culture
* history
* social gathering
* individual café stories

while operating a high-volume, all-day restaurant model.

Each Dishoom café is also given its own founding myth rooted in an aspect of Bombay history or culture, intended to create a distinct personality for that location.

The research should therefore test whether:

Narrative Depth

can coexist with

Operational Simplicity.

Dishoom Covent Garden describes itself as mostly walk-in.

# Core Research Question 1

## Is Walk-In Hospitality a First-Class Digital Journey?

KHDS currently gives significant attention to reservation access.

Dishoom challenges whether:

Reservation Throughout the Journey

should always be interpreted through formal booking.

The deeper behaviour may instead be:

Participation Throughout the Journey.

Research should examine whether Dishoom successfully communicates:

* just come
* reserve if appropriate
* queue if necessary
* group-book when complexity increases

without making the guest feel uncertain.

Low to Medium

# Success Criteria

The Dishoom research cycle succeeds if it helps KHDS answer:

* Can walk-in-first hospitality remain digitally coherent?
* Does Reservation Throughout the Journey survive when reservation is secondary?
* Is Multi-Intent Hospitality now stronger than Dual-Path Hospitality?
* Does Human Confirmation as Hospitality become too narrow to keep?
* Does Escalating Reservation Complexity survive high-volume hospitality?
* Can narrative depth coexist with operational simplicity?
* Does cultural heritage behave differently from restaurant heritage?
* Can multi-location hospitality maintain location-specific identity?
* Does Photography Should Demonstrate Difference survive casual comfort food?
* Is Atmosphere Through Worldview strengthened, weakened, or better explained elsewhere?

The correct outcome is not maximum pattern support.

The correct outcome is better knowledge.

---

# Final Research Rule

Dishoom should not be treated as proof that KHDS works internationally.

It should be treated as another attempt to make KHDS fail.

The framework is useful only if it can survive restaurants that operate differently from the restaurants that helped create it.

FYN asked:

> How much commitment can hospitality require?

Dishoom asks:

> How much hospitality can happen before commitment is required at all?

That is the experiment.

Evidence wins.
