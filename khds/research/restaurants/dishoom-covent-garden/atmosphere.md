# Atmosphere

## Observation

Dishoom Covent Garden creates atmosphere through cultural interpretation, social energy, material memory, and location-specific storytelling.

The café does not simply reproduce an old Irani café.

Dishoom explicitly describes its design approach as a modern homage to Bombay’s Irani café culture rather than literal historical reconstruction. Its design language uses elements such as:

* marble tables
* bentwood chairs
* ceiling fans
* ageing mirrors
* monochromatic tones
* family photographs
* period-inspired artwork

to create a narrative environment.

Covent Garden adds its own specific atmospheric layer through the world of early Indian cinema.

The location’s official page positions the café around:

* Bombay in 1949
* early talkies
* glamour
* performance
* golden light
* music
* cinematic nostalgia

and states that the atmosphere of the Talkies era is captured within the Covent Garden restaurant.

Atmosphere therefore emerges through:

Cultural Memory

*

Cinematic Story

*

Physical Design

*

Social Energy

rather than one purely aesthetic style.

# Evidence

Dishoom states that each café is designed to capture the essence of an earlier Bombay within a modern space while honouring the legacy of Bombay’s Irani cafés.

Its design methodology explicitly rejects literal historical imitation and instead uses selected material and visual references to create homage.

Examples include:

* marble tables
* bentwood chairs
* ceiling fans
* ageing mirrors
* monochromatic palette
* family photographs
* artwork that carries its own stories.

The Covent Garden location is given a distinct narrative world.

Its page introduces:

## Bombay, 1949

and frames the restaurant around early Indian talking pictures, performance, cinema, glamour, and social excitement.

Dishoom also describes the Covent Garden interiors as:

Bombay talkies-inspired.

The café remains open throughout the day and presents itself as a place for:

* breakfast
* lunch
* afternoon chai
* dinner
* evening drinks.

This means its atmosphere must support multiple social states without losing its overall identity.
