# Reservation

## Observation

Cultiva makes reservation available from the beginning of the guest journey and keeps it accessible through both the primary navigation and the homepage hero.

The booking action is presented clearly and confidently, but it does not overpower the restaurant’s emotional introduction.

When the guest selects the reservation action, they are taken to a dedicated reservation page containing an embedded EatApp booking widget.

---

## Evidence

- “Reservations” appears in the primary navigation.
- “BOOK YOUR TABLE” appears directly beneath the homepage opening statement.
- The reservation action is available before the guest explores the menu, farm, events, or full restaurant story.
- The booking flow is hosted on a dedicated page within Cultiva’s website.
- The reservation page uses an embedded EatApp widget.
- The guest remains within Cultiva’s branded website environment when beginning the booking process. 
---

## Interpretation

Cultiva treats reservation as a core part of the experience rather than as a final conversion placed at the bottom of the website.

The booking action is introduced alongside the emotional promise of the restaurant. This allows two types of guests to proceed naturally:

- guests who are ready to reserve immediately
- guests who want to explore the restaurant before committing

This supports guest choice without interrupting the narrative.

The dedicated reservation page creates a clear transition from inspiration to action. However, the final experience depends partly on a third-party booking platform, meaning some control over usability, performance, and guest data may sit outside Cultiva’s own system.

## Existing KHDS Pattern Strengthened

### Reservation Throughout the Journey

Cultiva strongly supports this principle because booking is accessible:

- in the main navigation
- within the homepage arrival
- before detailed restaurant information
- through a dedicated reservation destination

The action remains visible without dominating the emotional experience.

---

## Candidate Pattern

### Dual-path hospitality

A hospitality website should support both the guest who is ready to reserve immediately and the guest who wants to build confidence through exploration first.

Status: Candidate

---

## Business Interpretation

Cultiva understands that some visitors arrive with existing intent.

By placing reservation prominently at the beginning, the website reduces the distance between interest and commitment.

The embedded system also allows the restaurant to accept direct bookings through its own website rather than sending guests to a restaurant-discovery marketplace.

However, because the final booking technology is provided by EatApp, further research would be required to determine:

- who owns the guest data
- whether booking deposits are supported
- whether the restaurant pays per-cover or platform fees
- whether confirmations and follow-up communication remain fully branded

The website alone does not provide enough evidence to answer those questions.
