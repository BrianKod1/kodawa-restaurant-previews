identifiable vulnerability

and:

Contradiction 4 — A Polished Plate Can Demonstrate Craft and Conceal Variability

possibility.

It does not prove:

Contradiction 5 — Process Photography Can Prove Work Without Proving the System

Hands chopping vegetables establish:

or:

and:

or:
