# Photography

## Observation

Photography occupies most of the visual space across major sections.

Images are cinematic rather than informational.

People, food, lighting, and atmosphere work together to communicate quality before any detailed explanation.

---

## Evidence

- Large full-screen imagery.
- Consistent photographic style.
- Warm lighting throughout.
- Close-up food photography.
- Environmental photography establishes atmosphere.
- Images are given space to breathe.

---

## Initial Insight

Photography is the primary storytelling device throughout the experience.

Photography is treated as communication rather than decoration.

Each image advances the guest's emotional understanding of the restaurant.

---

## Candidate Pattern

Photography before decoration.

Use photography to communicate atmosphere, quality, and emotion before explanation.

Images should carry narrative rather than simply fill space.
