# Reservation

## Observation

Reservation actions appear throughout the website.

A reservation button is visible in the site header.

A floating reservation button remains accessible while browsing.

Booking language is concise and consistent.

Reservation controls maintain the same visual style across the experience.
---

## Evidence

- Reservation button appears in the site header.
- Floating reservation button remains accessible during browsing.
- Booking language is concise and action-oriented.
- Reservation actions maintain visual consistency across the site.

---

## Initial Insight

The website does not wait until the end to ask for a reservation.

Instead, it ensures booking is always available while allowing the guest to progress naturally through the story.

The reservation flow supports the narrative instead of competing with it.

This reduces friction without creating pressure.

---

## Candidate Pattern

Own the guest relationship.

Reservation should be accessible throughout the experience without overwhelming the guest.

Booking opportunities should appear when intent naturally increases rather than only at the end of the journey.
