# Atmosphere

## Observation

Warm colours are used consistently throughout the website.

Photography, typography, spacing, and motion follow the same visual language.

No single visual element dominates the experience.

The same emotional tone is maintained across sections.

---

## Evidence

- Warm, restrained colour palette.
- Generous spacing in some of its pages.
- Subtle motion reinforces rather than distracts.
- Photography and layout create a sense of place.

---

## Initial Insight

Atmosphere emerges from the interaction of multiple design systems rather than any individual component.

Consistency creates immersion.

---

## Candidate Pattern

Consistency creates atmosphere.

A premium hospitality experience is created when colour, typography, photography, motion, and spacing communicate the same emotional message.
