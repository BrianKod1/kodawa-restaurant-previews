# Knowledge Extraction

## Restaurant

Ankole

---

# What surprised us?

The homepage explains very little.

Instead, it trusts imagery, spacing, and atmosphere to communicate quality before presenting detailed information.

---

# What repeated?

Several behaviours consistently appeared throughout the experience:

- Emotion before information.
- Photography as communication.
- Consistent visual restraint.
- Reservation is accessible accross the journey

---

# What challenged our assumptions?

Premium hospitality websites do not prioritize information density.

They prioritize emotional confidence.

Traditional UX encourages answering questions quickly.

Hospitality demonstrates that creating desire first can produce a stronger user journey.



---

# What should KHDS remember?

Premium hospitality websites are not designed to deliver information as quickly as possible.

They are designed to create confidence before commitment.

Emotion earns attention.

Story creates meaning.

Photography creates appetite.

Reservation converts intent into action.
