# Reservation

## Observation

Talisman presents reservation as an important practical step within the homepage journey.

The restaurant strongly recommends booking in advance and provides a direct path to make a reservation.

Unlike Cultiva, where reservation is introduced directly within the emotional opening, Talisman places booking within a broader sequence that first establishes food, story, ambience, and restaurant credibility.

The reservation experience therefore feels operationally clear, but less immediate than Cultiva’s arrival-level booking approach.

---

## Evidence

- The homepage contains a dedicated “Reservations” section.
- Talisman explicitly recommends that diners reserve a table in advance.
- The website states that the team will confirm the reservation by phone or email.
- A direct reservation link is provided.
- Opening hours and kitchen closing times appear alongside the reservation information.
- Guests can also contact the restaurant through:
  - email
  - telephone
- The reservation email is displayed as `reservations@thetalismanrestaurant.com`.
- The phone number is displayed as `+254 705 999 997`.
- Reservation information is integrated into the restaurant’s own website rather than being hidden on a separate discovery platform. 

---

## Interpretation

Talisman treats reservation as a guided request rather than a fully instant booking transaction.

The sequence appears to be:

Guest submits reservation request

↓

Talisman receives request

↓

Restaurant team reviews availability

↓

Confirmation is sent by phone or email

This creates a human-controlled booking process.

The restaurant retains direct involvement in confirming the table, but the guest may not receive immediate certainty at the moment of submission.

## Tension Identified

### Personal Service Versus Immediate Certainty

Talisman’s process may create a more personal relationship between the guest and restaurant team.

However, requiring later confirmation may also introduce uncertainty.

The guest may not immediately know:

- whether the requested time is available
- how quickly confirmation will arrive
- whether the request has been successfully received
- what alternatives are available if the table is unavailable

This creates a trade-off between human service and booking speed.

## Own the Guest Relationship

Talisman begins the reservation journey through its own website and provides direct email and phone access.

This supports direct communication between the restaurant and guest.

- where reservation data is stored
- whether an external booking tool processes the request
- whether guest information is used for future communication

---

## Possible Limitation

The booking process does not appear to promise immediate confirmation.

This may create friction for guests who:

- are comparing multiple restaurants
- need certainty quickly
- are booking from another time zone
- are organising a time-sensitive occasion
- prefer self-service availability selection

The homepage also places detailed operating hours and kitchen times within the reservation section.

This information is useful, but it may compete visually with the primary booking action.

These are design interpretations, not evidence of actual guest behaviour.
