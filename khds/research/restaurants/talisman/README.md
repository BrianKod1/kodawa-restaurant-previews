# Talisman Research Dossier

## Restaurant

The Talisman Restaurant

## Location

Karen, Nairobi, Kenya

## Research Track

Independent Hospitality Research

Talisman is being studied as an existing restaurant experience that was not designed by Kodawa Labs.

Its website is treated as independent evidence for KHDS research.

---

## Status

Talisman Research Dossier Complete
---

## Objective

Understand how Talisman communicates its identity, atmosphere, dining experience, food offering, and reservation journey through its digital presence.

The research will evaluate whether Talisman:

- strengthens existing KHDS principles
- challenges existing assumptions
- confirms emerging candidate patterns
- introduces new hospitality behaviours

---

## Restaurant Positioning

Talisman presents itself as an elegant gastrolounge focused on delivering a high-quality dining experience.

Its digital presence foregrounds:

- the restaurant experience
- menus
- events
- contact information
- location
- reservation access

The research should determine how effectively this positioning is translated into atmosphere, story, photography, and guest action.

## Research Questions

### Arrival

- Does Talisman lead with emotion, information, or action?
- What creates the first impression?
- How early is reservation introduced?

### Story

- Is identity communicated through place, history, food, or philosophy?
- Is the narrative explicit or implied?
- Does story appear before operational detail?

### Photography

- What subjects dominate the imagery?
- Does photography reveal place, process, people, or finished dishes?
- Does imagery carry narrative meaning?

### Reservation

- How is booking introduced?
- Does the site support both immediate and exploratory guests?
- Does the restaurant retain the guest within its branded experience?

### Atmosphere

- Which visual and narrative elements create mood?
- Is atmosphere consistent across the site?
- Does the digital experience reflect the restaurant’s positioning?

- inform the KHDS Pattern Registry
