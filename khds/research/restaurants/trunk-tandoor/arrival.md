# Arrival

## Observation

The homepage opens with a full-screen cinematic hero rather than immediate information.

The guest experiences atmosphere before details.

Restaurant identity is communicated through imagery, typography, and pacing before practical information.

---

## Evidence

- Full-screen hero image occupies the first viewport.
- Restaurant name appears prominently.
- Supporting copy is minimal.
- Reservation CTA exists but does not dominate the experience.
- Navigation remains understated.

---

## Initial Insight

The website delays functional information in favour of emotional immersion.

This creates anticipation before explanation.

---

## Candidate Pattern

Emotion before interface.

Lead with emotional immersion before functional information.

Practical details should support desire, not replace it.
