# Knowledge Extraction

## Restaurant

Trunk & Tandoor

---

# What surprised us?

The website explains very little.

Instead, it trusts imagery, typography, pacing, and atmosphere to communicate quality before presenting detailed information.

---

# What repeated?

Several behaviours consistently appeared throughout the experience:

- Emotion before information.
- Story before explanation.
- Photography as communication.
- Consistent visual restraint.
- Reservation opportunities remain accessible without dominating the journey.

---

# What challenged our assumptions?

Initially, multiple reservation buttons appeared excessive.

However, observation suggests each reservation opportunity corresponds to a different stage of guest intent rather than unnecessary repetition.

The reservation system supports the experience instead of interrupting it.

---

# What should KHDS remember?

Premium hospitality websites are not designed to deliver information as quickly as possible.

They are designed to create confidence before commitment.

Emotion increases attention.

Story creates connection.

Photography creates desire.

Reservation converts intent into action.
