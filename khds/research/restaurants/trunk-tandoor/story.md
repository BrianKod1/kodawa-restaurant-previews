# Story

## Observation

The website does not immediately describe the restaurant.

Instead, it invites the guest into a feeling.

The copy is intentionally restrained.

The restaurant trusts atmosphere and imagery to communicate identity before explanation.

---

## Evidence

- Minimal headline copy.
- Short supporting text.
- No lengthy brand history above the fold.
- Photography carries much of the narrative.
- Typography reinforces the premium positioning.

---

## Initial Insight

The guest discovers the restaurant rather than having it explained.

This creates curiosity and encourages exploration.

---

## Candidate Pattern

Story before features.

Invite guests into the restaurant's narrative before asking them to evaluate its offerings.
