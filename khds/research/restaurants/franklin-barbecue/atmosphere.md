# Research Objective

Study how Franklin Barbecue creates atmosphere in a hospitality model where the strongest visible signals appear to come from:

* product
* craft
* founder authority
* informality
* physical participation
* waiting
* counter-service rhythm
* social presence

* formal ceremony

* designed escape

> **Can atmosphere emerge primarily from behaviour, craft, and participation rather than from a heavily authored visual world?**

# Atmosphere Question 1

## What appears to create the emotional environment?

The visible Franklin system suggests several possible contributors.

### Product

Barbecue remains central.

### Craft

Aaron Franklin’s educational and technical authority extends well beyond the restaurant itself. The official About page connects the restaurant to books and the *BBQ with Franklin* instructional series, which teaches barbecue methods from beginning to end.

### Social Participation

The official private-events page explicitly describes the normal dine-in arrival as joining the line, getting a chair, being greeted by a line manager, and being able to enter the dining room before service begins.

### Informality

The visible participation instructions are conversational rather than ceremonial.

### Current Interpretation

Franklin’s atmosphere may be produced less by:

designed visual mythology

and more by:

what guests physically do.

* scarcity creates atmosphere
