# Research Summary

Alinea introduces one of the most controlled hospitality models in the KHDS research set.

Its public experience is organised around:

- three materially different dining experiences
- advance booking
- prepaid participation
- explicit timing
- high financial commitment
- strong policy structure
- clear confirmation states
- experience-specific accommodation
- significant institutional authority
- highly differentiated product positioning

The strongest current Alinea logic is:

Experience

↓

Difference

↓

Guest Fit

↓

Commitment

↓

Confirmed Access

↓

Structured Participation

This creates a major contrast with Franklin.

↓

Participation

## Alinea

↓

Compatibility

↓

↓

Alinea therefore gives KHDS a valuable opposite case:

Franklin reduces structure.

the universal model.

- urgency

- emotional manipulation

# Provisional Alinea Knowledge Statement

Alinea demonstrates that:

highly controlled hospitality

does not automatically conflict with:

KHDS.

It instead forces KHDS to distinguish more carefully between:

- commitment and manipulation
- complexity and predictability
- participation and freedom
- certainty and flexibility
- source proof and result proof
- explanation and mystery
- biography and authority
- worldview and experience architecture

The strongest Alinea correction is:

## Complexity Is Not the Main Self-Service Boundary

A highly complex hospitality experience can still remain:

digitally structured

when the relevant needs are:

predictable.

That places major pressure on:

`Human Assistance When Complexity Requires It`.

The second major correction concerns:

proof.

Franklin pushed KHDS toward:

showing the source of difference.

Alinea shows that:

sometimes the result

experience

authority

or

space

may already provide enough evidence.

The stronger question becomes:

> **What evidence gap actually remains?**

The third major correction concerns:

atmosphere.

Alinea adds another strong case where:

worldview

does not clearly appear necessary for:

atmospheric coherence.

Experience architecture itself may organise:

the atmosphere.

The fourth major correction concerns:

use.

Alinea gives KHDS strong reason not to promote:

`Atmosphere Through Use`

too quickly.

In some hospitality models:

guest use creates atmosphere.

In others:

designed atmosphere shapes guest use.

The fifth major correction concerns:

story.

Franklin established:

people first when people explain the experience.

Alinea establishes:

experience first when experience explains itself.

Together they point toward a broader future rule:

> **Narrative weight should follow explanatory value.**

That is not ready for promotion.

But it is now one of the strongest directions entering the comparison cycle.
