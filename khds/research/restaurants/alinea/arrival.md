# Research Objective

Study how Alinea introduces a guest to a hospitality experience built around:

- specific dining experiences
- advance selection
- prepaid reservation
- strong commitment
- controlled timing
- explicit policy
- limited post-purchase flexibility
- structured digital participation

The central question is:

> **How should arrival work when the guest is not simply deciding whether to visit, but which version of the experience to purchase?**

Alinea is valuable because its official site presents three distinct nightly experiences:

- Kitchen Table
- Gallery
- Salon

and directs guests into a Tock-based booking system rather than a walk-in-led model. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
