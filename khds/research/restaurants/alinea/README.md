# Research Role

Alinea is the ninth independent restaurant in the KHDS research set.

It was selected specifically to attack several patterns strengthened or promoted after Franklin Barbecue.

Franklin demonstrated that hospitality participation can remain:

- spontaneous
- physical
- walk-up-first
- operationally simple

Alinea introduces nearly the opposite participation pressure.

Its public booking system is built around:

- specific dining experiences
- advance selection
- prepaid reservations
- defined timing
- strong policy structure
- limited flexibility after commitment

Current Tock listings show prepaid reservation formats for Alinea experiences, while the restaurant’s FAQ states that bookings are only confirmed once payment has been accepted. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

This gives KHDS a strong contradiction case for:

`Walk-In as First-Class Participation`

and a harder test of the broader:

Participation Architecture.

# Reservation or Ticket?

Alinea provides a useful boundary case between:

an analytical question.
