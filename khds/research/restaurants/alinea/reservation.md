# Research Objective

Study how Alinea converts guest interest into participation through a hospitality model built around:

- advance booking
- prepaid access
- materially different dining experiences
- explicit timing
- significant financial commitment
- visible policy
- limited post-booking flexibility
- structured self-service
- human assistance for exceptions and bespoke needs

The central question is:

> **How should KHDS model participation when the standard route is already highly structured and prepaid?**

Alinea currently uses Tock for standard reservation management, and the public FAQ shows that a booking is only confirmed after payment is accepted. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# Participation Interpretation

Alinea does not weaken:

`Participation Throughout the Journey`.

It strengthens it.

When participation is:

expensive

time-bound

experience-specific

and

prepaid,

the guest needs more—not less—clarity about:

what they are entering.

Alinea also strongly supports:

`Commitment Without Pressure`.

It provides the most demanding current test of the idea.

The observable commitment is substantial.

But KHDS should continue separating:

commitment

from:

manipulation.

The biggest governance pressure is now:

`Human Assistance When Complexity Requires It`.

FYN suggested:

complexity increases human need.

Franklin showed:

physical flow can create human need even when the route is simple.

Alinea now shows:

very high complexity can remain digital when it is:

predictable.

That means:

complexity

is increasingly weak as the main trigger.

A stronger emerging abstraction may eventually become:

> **Use structured self-service where the participation need is predictable; use human assistance where coordination, uncertainty, judgment, or exceptions exceed what the structure can handle.**

Do not rename yet.

But this now deserves major governance review after the full Alinea cycle.

Alinea also makes:

`Compatibility Before Commitment`

much stronger.

A guest should not discover:

after consequential payment

that the selected hospitality product cannot accommodate a material need.

Finally, Alinea gives KHDS a much clearer participation-state model:

Interest

↓

Availability

↓

Selection

↓

Payment

↓

Confirmation

That will likely be useful beyond fine dining.

# Final Participation Statement

Franklin taught KHDS that:

a simple route can still produce uncertain access.

Alinea teaches the inverse:

> **A more complex route can produce highly certain access once the relevant commitment is complete.**

That means KHDS must stop treating:

route complexity

and

outcome certainty

as though they are the same thing.

The question may not be:

> How complex is this hospitality need?

The better question may be:

> **How predictable is it?**

Alinea publicly structures substantial complexity through:

- experience selection
- payment
- rescheduling rules
- transfer rules
- lateness rules
- dietary constraints
- wine options
