# Research Objective

Study what Alinea’s photography is actually required to prove in a hospitality model built around:

- highly differentiated dining experiences
- experimental cuisine
- high commitment
- strong reputation
- surprise
- theatricality
- experience-specific participation
- significant pre-arrival uncertainty

The central question is:

> **When the hospitality product depends partly on surprise, how much should photography prove before it begins to reveal too much?**

Alinea is especially valuable because its official site contains a dedicated gallery and photography across its experience pages, while its homepage publicly distinguishes the Kitchen Table, Gallery, and Salon. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

This makes it a strong stress test for:

- Photography Before Decoration
- Photography as Proof
- Photography Should Demonstrate Difference
- Photography Through Layers of Experience
- Photography as Use-Case Evidence
- Show the System Behind the Plate
- Promise Before Proof
