> **Farm photography is strongest as place and productive-context proof before it becomes ingredient or process proof.**

> **Evidence freshness and evidence durability remain distinct: a current dish may have short future relevance while a productive-place or method image may remain useful for longer-lived claims.**

> **A distributed hospitality system may require relational sequencing, captions, text, diagrams, or other media because photography alone may struggle to prove a network.**

An eighth is:

> **Photography Should Demonstrate Difference gains a new mechanism: Difference Through Relationship. The meaningful difference may live between the evidence subjects rather than inside one dramatic image.**

A ninth is:

> **Productive Relationship Legibility now has strong support across Arrival, Atmosphere, and Photography.**

A tenth is:

> **Place as Productive Context is strongly reinforced, while Place as Operating Infrastructure remains unproven because visual relationship does not establish operational causality.**
