The primary question is:

> **How does a destination tasting-menu restaurant structure access, commitment, dietary participation, and pre-arrival certainty?**

The secondary question is:

> **When the visit requires both a reservation and meaningful travel planning, should participation architecture account for the full visit rather than only the table booking?**
