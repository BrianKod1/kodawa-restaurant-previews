# Reservation Observation 1 — Reservation Is Encouraged, Not Mandatory

## Observation

Din Tai Fung UK explicitly states:

reservations are:

not mandatory.

Guests are encouraged to:

reserve online

but:

walk-ins are accepted.

## Interpretation

This immediately challenges:

reservation-first thinking.

The restaurant does not frame:

advance booking

as:

the only legitimate way:

to participate.

Instead:

reservation

and:

walk-in

coexist.
