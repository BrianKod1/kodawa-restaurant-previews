# KHDS Arrival Comparison

## Purpose

Compare how independent hospitality brands introduce the guest to their digital experience.

This document asks:

* What does the guest encounter first?
* What creates initial interest?
* How is confidence built?
* When does practical information appear?
* When does action appear?
* Is commitment required before participation?
* Which KHDS patterns survive across different hospitality models?
* Which patterns require refinement when the model changes?

The purpose is not to identify one ideal homepage sequence.

The purpose is to identify transferable arrival behaviours.

# Comparison Finding 1

## There Is No Universal Arrival Sequence

The current evidence strongly contradicts any fixed hospitality homepage formula such as:

Hero

↓

Story

↓

Gallery

↓

Menu

↓

Reservation.

The restaurants reach confidence through different mechanisms.

### Ankole

Place.

### Cultiva

Worldview.

### Talisman

Heritage.

### Mawimbi

Promise.

### Bistro Lolo

Familiarity.

### FYN

Authority + structure.

### Dishoom

Cultural identity + participation choice.

### Current Conclusion

The transferable behaviour is not:

a universal content sequence.

It is:

## Progressive Guest Orientation

The arrival should progressively help the guest understand:

* what kind of place this is
* why it matters
* what they can expect
* whether it fits their intent
* how they can participate

# Comparison Finding 5

## Reservation Throughout the Journey Is Under Major Abstraction Pressure

### Current Status

Emerging

### Previous Working Idea

Reservation should remain accessible throughout the guest journey without interrupting the hospitality experience.

### Dishoom Challenge

Dishoom does not treat reservation as the universal participation mechanism.

The guest may instead:

* walk in
* reserve
* group book
* inquire

### Pattern Outcome

## Underlying Behaviour Supported

## Current Name Partially Contradicted

### Confidence

High

### Stronger Possible Abstraction

## Participation Throughout the Journey

Possible definition:

> A hospitality experience should keep the appropriate path to participation understandable and accessible throughout the guest journey.

Participation may include:

* reservation
* walk-in
* queue
* direct enquiry
* group booking
* ticket
* order

depending on the hospitality model.

### Cross-Restaurant Fit

#### Ankole

Participation mainly through structured reservation.

#### Cultiva

Participation through reservation integrated with story.

#### Talisman

Participation through request and confirmation.

#### Mawimbi

Participation through multiple booking channels.

#### Bistro Lolo

Participation through flexible intent-sensitive access.

#### FYN

Participation through high-commitment structured reservation.

#### Dishoom

Participation through walk-in-first, reservation, and group routes.

### Comparison Recommendation

This is now a **serious rename candidate**.

However:

Do not rename from arrival comparison alone.

The reservation comparison must determine whether:

`Participation Throughout the Journey`

is the stronger canonical abstraction.

# Comparison Outcome

Dishoom does not break the broader arrival framework.

It breaks several of the words KHDS has been using to describe it.

The strongest transferable arrival behaviour is no longer:

> Lead the guest toward reservation.

It is increasingly:

> Orient the guest toward the correct form of participation.

That form may be:

* reservation
* walk-in
* group booking
* human coordination
* high-commitment reservation

depending on the hospitality model.

This gives Dishoom a major role in the current research cycle.

It does not invalidate:

`Reservation Throughout the Journey`.

It exposes the possibility that the pattern was named after the most common implementation observed in the first six restaurants rather than the deeper guest need.

The same issue exists with:

`Dual-Path Hospitality`.

Dishoom strongly supports multiple legitimate guest intents while contradicting the idea that those intents should naturally collapse into two routes.

The comparison therefore carries two major governance hypotheses forward:

## Hypothesis 1

`Reservation Throughout the Journey`

may become:

`Participation Throughout the Journey`.

## Hypothesis 2

`Dual-Path Hospitality`

may become:

`Multi-Intent Hospitality`.

Neither change should happen yet.

The reservation comparison must test them directly.
