# Arrival Comparison

## Restaurants Compared

- Trunk & Tandoor (Kodawa Concept)
- Ankole

---

## Common Behaviours

Both homepages opens with a full-screen cinematic hero rather than immediate information.

The guest experiences atmosphere before details.

Restaurant identity is communicated through imagery, typography, and pacing before practical information.
---

## Differences

Ankole

Uses stronger brand storytelling earlier.
Employs richer navigation options.
Balances atmosphere with clearer operational information.

Kodawa Concept

Uses more restrained copy.
Delays practical information further.
Places greater emphasis on cinematic pacing.

---

## Candidate Patterns Strengthened

Emotion before interface

Emotional context before functional information

Practical details support desire

---

## New Candidate Patterns

None.

No new reservation behaviours were observed
beyond the existing KHDS candidate patterns.

## Confidence

Medium

Reason:

suggesting they are transferable.
