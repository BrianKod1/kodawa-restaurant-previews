# Purpose

Compare how:

independent hospitality experiences

introduce:

meaning

product

place

utility

participation

evidence

and:

commitment

before:

the guest enters:

the main hospitality journey.

This comparison asks:

> **What does a guest need to understand before the interface asks them to act?**

The research no longer supports:

one universal:

arrival sequence.

Instead:

arrival appears to be:

an uncertainty-resolution architecture.

Different restaurants resolve:

different uncertainties

through:

different combinations of:

- meaning
- product
- place
- atmosphere
- authority
- story
- craft
- system explanation
- participation explanation
- operational reality
- evidence
- utility

The objective is not:

to identify:

one ideal homepage order.

It is:

to understand:

what the guest needs

for:

appropriate confidence

before:

participation.
