# Purpose

Compare how:

independent hospitality experiences

move guests between:

interest

intent

commitment

access

reservation

walk-in participation

waiting

self-service

human assistance

group participation

event participation

and:

completion.

This comparison asks:

> **How should a hospitality system route different guest intents into the right form of participation without forcing every guest into the same booking model?**

The research no longer supports:

reservation

as:

the universal abstraction.

The stronger model is:

Participation Architecture.

Reservation is:

one possible participation state

inside:

a wider hospitality system.
