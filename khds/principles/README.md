# KHDS Principle Library

## Purpose

The KHDS Principle Library documents hospitality design principles supported by research and comparative analysis.

This library translates research findings into guidance that can inform future Kodawa Labs hospitality experiences.

---

## Evidence Status

Entries in this library may exist at different levels of maturity:

Observed

↓

Candidate

↓

Emerging

↓

Validated

↓

KHDS Principle

A documented entry is not automatically a validated KHDS Principle.

Its status must reflect the strength and independence of the supporting evidence.

---

## Research Tracks

### Independent Hospitality Research

Research conducted on existing restaurant websites that Kodawa Labs did not design.

Purpose:

Discover transferable hospitality behaviours without evaluating our own work.

### Kodawa Labs Concept Validation

Evaluation of Kodawa Labs concepts against KHDS research and principles.

Purpose:

Determine whether our work applies the knowledge correctly.

These two evidence sources must remain clearly identified.

## Rule

Every principle entry must include:

- Definition
- Current status
- Supporting evidence
- Evidence source type
- Confidence level
- Design implications
- Anti-patterns
- Research references

No principle may be promoted beyond the level supported by its evidence.
