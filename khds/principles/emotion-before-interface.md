# Emotion Before Interface

## Status

Candidate

---

## Definition

Emotional context should precede functional information.

Guests should first experience the atmosphere, identity, and feeling of a place before being asked to evaluate practical details.

---

## Why It Matters

People make emotional judgments before analytical ones.

A premium hospitality experience creates desire first and supports that desire with information at the appropriate moment.

---

## Confidence

Medium

Reason:

The behaviour has been observed across one independent restaurant and one Kodawa Labs concept.

Additional independent restaurant studies are required before promotion.

---

## Design Implications

### Do

- Lead with immersive imagery.
- Keep hero copy concise.
- Delay detailed operational information until interest has been established.
- Let atmosphere create curiosity before presenting facts.

### Avoid

- Dense information above the fold.
- Multiple competing calls-to-action.
- Leading with logistics instead of experience.
- Overloading the guest with decisions immediately.
