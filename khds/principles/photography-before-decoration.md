# Photography Before Decoration

## Status

Candidate

---

## Definition

Authentic photography should communicate atmosphere, quality, and emotion before decorative graphics attempt to do so.

---

## Why It Matters

People make emotional judgments before analytical ones.

A premium hospitality experience creates desire first and supports that desire with information at the appropriate moment.

---

## Supporting Evidence

### Independent Hospitality Research

### Kodawa Labs Concept Validation

Observed in:

## Confidence

Medium

Reason:

The behaviour has been observed across one independent restaurant and one Kodawa Labs concept.

Additional independent restaurant studies are required before promotion.

---

## Design Implications

### Do

Use imagery as storytelling.
Maintain photographic consistency.
Prioritize authentic experiences.

### Avoid

Decorative stock graphics.
Imagery without purpose.
Inconsistent photographic styles.
