# Atmosphere through consistency

## Status

Candidate

---

## Definition

Atmosphere emerges when typography, photography, colour, spacing, and motion consistently communicate the same emotional message.

---

## Why It Matters

People make emotional judgments before analytical ones.

A premium hospitality experience creates desire first and supports that desire with information at the appropriate moment.

---

## Supporting Evidence

### Independent Hospitality Research

Observed in:

## Confidence

Medium

Reason:

The behaviour has been observed across one independent restaurant and one Kodawa Labs concept.

Additional independent restaurant studies are required before promotion.

---

## Design Implications

### Do

Maintain one emotional language.
Align every visual system.
Create rhythm.

### Avoid

Competing visual styles.
Inconsistent pacing.
Elements that feel disconnected.
