# Story Before Features

## Status

Candidate

---

## Definition

The guest should understand why the restaurant matters before being asked what the restaurant offers.

---

## Why It Matters

People make emotional judgments before analytical ones.

A premium hospitality experience creates desire first and supports that desire with information at the appropriate moment.

---

## Supporting Evidence

### Independent Hospitality Research

Observed in:

- Ankole

### Kodawa Labs Concept Validation

Observed in:

- Trunk & Tandoor Concept

---

## Confidence

Medium

Reason:

The behaviour has been observed across one independent restaurant and one Kodawa Labs concept.

Additional independent restaurant studies are required before promotion.

---

## Design Implications

### Do

Let narrative introduce the brand.
Build curiosity.
Reveal details progressively.

### Avoid

Feature lists above the fold.
Long operational explanations early.
Treating the homepage like a brochure.
