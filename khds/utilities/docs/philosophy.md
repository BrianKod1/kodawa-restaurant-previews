# Kodawa Hospitality Design System (KHDS)

> We don't build restaurant websites.
>
> We build digital hospitality experiences.

---

# Why KHDS Exists

The Kodawa Hospitality Design System (KHDS) exists to create hospitality experiences that feel intentional, memorable, and timeless.

It is not a collection of UI components.

It is a shared philosophy, engineering framework, and design language that allows Kodawa Labs to build consistently exceptional experiences while preserving the unique identity of every restaurant.

KHDS separates universal experience principles from brand identity.

The system defines structure.

Each restaurant provides its own personality.

---

# Mission

To help hospitality businesses own their digital guest experience through thoughtful design, reusable engineering, and systems that grow with the business.

---

# Vision

To become the foundation behind hospitality experiences that feel as authentic online as they do in person.

---

# The Twelve Foundations

## 1. Emotion before Interface

People remember how a place made them feel before they remember how the interface worked.

---

## 2. Story before Features

Every interaction should move the guest through a narrative.

---

## 3. Photography before Decoration

Real experiences are more powerful than decorative graphics.

---

## 4. Restraint before Complexity

Removing is often more valuable than adding.

---

## 5. Motion with Purpose

Animation exists to communicate, not entertain.

---

## 6. Mobile is the Primary Experience

Hospitality begins in someone's hand.

---

## 7. Pacing is our Signature

Interfaces should feel calm, confident, and intentional.

---

## 8. Respect the Brand

KHDS never imposes its own visual identity.

It reveals the restaurant's.

---

## 9. Create Emotion or Business Value

Every element should justify its existence.

---

## 10. Own the Guest Relationship

Restaurants should own every stage of the customer journey.

---

## 11. Build for Legacy

Optimize for long-term quality rather than short-term convenience.

---

## 12. Craft Over Convenience

Choose the solution that meaningfully improves the experience.

---

# Architecture

KHDS separates responsibilities into distinct layers.

```
Tokens
↓

Primitives

↓

Experiences

↓

Restaurant Theme

↓

Restaurant Content
```

Each layer has a single responsibility.

---

# What Belongs in KHDS

Reusable layout systems.

Reusable interaction patterns.

Accessibility.

Animation standards.

Typography hierarchy.

Spacing.

Engineering conventions.

Documentation.

Shared components.

Anything every restaurant should inherit.

---

# What Does NOT Belong in KHDS

Restaurant colours.

Restaurant fonts.

Logos.

Photography.

Menus.

Brand copy.

Business-specific behaviour.

These belong inside the restaurant theme.

---

# Engineering Principles

Every file should exist for a reason.

Every abstraction should reduce complexity.

Prefer composition over duplication.

Prefer clarity over cleverness.

Build systems that become easier to maintain over time.

---

# AI Principles

Future AI agents are expected to execute KHDS standards rather than invent them.

AI should:

Research before generating.

Understand the restaurant before designing.

Reuse components before creating new ones.

Respect brand identity.

Never violate the Twelve Foundations.

KHDS provides the rules.

AI executes them.

---

# Decision Checklist

Before introducing any new component, ask:

Does it strengthen at least one Foundation?

Is it reusable?

Does it belong in KHDS or the restaurant theme?

Does it simplify future development?

Would every future restaurant benefit from inheriting it?

If the answer is no, reconsider.

---
