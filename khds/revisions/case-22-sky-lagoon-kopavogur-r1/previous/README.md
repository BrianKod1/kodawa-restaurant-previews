## Unusable or limited source attempts

The homepage, visit, contact, FAQ, experience, booking, dining and terms pages on skylagoon.com returned loading shells through direct web reads. The supported Browser runtime was initialized; discovery returned no available browser. Rendered inspection was therefore attempted but could not run. This limitation belongs to this research session, not necessarily to the public website.

The [accessibility PDF](https://www.skylagoon.com/getmedia/c3d03421-f9cc-4a2c-969f-0a1a4432e33c/Sky-Lagoon-Accessibility.pdf) returned 404. An old opening-release PDF could not be fetched. Pursuit's two current news pages also returned shells. A 2023 brochure PDF exceeded the web fetch size limit; the readable brochure section was used with its temporal limitation. The tourism-board resort article yielded almost no substantive venue text and was not used for detailed claims.

Direct prices, terms, accessibility equipment, current menus, mobile interaction, image sequencing and source-only health claims remain excluded or unverified. No booking, payment, account access, communication or form submission occurred. The evidence does not establish operational performance, guest outcomes, independent sustainability results or causality.
