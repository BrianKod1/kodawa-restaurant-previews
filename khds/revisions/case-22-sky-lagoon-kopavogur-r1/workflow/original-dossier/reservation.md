# Sky Lagoon — booking, access and participation

Case #22

Research disposition saved 2026-09-09. Roles: adult day visitor, accompanying guardian, and intermediary purchaser. Clinical treatment and overnight accommodation are outside this selected route.

## Observations

Icelandair sells a Saman offer without transfers, identifying admission, ritual, shared changing facilities and a towel. Its page states a minimum age of 12 and adult accompaniment for ages 12–14. These are the seller's published offer rules, not a tested gatekeeping process. [Icelandair admission offer](https://www.icelandair.com/vacations/tours/sky-lagoon-pure-pass-without-transfers/), accessed 2026-09-09. Proof levels 1–2: existence and rule.

The US-facing package terms identify Icelandair as the contracting party. The standard tour section distinguishes separate add-ons from tours included in packages; the latter are described as non-refundable and non-changeable. This is a published contractual statement for that channel, not a legal conclusion about every customer or Sky Lagoon's direct sales. [Icelandair package terms, Contract and Standard Tours sections](https://www.icelandair.com/support/terms-and-conditions/packages/), accessed 2026-09-09.

## Interpretation

Reservation is an agreement about a bounded visit, not a room-night. The key comparison variables are seller, eligibility, service entitlement, transport inclusion and change route. A hospitality interface should preserve these together when a guest changes package or seller.

Candidate Capacity Proof receives a sector boundary: an advertised offer does not disclose simultaneous capacity in admission, changing areas or ritual spaces. A date selector would not by itself establish that all required resources are usable for the guest.

## Evidence gaps and tests

No live inventory, checkout, payment, confirmation, ticket scan or refund was exercised. Direct Sky Lagoon terms and booking pages returned shells; search-only prices, cancellation windows and direct eligibility details are not adopted as findings. [Direct booking attempted](https://www.skylagoon.com/booking/select-time/) and [direct terms attempted](https://www.skylagoon.com/terms-conditions/), accessed 2026-09-09.

Test whether guests can state their actual seller and refund route before payment, then whether a failed booking can be resolved without repeating sensitive information. Outcomes remain unverified; no maturity movement.
