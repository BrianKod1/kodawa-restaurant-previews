how Trunk & Tandoor production should be evaluated

once:

implementation begins.

to prove:

KHDS works.

to record:

what happens

- implementation friction
- pattern conflict
- unnecessary complexity
- missing architecture
- content limitations

- brand tension
- guest-experience risk
- business-value hypotheses
- implementation patterns
- research questions

# Evidence Discipline

Production validation should distinguish:

---

## Observable Production Evidence

Examples:

- component reused successfully
- layout works on mobile
- token architecture supports theme
- booking state is clear
- proof sequence can be built
- performance budget is met
- interaction remains accessible

---

## Implementation Interpretation

Examples:

- pacing feels more coherent
- use-case photography appears important
- a pattern creates unnecessary density
- a section appears redundant

These are:

production interpretations.

---

## Guest Evidence

Requires:

actual users.

Examples:

- guest understands positioning
- guest feels atmosphere
- guest finds booking easy
- guest prefers one flow

Do not infer:

from:

our own review.

---

## Business Evidence

Requires:

actual performance data.

Examples:

- reservations increased
- conversion improved
- more direct bookings
- higher event inquiries
- lower abandonment
- stronger repeat behaviour

Do not infer:

from:

design quality.
