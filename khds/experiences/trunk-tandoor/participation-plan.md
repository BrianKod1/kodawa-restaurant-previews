# Participation Principle 1

> **Intent complexity does not automatically require participation complexity.**

A guest may arrive for:

- dinner
- cocktails
- a date
- a celebration
- a social evening

while:

still needing:

one simple reservation route.

Do not manufacture:

separate workflows

without:

operational justification.

---

# Participation Principle 2

> **Automation should solve predictable needs.**

A need should remain:

self-service

when:

the rules

inputs

and outcomes

are sufficiently predictable.

Human assistance should appear when:

the structured route becomes:

insufficient.

---

# Participation Principle 3

> **Commitment should match consequence.**

Trunk & Tandoor currently appears:

medium commitment.

The guest should not be asked for:

high-commitment behaviour

unless:

the restaurant actually requires it.

Do not invent:

- deposits
- prepayment
- ticketing
- fixed menus
- rigid arrival windows
- eligibility gates

without:

# Participation Principle 4

- choosing

- submitting
- pending
- confirmed
- unavailable

# Participation Failure Criteria

The participation system is failing if:

- reservation is hard to find
- every guest is routed to WhatsApp unnecessarily
- every guest is forced through a long form
- multiple motivations create unnecessary booking paths
- confirmation remains ambiguous
- fake scarcity appears
- deposits are added without operational need
- marketing consent is bundled into booking
- walk-in status is invented
- large-group thresholds are guessed
- human assistance is used where structured self-service would be simpler
- structured self-service is used where judgment is clearly needed
- the booking experience becomes visually theatrical at the cost of clarity
- the guest cannot tell whether they are actually booked
