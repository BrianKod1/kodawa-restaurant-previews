# Journey Principle

> **The guest should never be asked for more commitment than the experience has earned.**

This is especially important for:

Trunk & Tandoor

because:

the digital experience is selling:

more than:

food.

It is selling:

a particular kind of evening.

---

# Journey Principle 2

> **Functional access should remain available even while emotional desire is still being built.**

This resolves a major Phase 2 tension between:

Emotion Before Interface

and

Participation Throughout the Journey.

The guest should not have to:

complete the story

before:

finding Reserve.

---

# Journey Principle 3

> **Every stage should reduce one meaningful uncertainty.**

The journey should not add:

sections

simply because:

restaurant websites usually contain them.

Each stage should answer:

a guest question.

# Journey Principle 4

> **The journey should create confidence before commitment, but not delay commitment when confidence already exists.**

This supports:

different guest readiness levels.

# Journey Failure Criteria

The journey is failing if:

- the hero looks premium but says little
- the guest cannot tell what kind of restaurant this is
- Nairobi becomes generic scenery
- the food feels secondary to design
- social imagery makes the brand feel like a club
- reservation is difficult to find
- every section contains a CTA
- story delays practical information unnecessarily
- image volume creates fatigue
