# Current Experience Definition

Trunk & Tandoor is currently defined as:

> **A fire-led, Indian-inspired rooftop social dining experience that lets guests experience Nairobi from above as the city transitions into night.**

Primary emotional promise:

> **Escape the city without leaving it.**

Primary atmosphere mechanism:

> **Designed Rooftop Escape Through Fire, Elevation, City Glow, and Social Energy.**

Primary narrative organiser:

> **A Different Experience of Nairobi.**
