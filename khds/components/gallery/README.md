# KHDS Gallery

## Purpose

The Gallery extends the restaurant's story through immersive photography.

Its objective is not to display images.

Its objective is to deepen emotional connection and increase desire.

---

## Primary Goal

Help the guest imagine themselves inside the experience.

---

## Guest Journey Stage

Curiosity

↓

Exploration

↓

Desire

---

## Supported Principles

✓ Photography Before Decoration

✓ Story Before Features

✓ Emotion Before Interface

✓ Atmosphere Through Consistency

## Required Characteristics

A KHDS Gallery should:

- communicate atmosphere before information
- use authentic restaurant photography
- create visual rhythm
- showcase people, food, and environment
- encourage exploration
- support the restaurant narrative
- maintain visual consistency

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- stock photography
- random image collections
- inconsistent editing
- overcrowded layouts
- tiny thumbnails
- decorative images without purpose
- poor image quality

---

## Success Criteria

The guest should be able to answer:

"What would it feel like to be here?"

before asking

"What does the restaurant serve?"
