# Hero Implementation

## Responsibilities

The Hero is responsible for creating the guest's first emotional impression.

It should establish atmosphere before presenting detailed information.

---

## Required Elements

- Full-screen or near full-screen photography
- Minimal headline
- Short supporting copy
- Reservation CTA
- Clear visual hierarchy

---

## Behaviour

- Create anticipation immediately.
- Delay operational information.
- Encourage scrolling.
- Keep reservation visible without dominating the experience.

---

## Interaction Rules

- Hero content should remain readable over imagery.
- Scrolling should reveal the story naturally.
- Motion should reinforce immersion, never distract.

---

## Visual Rules

- Photography occupies the majority of the visual space.
- Typography remains restrained.
- Generous spacing.
- High contrast for readability.

---

## Accessibility

- Hero images require descriptive alt text.
- Text contrast must meet accessibility standards.
- CTA must be keyboard accessible.

---

## Responsive Behaviour

Desktop
- Full cinematic hero.

Tablet
- Preserve emotional impact while reducing height.

Mobile
- Prioritize photography and CTA.
- Maintain readable typography.
- Avoid excessive vertical scrolling before reaching the CTA.

---

## Implementation Checklist

□ Emotional first impression
□ Story begins immediately
□ Photography is authentic
□ Reservation available
□ Responsive
□ Accessible
□ Supports KHDS principles
