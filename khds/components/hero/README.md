# KHDS Hero

Mission

Create emotional anticipation that motivates the guest to continue exploring the experience.

The Hero exists to establish feeling before explanation.

## Purpose

The Hero is responsible for creating the guest's first emotional impression.

Its objective is not to explain the restaurant.

Its objective is to make the guest want to continue.

---

## Primary Goal

Create anticipation.

---

## Guest Journey Stage

Arrival

↓

Curiosity

↓

Exploration

---

## Required Characteristics

A KHDS Hero should:

- communicate atmosphere immediately
- establish emotional tone
- use authentic photography
- contain concise copy
- provide a clear reservation path
- avoid overwhelming the guest

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- excessive text
- multiple competing CTAs
- stock imagery
- feature-first messaging
- visual clutter

---

## Success Criteria

The guest should be able to answer:

"What kind of experience am I about to have?"

before asking

"What does this restaurant offer?"
