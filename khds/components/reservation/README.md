# KHDS Reservation

## Mission

Enable the guest to commit at the moment they are ready.

Reservation should feel like the natural continuation of the hospitality experience rather than a sales prompt.

---

## Purpose

The Reservation component transforms emotional interest into confident action.

It should always remain accessible while respecting the guest's pace and preserving the restaurant's atmosphere.

---

## Primary Goal

Reduce friction between desire and commitment.

---

## Guest Journey Stage

Interest

↓

Decision

↓

Commitment

## Required Characteristics

A KHDS Reservation component should:

- remain accessible throughout the experience
- communicate confidence and clarity
- require as few steps as possible
- preserve the emotional tone of the website
- encourage commitment without creating pressure
- integrate naturally with the surrounding content

---

## Reservation Philosophy

Reservation is not a marketing element.

It is a hospitality service.

Guests should never feel pushed into booking.

They should feel invited.

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- aggressive pop-ups
- repeated booking prompts
- countdown timers
- fake urgency
- hidden reservation actions
- complicated booking flows
- unnecessary form fields
- distracting animations

---

## Success Criteria

The guest should be able to answer:

"How do I reserve?"

immediately,

while still feeling immersed in the restaurant experience.

The booking process should feel effortless rather than transactional.
