# KHDS Footer

## Mission

Conclude the guest journey with confidence, clarity, and a lasting impression.

The Footer should reinforce the restaurant's identity while making essential information easy to find without feeling like an afterthought.

---

## Purpose

The Footer provides the guest with final opportunities to:

- make a reservation
- contact the restaurant
- discover practical information
- continue engaging with the brand

It should complete the hospitality experience rather than simply end the page.

---

## Primary Goal

Transform confidence into action while leaving the guest with a strong final impression.

---

## Required Characteristics

A KHDS Footer should:

- reinforce the restaurant identity
- provide essential information clearly
- maintain visual consistency with the rest of the experience
- keep reservation accessible
- encourage continued engagement
- feel calm and intentional
- avoid unnecessary clutter

---

## Information Priorities

The Footer should prioritize:

1. Reservation
2. Restaurant Identity
3. Contact Information
4. Location
5. Opening Hours
6. Social Channels
7. Copyright and Legal Information

Optional information should never compete with these priorities.

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- cluttered layouts
- excessive navigation links
- duplicate content
- oversized social media sections
- weak visual hierarchy
- inconsistent branding
- multiple competing calls-to-action
- decorative elements without purpose

---

## Success Criteria

The guest should be able to answer:

"How do I contact or visit this restaurant?"

and

"How do I make a reservation?"

without searching.

The Footer should leave the guest with the same feeling of quality established at the beginning of the experience.
