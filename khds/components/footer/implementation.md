# KHDS Footer Implementation

## Objective

Implement a footer that concludes the guest journey with clarity, confidence, and continuity.

The Footer should make essential information easy to find while preserving the restaurant’s emotional tone and visual identity.

---

## Component Responsibilities

The Footer component must:

- reinforce the restaurant’s brand identity
- provide essential contact and location information
- keep reservation accessible
- support continued engagement
- maintain consistency with the rest of the website
- conclude the page without feeling abrupt or cluttered

---

## Required Elements

A KHDS Footer should include:

- Restaurant Logo or Name
- Reservation CTA
- Address
- Contact Information
- Opening Hours
- Primary Navigation Links
- Social Media Links
- Copyright Information

Optional:

- Newsletter Signup
- Secondary Locations
- Legal Links
- Accessibility Statement
- Short Brand Message

Only include optional elements when they add clear guest or business value.

---

## Information Hierarchy

Recommended priority:

1. Reservation
2. Restaurant Identity
3. Location
4. Contact Information
5. Opening Hours
6. Navigation
7. Social Channels
8. Legal Information

The most important actions should remain visually clear without competing with one another.

---

## Layout Behaviour

The Footer may use:

- a single-column layout
- a multi-column layout
- an editorial layout
- a reservation-focused layout

The structure should reflect the amount of information available.

Avoid forcing a complex multi-column footer when the restaurant only needs a few essential details.

---

## Behaviour

The Footer should:

- appear as a natural continuation of the page
- remain visually calm
- provide clear final actions
- preserve the restaurant’s tone
- avoid introducing new visual styles
- make contact and booking information easy to scan

The Footer should never feel like a storage area for leftover content.

---

## Reservation Behaviour

Reservation should remain clearly available in the Footer.

The CTA should:

- use consistent language
- match the primary reservation action used elsewhere
- remain visually prominent without overpowering the Footer
- link directly to the booking flow

Do not introduce a new booking process or alternate reservation destination here.

---

## Visual Guidelines

The Footer should use:

- consistent typography
- generous spacing
- restrained colour usage
- clear hierarchy
- subtle separators where needed
- brand-aligned imagery or texture only when purposeful

The Footer should feel connected to the rest of the site rather than designed as a separate section.

---

## Responsive Behaviour

### Desktop

- Use columns when the content justifies them
- Maintain generous spacing
- Keep reservation and contact details easy to identify
- Avoid excessively wide text blocks

### Tablet

- Reduce column count where necessary
- Preserve clear grouping
- Maintain comfortable touch targets

### Mobile

- Use a single-column or stacked layout
- Keep reservation near the top of the Footer
- Make contact details easy to tap
- Avoid dense link clusters
- Maintain readable spacing between groups

The mobile Footer should feel intentional, not like collapsed desktop content.

---

## Contact Links

Use interactive links where appropriate:

- phone numbers should use telephone links
- email addresses should use email links
- addresses should link to a map or directions
- social icons should have accessible labels

External links should open predictably and should not interrupt essential tasks.

---

## Accessibility

The Footer must:

- use semantic footer markup
- maintain logical heading order
- provide visible focus states
- use descriptive link labels
- maintain accessible colour contrast
- ensure icons have accessible names
- support keyboard navigation
- avoid relying on colour alone to communicate meaning

Repeated navigation should remain understandable to screen-reader users.

---

## Motion

Motion should be minimal.

Allowed:

- subtle link feedback
- focus transitions
- restrained reveal animation
- hover feedback

Avoid:

- autoplay animation
- distracting background movement
- decorative motion
- effects that delay access to information

The Footer’s role is completion, not spectacle.

---

## Performance

The Footer should:

- avoid unnecessary scripts
- use lightweight icons
- load optional embeds only when needed
- avoid large decorative assets
- preserve fast page completion

The final section of the page should not create a noticeable performance penalty.

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- excessive link lists
- duplicate navigation without purpose
- several competing CTAs
- outdated contact information
- inconsistent reservation links
- large social-media walls
- poor contrast
- tiny legal text
- visually disconnected styling
- unnecessary forms
- cluttered mobile layouts

---

## Content Maintenance

Footer information must be easy to update.

Regularly verify:

- opening hours
- phone number
- email address
- location
- reservation link
- social channels
- copyright year

Incorrect practical information damages trust.

---

## Implementation Checklist

□ Restaurant identity included

□ Reservation CTA included

□ Address included

□ Contact information included

□ Opening hours included

□ Navigation links reviewed

□ Social links reviewed

□ Legal information included

□ Telephone and email links work

□ Map or directions link works

□ Responsive layouts tested

□ Mobile spacing verified

□ Keyboard navigation verified

□ Focus states visible

□ Colour contrast checked

□ Reservation link matches the rest of the website

□ Outdated or duplicate content removed

---

## Success Criteria

The Footer succeeds when the guest can quickly:

- make a reservation
- contact the restaurant
- find the location
- confirm opening hours
- continue engaging with the brand

The final impression should feel as intentional and polished as the first.
