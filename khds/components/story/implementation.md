# KHDS Story Implementation

## Objective

Implement a storytelling experience that builds emotional connection before presenting detailed information about the restaurant.

The Story component should transform curiosity into trust by revealing the restaurant's identity, philosophy, craftsmanship, and personality.

---

## Component Responsibilities

The Story component must:

- communicate the restaurant's identity
- establish emotional connection
- reveal the people behind the experience
- support the narrative with authentic photography
- reinforce the restaurant's atmosphere
- prepare the guest for reservation
- preserve the pacing of the experience

---

## Required Elements

A KHDS Story should include:

- Section Heading
- Narrative Copy
- Supporting Photography
- Brand or Restaurant Philosophy
- Signature Experience
- Clear Visual Hierarchy

Optional:

- Chef Story
- Founder Story
- Restaurant History
- Cultural Inspiration
- Craftsmanship Highlights
- Guest Testimonials

Every element should strengthen the restaurant's narrative.

---

## Narrative Structure

Recommended storytelling sequence:

Restaurant Identity

↓

Purpose

↓

Craftsmanship

↓

Signature Experience

↓

Guest Promise

↓

Invitation to Continue

The narrative should flow naturally rather than feeling like isolated content blocks.

---

## Content Guidelines

Story content should:

- be authentic
- be concise
- focus on the guest experience
- highlight what makes the restaurant unique
- create emotional connection
- remain conversational

Avoid marketing clichés and exaggerated claims.

The Story should feel genuine.

---

## Photography Behaviour

Photography should:

- reinforce the written narrative
- show authentic moments
- reveal atmosphere
- highlight craftsmanship
- support the emotional tone

Images should never feel decorative.

Every photograph should strengthen the story being told.

---

## Layout Behaviour

The Story component may use:

- editorial layouts
- split-content layouts
- alternating image and text blocks
- immersive full-width storytelling
- layered visual compositions

Whitespace should create rhythm between ideas.

The layout should encourage reading without feeling dense.

---

## Behaviour

The Story should:

- encourage continued exploration
- reveal information progressively
- maintain emotional momentum
- support natural scrolling
- integrate smoothly with surrounding components

The Story should never interrupt the guest journey.

---

## Visual Guidelines

The Story should use:

- restrained typography
- generous spacing
- consistent photography
- balanced text widths
- strong visual hierarchy
- subtle emphasis where needed

Avoid visual clutter or competing focal points.

---

## Responsive Behaviour

### Desktop

- Editorial layouts
- Comfortable reading width
- Balanced text and imagery
- Generous spacing

### Tablet

- Simplified layouts
- Reduced image complexity
- Maintain visual hierarchy

### Mobile

- Single-column flow
- Comfortable reading length
- Large imagery
- Clear spacing
- Easy scrolling

Reading should remain effortless across every device.

---

## Accessibility

The Story must:

- use semantic headings
- maintain logical heading hierarchy
- provide descriptive alternative text
- ensure readable line lengths
- maintain accessible colour contrast
- support keyboard navigation where interactive elements exist

Storytelling should remain accessible to every guest.

---

## Motion

Motion should support storytelling.

Allowed:

- subtle fade-ins
- gentle image reveals
- progressive content appearance
- restrained scroll animations

Avoid:

- distracting animations
- excessive movement
- automatic content changes
- motion that interrupts reading

Animation should reinforce pacing, never compete with it.

---

## Performance

The Story should:

- optimise photography
- lazy-load off-screen imagery
- minimise layout shifts
- preserve smooth scrolling
- avoid unnecessary scripts

Performance should never compromise immersion.

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- long uninterrupted text blocks
- corporate marketing language
- stock photography
- disconnected imagery
- inconsistent typography
- excessive animations
- decorative graphics without purpose
- repeating information
- storytelling that lacks emotional progression

---

## Content Maintenance

Review regularly:

- restaurant story
- chef or founder information
- photography quality
- brand messaging
- cultural references
- signature experiences

The Story should evolve as the restaurant evolves.

---

## Implementation Checklist

□ Narrative structure complete

□ Restaurant identity communicated

□ Authentic photography included

□ Story supports the guest journey

□ Visual hierarchy established

□ Responsive layouts implemented

□ Accessibility verified

□ Photography optimised

□ Motion refined

□ Performance tested

□ Story pacing reviewed

□ KHDS principles supported

---

## Success Criteria

The Story component succeeds when the guest understands:

- who the restaurant is
- what it stands for
- why it is unique
- what experience it promises

before reaching the reservation decision.

The guest should feel emotionally connected to the restaurant, not simply informed about it.
