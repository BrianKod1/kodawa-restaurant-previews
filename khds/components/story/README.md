# KHDS Story

## Mission

Help the guest understand why the restaurant exists before asking them to decide what to order.

The Story component creates emotional connection by revealing the restaurant's identity, philosophy, people, and purpose.

---

## Purpose

The Story component transforms curiosity into connection.

It answers the question:

"Why should I care about this place?"

before attempting to persuade the guest to book a table.

---

## Primary Goal

Build trust through authentic storytelling.

---

## Required Characteristics

A KHDS Story should:

- communicate the restaurant's identity
- explain what makes the restaurant unique
- reveal the people behind the experience
- support storytelling with authentic photography
- build emotional investment
- preserve the pacing of the experience
- prepare the guest for reservation

---

## Storytelling Philosophy

Guests rarely fall in love with features.

They connect with people, purpose, tradition, craftsmanship, and memorable experiences.

The Story component should reveal the restaurant's personality before introducing operational information.

---

## Information Priorities

The Story should communicate:

1. Brand Identity
2. Restaurant Philosophy
3. Signature Experience
4. Craftsmanship
5. Team or Founder Story
6. Cultural Inspiration
7. Guest Promise

Every section should strengthen the emotional connection.

---

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- corporate marketing language
- generic mission statements
- feature lists
- lengthy paragraphs
- repetitive storytelling
- stock imagery
- disconnected photography
- introducing practical information too early

---

## Success Criteria

The guest should be able to answer:

"Why is this restaurant special?"

before asking:

"What does the menu look like?"

The Story should leave the guest emotionally invested in the experience.
