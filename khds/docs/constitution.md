# KHDS Constitution

## Mission

Build digital hospitality experiences that create measurable business value through research, evidence, craftsmanship, and repeatable design principles.

---

# Article I

Research precedes implementation.

KHDS does not invent patterns.

KHDS discovers patterns.

---

# Article II

Evidence outweighs opinion.

No principle becomes part of KHDS without supporting research.

---

# Article III

Respect every brand.

KHDS provides structure.

Restaurants provide identity.

---

# Article IV

Experiences outperform interfaces.

Every implementation should improve the guest journey rather than simply increase functionality.

---

# Article V

The Twelve Foundations govern every architectural decision.

No implementation may knowingly violate them without documented justification.

---

# Article VI

Patterns are promoted through validation, not popularity.

Repeated evidence creates standards.

---

# Article VII

KHDS exists to help restaurants build stronger guest relationships and stronger businesses.

Technology is the tool.

Hospitality is the purpose.
